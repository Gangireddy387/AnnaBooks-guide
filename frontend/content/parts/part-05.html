5. Vendors, bills, and the vendor portal

5.1 Vendor center, new vendor, and purchase items

A vendor is a business you buy from. Vendor › Vendor Center shows each vendor's bills, payments, and balance.

  1. 1Go toVendor › New › New Vendor
  2. 2DoFill tabs, click NextVendor ID, name, terms, method
  3. 3ClickSave and CloseNo journal; no debt yet
  4. 4DecideStock you resell?YesNew Inventory ItemNoNew Purchase Item
  5. 5DoPick the accountAssets, Expense or Purchase
  6. 6ClickSave and Close
Step 1

Open New Vendor

Vendor › New › New Vendor opens a form with five tabs: Basic Information, Payment & Settings, Contact Details, Address Information, and Documents. On Basic Information, Vendor ID and Vendor Name are required; type V-2001 and Deskline Supplies. AnnaBooks checks the ID as you type and shows This Vendor ID already exists in this company. if it is taken. Active is ticked, Vendor Type offers Supplier, Service Provider, and others, and Vendor Since and Balance as of start at today. Fax, Web page address, Tax ID, and Notes are optional.

Leave Opening Balance (USD) at 0; Balance (USD) is read-only. Saving a vendor records no debt and no journal entry, even with an Opening Balance. The debt starts when you save a bill. Click Next to move to Payment & Settings.

Step 2

Save the vendor

Payment & Settings has two required fields: Payment Terms, where you pick Net 30, and Payment Method, where Deskline gets Cheque. Bank Name, Bank Account Type (Checking or Savings), Bank Routing Number, Account Number, Bank Branch, Credit Limit(USD), Account, Preferred shipping method, Vendor Group, and the tax fields are optional. Contact Details takes Contact Name, Title, Business Phone, and Email per contact, one marked Primary Contact. Address Information takes an Address Type and Address Line 1; a Billing address needs every field.

Click Next through each tab. Save and New and Save and Close appear only on Documents, where Add Document attaches files; click Save and Close there. Add City Parking (V-2002, Service Provider) the same way; its parking bill is entered later on Create Bill.

Step 3

Add the item you buy

Vendor › New › New Purchase Item opens Select Item Type with two choices, Service Item (picked by default) and Product Item; click Continue. A Service Item has two tabs. Basic Information needs a Service Name. On Service Information, I buy this service is ticked and requires Purchase Price(USD) and Expense Account, with optional Preferred Vendor, Vendor Service No, and Description on Purchase Transaction. A Product Item works the same way with Product Name and Purchase Account.

Stock you resell, like the Laptop sleeve at $12.00, is added in Inventory › New › New Inventory Item instead. Type the Inventory Name on Basic Information. Sales & Purchase Details asks for Purchase Price(USD), Cost of Good Sold and Assets Account (both required), and Preferred Vendor. Click Save and Close.

Set the sleeve's Preferred Vendor to Deskline Supplies. Create Bill only lists inventory items whose preferred vendor matches the bill's vendor.

Step 4

Then enter a bill or pay now

With bill tracking on, Vendor › Bills › Create Bill records what you owe and Vendor › Pay Bills › Pay Bill pays it later. To pay on the spot with no open bill, click Vendor › Record Expenses. The Record Expenses window offers two choices: Credit Card, picked by default, and Cash.

Click Continue. Credit Card opens Credit Card Charge and Cash opens Cash Expense. Cancel, or the close button in the corner, shuts the window without opening anything. Pick one path for each vendor invoice, a bill or an expense, never both, or the cost is counted twice.

More detail on this screen

Vendor › Vendors lists every vendor with Active, Vendor ID, Vendor Name, one column per month, Year to Date, Balance, and Actions, and offers Export, Import, Add New Vendor, Current View, a Vendor Type filter, and a Search vendor... box. Vendor › Vendors Items lists purchase items. On New Vendor, Next stays disabled while the current tab has an error. A Service Item's Expense Account can only be an expense or cost of goods sold account. A Product Item's Purchase Account can also be a fixed asset account, but never an inventory account. Create Bill warns you if an inventory item has no Assets Account. Vendor notes are reminders and never post. Add New Payment Term at the end of the Payment Terms list creates a new term.

5.2 Enter bill and create bill

Vendor › Bills › Create Bill records a bill you owe, and Vendor › Bills › Vendor Bill opens a similar form. You choose the vendor, the dates, the Bill No, and the lines.

  1. 1DecideBill tracking on?Set in Create Company wizardYesCreate Bill shownNoOnly Vendor Bill
  2. 2Go toVendor › Bills › Create Billor Bills › Vendor Bill
  3. 3DoVendor Name, dates, Bill NoBill Date, Due Date
  4. 4DoAdd account or item lines
  5. 5ClickSave and CloseThen opens Pay Bill
  6. 6ResultExpense ↑ · A/P ↑Bank unchanged until paid
Step 1

Check that bill tracking is on

Bill tracking is your answer to “Do you want to track bills you owe?” in the Create Company wizard. When it is on, Vendor › Bills shows four entries: Vendor Bill, Create Bill, Drafts & Captured, and Bill Mail Setup, and Vendor › Pay Bills shows Pay Bill above Vendor Payments.

When it is off, Bills shows only Vendor Bill and Pay Bills shows only Vendor Payments. Vendor Bill opens a similar form, Enter Bill, which shows Please Select a Vendor until you pick a vendor, and only then shows the itemize tabs. Northline has bill tracking on, so click Create Bill.

Step 2

Choose the vendor, dates, and lines

Create Bill has Bill Date and Bill No at the top, then Vendor Name, which is required; Bill Date must also be filled in. Pick Deskline Supplies, and Contact Name, Phone, and Payment Terms fill in from the vendor. Due Date follows the terms, so a 03/06/2026 bill on Net 30 is due 04/05/2026. Type the vendor's own number, DS-8891, in Bill No.

Three tabs set how lines are entered: Itemize by Consultant Hours (consultant industries only, like Northline's), Itemize by Account, and Itemize by Product/Service. For DS-8891 use Itemize by Account: Office Supplies Expense, Printer paper and toner, Amount (USD) 186.40; Add Account Row adds a line. DS-8900 uses Laptop sleeve, Quantity 10, at the item's Unit Price of $12.00. CP-310 is $240.00 to Travel Expense.

Step 3

Save the bill

Below the lines, Write comments for this Create Bill. takes an optional note, and Attach a file adds the vendor's PDF under Attachments. Total (USD) shows the bill total. Cancel leaves without saving, and Save and New saves the bill and clears the form for the next one.

Click Save and Close. A progress window runs two steps, Save bill and Create journal entries, then says All steps completed successfully. Click OK to finish. After OK, AnnaBooks opens Pay Bill. Each line's account goes up (an expense, or the inventory Assets Account), Accounts Payable goes up, and the bank does not change.

If the progress window says Bill is saved (journal was skipped) or Bill is saved; journal entries could not be posted, the bill is saved but is not in your reports yet.

Step 4

Pay it when it is due

The saved bill stays Open until Pay Bill clears it (see 5.4). Vendor › Bills And Expenses lists it with Type, Date, No, Vendor Name, Original Amount, Balance, Status, and Voided, under cards for Total Bills Amount, Outstanding Balance, and Total Expenses. Before any payment, DS-8891, DS-8900, and City Parking's CP-310 show Open with their full balance.

Enter each vendor invoice only once, using the bill number the vendor printed. The Due Date decides when an unpaid bill counts as overdue, and Pay Bill can list only the bills due by a date you choose.

More detail on this screen

Save and New saves the bill and clears the form for the next one. Create new Vendor at the end of the Vendor Name list opens a quick vendor form. Saving without a vendor or a date shows Please Complete Required Fields with Vendor Name is required or Bill Date is required. Vendor › Bills And Expenses lists every bill with its status and remaining amount. A bill line can use an account directly, a purchase item, or an inventory item linked to the same vendor. Only inventory lines change quantity on hand. The Due Date decides when an unpaid bill counts as overdue.

5.3 Drafts, captured mail, and bill mail setup

Drafts & Captured holds bills that are not in the books yet, including bills captured from vendor email. Nothing here is accounts payable until you open it and save it on Create Bill.

  1. 1Go toVendor › Bills › Bill Mail Setup
  2. 2ClickSave & open inboxSenders and mail rules only
  3. 3DoInbox › ConnectOutlook or Gmail
  4. 4Not posted yetCaptured draft billNot A/P, not expense
  5. 5DecideDraft correct?YesReview & adjustNoLeave as draft
  6. 6ClickSave and CloseIn Create Bill: Draft → Open
  7. 7ResultExpense ↑ · A/P ↑
Step 1

Set up bill mail

Vendor › Bills › Bill Mail Setup has three tabs. From senders lists Vendors on file with Select all and Unselect all; click Deskline Supplies, or type an address into Add email or @domain.com and click Add. Next: Mail rules opens the Mail rules tab, which sets When to check (Manual, Every day, or Immediate), How far back to load for Manual, an optional Subject contains filter, and Attachments only. Will load sums up the result.

Bill reminder mail can send daily reminders about unpaid bills to a Mail address at a Daily send time and Timezone. Click Save & open inbox to save and open Drafts & Captured, or Save settings to stay here. This only decides which emails load; it does not connect a mailbox or post anything.

Step 2

Connect the mailbox on Inbox

Open Vendor › Bills › Drafts & Captured. The toolbar has five tabs, Drafts, Split, Inbox, Captured, and History, plus Mail setup with a count of your senders, and Refresh (or Live when Mail rules use Immediate). On Inbox, choose Outlook or Gmail and click Connect; until then the panel says Connect your mail.

Once connected, the mailbox address shows and only mail from your Bill Mail Setup senders loads. Search loaded mail filters the list, and clicking a message shows its subject, sender, attachments, and body. The Captured tab then pairs these emails with draft bills, and its badge counts the matches. Deskline's email for DS-9012, $89.00, is captured but not posted.

Step 3

Review the draft beside the email

Captured shows each draft bill beside its Related mail; Previous mail and Next mail step through several matches. The Captured mail side marks each value Match or Differ against the draft, so check the vendor, amount, and date. Extract reads the attachment into a short summary, and the button then reads Refresh. Not this mail skips an email that belongs elsewhere, and Restore hidden matches brings skipped ones back.

Click Review & adjust to open the matched draft. On the Drafts tab, each card shows the vendor, bill number, a Captured or Draft badge, Payment terms, Due date, and Email, and its Open button does the same. Split shows the drafts and the mail side by side.

Step 4

Save only the bills that are correct

Review & adjust opens Create Bill with the draft filled in and a hint at the top: This draft is matched to captured mail, followed by the amount and Adjust hours, dates, or amount to match the invoice, then save. Check Vendor Name, Bill No DS-9012, Bill Date 03/26/2026, Due Date 04/25/2026, and the lines against the vendor's email.

Click Save and Close to change the bill from Draft to Open and post it like a typed bill. The History tab then keeps a record with Source draft, Supporting mail, Posted bill, and Adjustments. Leave DS-9012 as a draft if it does not belong in the books.

There is no Accept button. Saving the draft on Create Bill is what puts it in the books.

More detail on this screen

The History tab lists captured invoices and vendor-portal submissions that were verified and posted, with Document no., Amount, Bill date, Closed, and any Adjustments shown as Original draft and Posted value. With Immediate mail rules the toolbar shows Live and matching mail reloads while the page is open; otherwise click Refresh. Extract builds a short summary of a captured email's attachment. Disconnect on the Inbox tab unlinks the mailbox. The Drafts tab can be searched by vendor, amount, or bill no and filtered by Vendor. Drafts & Captured and Bill Mail Setup appear only when bill tracking is on.

5.4 Pay bill and vendor payments

Pay Bill lists a vendor's open bills and pays them from a Cash, Bank, Credit Card, or Paypal account. You tick the bills and enter how much to pay on each.

  1. 1Go toVendor › Pay Bills › Pay BillNeeds bill tracking
  2. 2DoVendor Name, Pay FromBank, Cash, Card or Paypal
  3. 3DecidePayment Method Check?YesEnter Check NoNoSkip check fields
  4. 4DoTick bills, enter PaymentsLeave Credits at 0
  5. 5ClickSave and Close
  6. 6ResultA/P ↓ · Bank ↓Bill Open → Partial/Paid
  7. 7Go toPay Bills › Vendor PaymentsPayment listed here
Step 1

Open Pay Bill

Vendor › Pay Bills › Pay Bill opens with Date and No (Payment No) at the top. Vendor Name is required; pick Deskline Supplies, and Contact Name and Phone fill in. Pay From is also required and lists only Cash, Bank, Credit Card, and Paypal accounts. Pick Checking Account (1010), and the read-only Bank Balance (USD) shows $26,700.00.

Bills due lists the vendor's unpaid bills with Due Date, Bill No, Status, Original Amount(USD), Amount Due(USD), Credits(USD), and Payments(USD). Paid, cancelled, disputed, and draft bills are left out, so captured DS-9012 never appears. Filter by bill due on or before narrows the list by date. Deskline's two bills total $306.40.

Step 2

Choose Pay From and the check

Payment Method is required and starts at Cash; change it to Check. Two more fields then appear: Check No, where you type 2204, and TO BE PRINTED, which locks Check No and shows TO BE PRINTED in it instead. Tick the box at the start of each bill row, or the box in the header to tick them all; Credits and Payments stay greyed out until a row is ticked.

Enter Payments of 186.40 for DS-8891 and 120.00 for DS-8900. A smaller payment leaves the bill Partial, and next time Amount Due shows what was already paid. Total (USD) at the bottom adds up every ticked row, beside Unused Credits (USD).

Leave Credits at 0. Any amount typed there is added to the total taken from the Pay From account.

Step 3

Save the payment

Write comments for this Pay Bill takes an optional note. Cancel leaves without saving, and Save and New saves the payment and clears Pay Bill for the next vendor. If the Pay From account cannot cover Total (USD), the field shows Insufficient balance in the selected Pay From account.

Click Save and Close. A progress window runs Save payment and Create journal entries, a Success message reads Payment 2204 recorded, and AnnaBooks returns to the page you came from. Accounts Payable falls by $306.40 and Checking falls by $306.40, and both bills become Paid. The expense is not recorded again.

Step 4

Confirm Vendor Payments

Vendor › Pay Bills › Vendor Payments lists every payment with Pay Date, Pay No., Status, Vendor Name, Payment Method, Pay From Account, Amount Paid, and Comments. Current View is All by default, or pick Issued or Not issued. Date (Pay Date or Issue Date), Date Range, From, To, and Update narrow the list, and Export to CSV downloads it.

Click a row's arrow to see Paid Bills with Bill No, Bill Date, Bill Total, Amount Paid, Payment Method, Payment Account, and Bill Status. Payment 2204 for $306.40 shows Issued. City Parking's bill CP-310 is paid the same way, in full, with check 2205 for $240.00.

More detail on this screen

Filter by bill due on or before narrows the list to bills due by a date. Bank Balance shows the Pay From balance, and AnnaBooks warns you if it is too low. Click a bill's arrow to see its item, consultant, and account lines, each with a Line payment box that updates the bill's payment. A payment cannot be larger than what is still owed on the bill. Create new Vendor at the end of the Vendor Name list opens New Vendor. Save and New keeps you on Pay Bill for the next vendor. Check payments made here are what Write Check and Print Check work with later.

5.5 Vendor credit, cash purchase, and immediate expenses

Vendor Credit records a vendor's credit note, but it is not linked to any bill and creates no journal entry. For costs you pay on the spot, use Vendor › Record Expenses.

  1. 1DecideVendor issued a credit?YesVendor CreditNoRecord Expenses
  2. 2Go toVendor › New › Vendor CreditBank Account + lines
  3. 3ClickSave and CloseNot linked to any bill
  4. 4Go toVendor › Record ExpensesCredit Card or Cash › Continue
  5. 5ClickSave and CloseNo open bill created
  6. 6ResultExpense ↑ · Card ↑ / Bank ↓Card: bank waits
Step 1

Know what Vendor Credit does

Vendor › New › Vendor Credit opens New Vendor Credit with two tabs. Basic Information has Credit Note No, Date, Vendor Name (required), Contact Name, Phone, and Bank Account, which lists only Bank, Cash, and Paypal accounts. Click Next for Lines & Totals: Itemize by Account (Name, Description, Amount(USD)), Itemize by Product/Service (Name, Description, Quantity, Unit Price), Write comments for this Vendor Credit., and Total (USD).

Saving raises the chosen Bank Account's balance and lowers the line accounts' balances. It does not reduce the vendor's bill or Accounts Payable, and it creates no journal entry. Make it recurring and Save as draft only show a message that they are not implemented yet.

Step 2

Pay CP-310 in full

Because a credit cannot reduce a bill, the sample saves no City Parking credit note. Instead, open Vendor › Pay Bills › Pay Bill and pick Vendor Name City Parking, Pay From Checking Account (1010), Payment Method Check, and Check No 2205. Tick CP-310 and enter Payments of 240.00, leaving Credits at 0.00.

Click Save and Close. Accounts Payable and Checking each fall by $240.00, Travel Expense keeps the $240.00 the bill recorded, and City Parking's Balance on Vendor › Vendors is $0.00.

Step 3

Record a cost you pay now

Open Vendor › Record Expenses, choose Credit Card, and click Continue to open Credit Card Charge. Pay From is required and lists your credit card accounts; pick Harbor Visa, and a strip shows Credit limit, Balance owed, This charge, New balance, and Available after. No, Date, Vendor Name, Contact Name, and Phone are optional. Add lines on Itemize by Product/Service or Itemize by Account: CCE-1 is Blue Door Cafe, Travel Expense, $48.20.

Save and Close raises the expense and the card balance; Checking waits until you pay the card. Choosing Cash instead opens Cash Expense, with the same fields, and saving raises the expense and lowers the Pay From account right away. Neither one creates an open bill.

Step 4

Keep one record per invoice

Vendor › New › Cash Purchase opens New Cash Purchase with Basic Information and Lines & Totals tabs. Vendor Name, Payment Method, and Pay From are required; No, Date, Contact Name, Phone, Bill To, and Delivery Date are optional. With Payment Method Cheque it also shows TO BE PRINTED and Cheque No. Saving adjusts account balances, but it creates no journal entry.

Do not record an expense or a cash purchase for an invoice that already has a bill, or the cost is counted twice. Use a bill and Pay Bill when you pay later, or Record Expenses when you pay now, never both.

More detail on this screen

Credit Card Charge only accepts a card whose account has a credit limit (otherwise it says Credit limit is not configured for the selected credit card account.), and it refuses a charge above the available credit with Charge amount exceeds available credit. Cash Expense and Cash Purchase refuse a total larger than the Pay From balance with Insufficient balance in the selected account. Save as draft and Make it recurring on Vendor Credit, Cash Purchase, Credit Card Charge, and Cash Expense are not available yet. Company › Transactions Center lists Credit Card Expenses, Cash Expenses, and Cash Purchases under Vendors.

5.6 Write check and print check

Write Check and Print Check prepare paper checks for payments already saved on Pay Bill with Payment Method Check. Neither screen posts anything; checking and accounts payable moved when the payment was saved.

  2. 2DoPick VendorBank Account auto-fills
  3. 3DoTick a Pay BillCheck payments, status Pending
  4. 4DoCheck Number, Payment Amount
  5. 5ClickConfirm CheckReady to Print; no posting
  6. 6Go toVendor › Print Check
  7. 7ClickPrint CheckMarks Printed; no posting
Step 1

Open Write Check

Go to Vendor › New › Write check, or Banking › Write check; the page is titled Write Check. Under Vendor & Bank Details, pick Deskline Supplies in Vendor. Bank Account is read-only: it shows Auto-detected from selected pay bill until you tick a payment below, then fills in from that payment's Pay From account.

Nothing on this page posts. Checking and Accounts Payable already moved when the payment was saved on Pay Bill with Payment Method Check. Write Check only turns that payment into a check that is ready to print, and Cancel leaves without changing anything.

Step 2

Pick the Pay Bill payment

Vendor Pay Bills lists that vendor's Check payments that are not written yet, with Select, Pay Bill #, Date, Payment Method, Contact, Total Amount, Write Status, Print Status, and Bills Paid. Tick payment 2204, $306.40, 2 bill(s) paid. Bill Payment Details opens under it with Bill Number, Bill Date, Bill Total, Amount Paid, Remaining, Status, and Memo for DS-8891 and DS-8900.

Check Information then fills in. Check Date is the date for the check. Check Number shows TO BE PRINTED and cannot be typed, and Payment Amount ($306.40) and Amount in Words come from the payment and are read-only. Memo is optional (Optional memo for check).

Step 3

Confirm the check

Click Confirm Check. If no payment is ticked, AnnaBooks says Please select at least one pay bill to confirm. When it works, Check Confirmed reads Check has been confirmed and is ready to print, and the payment's Write Status becomes Done. Next time the list loads, 2204 no longer appears here; it waits in Print Check as Ready to Print.

No new journal entry is made and Checking does not change again. Save Draft Check saves the check as a draft (Draft Saved) without confirming it, so it does not reach Print Check. Cancel leaves the page.

Step 4

Print only if you need paper

Open Vendor › Print Check and pick the Vendor under Select Vendor. Select Checks to Print lists only checks confirmed on Write Check and not yet printed, with Pay Bill #, Date, and Total Amount; Select All and Clear All tick or clear them. Tick 2204.

Print Options then appear. Pick Printer(s), with Refresh to reload the list, and Check Style, which offers US Business Pre-printed Check (Stubs Only). That style prints only the stubs, so the check paper must already carry your company details. Remittance Advice Stub Fields chooses what the stubs show. Click Print Check. Printing marks the check Printed and posts nothing.

More detail on this screen

Print Check only lists checks confirmed on Write Check and not yet printed; a vendor with none shows No checks ready for printing found for this vendor. Select several checks and the button changes to Print N Checks. Remittance Advice Stub Fields shows or hides Vendor Name, Phone, Address, Bill Number, Bill Date, Bill Amount, Amount Paid, Description, and Total Payment on the stubs, and Check Layout Preview shows the result. Recent Print History lists Check #, Pay Bill #, Vendor, Amount, Printed Date, Printer, and Status, with a Void action that marks a check Voided. Reports › Banking includes Check Detail and Missing Checks, so enter check numbers in order.

Invite the vendor

On the bill card, click Submitted to open Submitted bill inside this page; the back arrow returns to the cards. The review card shows the Vendor portal badge, bill number, Consultant, Rate, Period, Hours, Payment terms, Submitted date, Due date, Email, and Attachments, which you click to preview. Check the vendor, number, dates, hours, and amount.

Click Create Bill to open Create Bill with it filled in. Add Note opens a note thread for that bill (Type a note…) so you can ask the vendor a question first. If an invoice draft exists for the same timesheet, the two cards sit side by side marked Same timesheet; the one you choose becomes the bill and the other draft is deleted.

There is no Accept button. The bill only enters your books when you save it on Create Bill.

Step 4

Save, then pay with Pay Bill

Create Bill opens on Itemize by Consultant Hours with the vendor, Bill No, dates, Consultant, Timesheet, Hours, and Hourly Rate (USD) taken from the submission. Check them, then click Save and Close. More detail on this screen

Resend invite sends the email again. Add Note keeps a note on that bill. If an invoice draft exists for the same hours, the card asks you to choose the vendor submission or the invoice draft, and the other one is deleted. A submitted invoice you have not saved stays out of aging and profit.

5.8 What the vendor sees in the portal

Vendors sign in on the vendor portal, not the AnnaBooks client sign-in, and see only their own invoices and receipts. Nothing they do posts to your books until you save the bill.

  1. 1Go toVendor portal sign-in/vendor/login, email + code
  2. 2Go toPortal › Submit invoiceMenu or Home button
  3. 3ClickSubmit invoiceInvoice details, hours, file
  4. 4Not posted yetIn reviewDraft until client posts
  5. 5DoReview invoicesIn review › Open › Paid
  6. 6DoReview receiptsPayments the client recorded
Step 1

The vendor signs in

The vendor portal sign-in is titled Vendor portal and asks for Email address (required). A first-time vendor types the email from the invite and clicks Continue; a Verification code field (6-digit code) appears, and Verify code checks it. The page then says Code verified. Set a password so you can review your invoices and receipts, and asks for Set password and Confirm password, both required.

Click LOGIN to finish. A returning vendor sees Password right after the email and clicks LOGIN, with no code. Company user? Sign in to AnnaBooks links to the client sign-in. Home is the vendor's start page; vendors cannot see your chart of accounts, other vendors, customers, payroll, or reports.

Step 2

They submit an invoice

Submit invoice, from the menu or the Home button, says Invoice the company that invited you. Invoice date and Invoice number are required, and Invoice number shows Auto-generated. Under Invoice details, Payment terms is read-only, Notes is optional (Optional notes for the company), and a Bill To card shows Northline's address, phone, and email.

Under Consultant hours, each line has Consultant, Description, Timesheet, Hours, and Hourly rate; only consultants linked to the vendor account are listed, and Add consultant line adds another. Consultant total and Invoice total add up the lines. Attachment takes a PDF or image (PNG, JPG, WEBP). Clicking Submit invoice sends it to your review queue without posting.

Step 3

They review their invoices

Review invoices lists every invoice for this vendor. Filters are Status (All, In review, Open invoice, Paid), Date (All, Today, This Month, This Year), From, To, and Search (Search your invoice no or status); Clear resets them. A Source legend marks each card Submitted Bill or Client Recorded Bill, so bills you type on Create Bill show here too.

Clicking an invoice shows its status, Bill to, Invoice date, Due date, Payment terms, Timesheets (Consultant, Period, Hours, Rate, Amount), Notes, and Attachments. An invoice shows In review until you save it, Open invoice once it is posted, and Paid after you pay it.

Step 4

They see receipts after you pay

Review receipts lists the payments you record for this vendor, with Date, From, To, and Search (Search receipt no or method) filters and Clear. Clicking a receipt shows Receipt date, Method, and Invoices paid with Invoice, Date, Invoice total, and Amount paid, then Notes, Invoices covered, Invoice subtotal, and Amount received.

Home sums everything up: Invoices you submitted, Due, Receipts received, Review status (Waiting review, Open invoice, Paid), and short lists of Invoices to review and Receipts to review. Removing their access does not remove bills you already posted.

More detail on this screen

Profile opens Update profile. Business details (Business name, Email, Phone, Address, Address details) are view only, so the vendor asks your company to change them. The vendor can upload, change, or remove a Business logo, and change the password with Current password, New password (at least 8 characters), Confirm new password, and Save password. Review invoices can be filtered by Status and by date. A vendor with no linked consultants sees No consultants are linked to your vendor account yet. and cannot bill hours.
