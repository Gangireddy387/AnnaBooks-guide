8. Reports

8.1 How every report is built

Reports Home lists the report groups for the open company, and the sidebar under Reports holds the same reports. Pick a report, set Report Basis and the dates, and press Update.

  1. 1Go toReports Homeor sidebar Reports › group
  2. 2DoPick the reportPurchase: sidebar or quick links
  3. 3DoReport Basis, Date RangeAccrual default · or Cash
  4. 4ClickUpdatereads posted entries only
  5. 5DecideNumber looks wrong?YesOpen GL, fix documentNoKeep the report
  6. 6ResultExport to CSV / Printno tax filing
Step 1

Open Reports Home or the sidebar

Reports Home, under the line Browse financial, sales, inventory, and payroll reports by category, shows one card per group: Company & Financial, Sales, Customers and Receivable, Vendors and Payables, Inventory, Banking, Budget, Fixed Asset, and Payroll. Click any line in a card to open that report. The sidebar under Reports holds the same reports in folders such as Company And Finance, Customers and Receivable, Sales, and Vendors and payables.

Reports Home hides the Purchase group, so open Purchase by Vendor Summary and the other purchase reports from Reports › Purchase in the sidebar or from the dashboard quick links. A few names differ between the two lists: Reports Home says Cash Flow Statement and A/R aging, where the sidebar says Cash Flow Report and A/R Aging Detail.

Step 2

Set Report Basis and the dates

Every report opens with a filter bar. Report Basis has two choices, Accrual and Cash, and starts on Accrual. Date Range offers All, This week, This month, Last week, Last month, This financial year, Last financial year, This financial quarter, Last financial quarter, Financial year to date, and Custom. From and To show the chosen dates as MM/DD/YYYY and can be typed in only when Date Range is Custom.

Each report starts on its own range: Profit And Loss and Trial Balance on All, Balance Sheet and General Ledger Report on This month, and the aging reports on Financial year to date. For the March figures pick Custom and enter 03/01/2026 to 03/31/2026. On the Balance Sheet the To date is the as-of date, and a payment dated in April stays out of March's profit.

Step 3

Press Update to read posted records

The button at the end of the filter bar loads the report. Most reports call it Update; Balance Sheet and General Ledger Report call it Apply, and the payroll reports call it Run report. On many reports a new Report Basis or Customer reloads the list at once, but Custom From and To dates wait for the button. The line under the title then repeats the Report Basis and Date Range used.

Only posted entries count, so drafts, captured mail, unaccepted portal bills, staged payroll, and staged imports stay out. The $89.00 Deskline draft DS-9012 and the $64.20 card import are not in the totals. When nothing matches, the report says so, for example No records to show, and Export to CSV and Print stay greyed out.

In the current version, Check Detail, Deposit Detail, Missing Checks, Reconciliation Discrepancy, Reconciliation Reports, the four Inventory reports, Depreciation Report, and Automatic Transactions show example rows, not your company's data.

Step 4

Trace a surprise back to the document

On Profit And Loss, click an amount to open its detail page, such as Travel Expense Details - $353.00. The Summary tab lists Account, Account Type, Report Basis, Date Range, Report Amount, Transactions, and Calculated Total. The Transactions tab lists Date, Entry Number, Description, Debit, Credit, Balance Change, and Running Balance, with a search box above; click an Entry Number to open its journal entry.

General Ledger Report, Transaction Details by Account, and the Transactions Center trace any other account. Correct the document itself, not the report, then press Update again. Export to CSV and Print give you a copy, and no report files tax.

More detail on this screen

Income is recorded when an invoice is posted, and expense when a bill or an immediate expense is posted. Paying a bill later moves cash but does not record the expense again. The March figures in this guide use the Accrual basis. Date Range choices are All, This week, This month, Last week, Last month, This financial year, Last financial year, This financial quarter, Last financial quarter, Financial year to date, and Custom; the aging reports offer a shorter list: Financial year to date, This Month, Last Month, This Quarter, Last Quarter, and Custom. Profit And Loss can also show zero balances and compare to last year. Many reports have a Help button that explains the report and its formulas, and long lists are split into pages with a per-page choice at the bottom.

8.2 Profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow, and the ledger

Profit And Loss shows income minus expenses for the period, and Balance Sheet shows what the company holds and owes on the To date. Both read posted entries only.

  1. 1Go toReports › Company And Finance
  2. 2DoProfit And LossBasis, Date Range, From/To
  3. 3ClickUpdateclick amount to drill in
  4. 4DoBalance SheetTo date = as-of date
  5. 5ClickApply
  6. 6DecideStatements tie?YesExport or printNoTrial Balance, GL
  7. 7ResultExport to CSV / Print
Step 1

Run Profit And Loss for the period

Open Reports › Company And Finance › Profit And Loss. The filter bar holds Report Basis, Date Range, From, To, and Update, with Export to CSV and Print beside it. The title reads Northline Staffing LLC · Profit and Loss Report. Under it, the Summary and Detail switch picks the layout (Detail is the default), and Show Zero Balances and Compare to Last Year start unticked.

Detail lists each Income and Expense account, such as Consulting Income $23,900.00 and Wage Expense $19,008.00. Summary shows only Total income, Total expenses, Gross profit, Operating expenses, Operating income, Other income, Other expense, and Net income: $4,027.60 for March. Compare to Last Year adds last year's column, a YTD column, and % of Income. Click any amount to see the transactions behind it; Help explains the formulas.

In the current version, credit memos, cash sales, and cost of goods on a sale do not post, so they do not change this report.

Step 2

Run the Balance Sheet on the To date

Open Balance Sheet. Its filter bar holds Report Basis, Date Range (starts on This month), From, To, and Apply, with Export to CSV, Print, and Help. The To date is the as-of date: each balance is what the account held at the end of that day. The left column, Liabilities & equity, holds what the company owes and what the owners put in plus the profit kept; the right column, Assets, holds what it has. Each table lists Account, Account #, and Balance.

Below the columns, Balance Sheet Formula shows Assets = Liabilities + Equity with the three totals: $28,350.60 = $14,323.00 + $14,027.60 for March. The check line reads Balance Sheet is Balanced with Perfect Balance, or Balance Sheet is NOT Balanced with the Difference. Click the history icon on an account to see its transactions.

Step 3

Check that the statements tie

Open Trial Balance. It has the same Report Basis, Date Range (starts on All), From, To, and Update. Accounts with activity are grouped by account type, with Account Name, Account #, Debit, and Credit, and a Total line for each type. The last row, Total, carries a badge: Balanced when debits equal credits, Not Balanced when they do not. March shows $48,223.00 on both sides, and assets equal liabilities plus equity.

Cash Flow Report explains how cash moved. Besides Report Basis and the dates, it has Method: Indirect Method or Direct Method. Accounts sit under Cash flows from operating activities and Investing, financing & cash, with Inflow, Outflow, and Net Effect columns, and Cash Flow Summary closes the page. It is not a bank register.

Step 4

Open the ledger when a line looks wrong

General Ledger Report opens a page titled General Ledger. Its filters are Report Basis, Date Range (starts on This month), From, To, Account Type, Account Filter, Journal Filter, and Source Type. Source Type offers All Sources, Invoices, Bills, Pay Bills, Receipts, and Journal Entries. Picking an account or a journal entry reloads the list; other changes wait for Apply.

Each account is a block headed by its name, number, and type, such as Checking Account (1010), with Opening and Current badges. Its columns are Date, Description, Memo, Debit, Credit, and Balance, ending with Period Net Change. Click a Description to open that document. Transaction Details by Account, Expense Report, and Income by Customer Detail answer narrower questions. Fix the document, then run the statement again.

More detail on this screen

Expenses include posted payroll, bills, card charges, cash expenses, and loan interest in the dates. Depreciation reaches this report only when it is posted to a depreciation account, for example with a journal entry. Profit And Loss offers a Summary or Detail view, Show Zero Balances, and Compare to Last Year. Cash Flow is called Cash Flow Report in the sidebar and Cash Flow Statement on Reports Home. General Ledger Report can filter by Account Type, Account Filter, Journal Filter, and Source Type, and its page title reads General Ledger. Cash Flow Report starts on Financial year to date and names the chosen Method under its title. Trial Balance shows only accounts with a balance in the dates. Balance Sheet Help lists which account types the report includes and which it leaves out.

8.3 Sales reports and accounts receivable

Sales by Customer Summary totals posted sales for each customer in the period, and Sales by Customer Detail lists each sale. Accounts receivable aging shows what customers still owe.

  1. 1Go toReports › Sales
  2. 2DoSales by Customer Summaryor Consultant; Customer, Date Range
  3. 3ClickUpdate
  4. 4Go toCustomers and ReceivableA/R Aging Summary / Detail
  5. 5DecideCustomer has balance?YesCustomers StatementNoPaid, drops out
  6. 6ResultExport / Printstatement for the customer
Step 1

Run sales by customer

Open Reports › Sales › Sales by Customer Summary. The filters are Report Basis (Accrual or Cash), Customer (All Customers or one customer), Date Range (starts on All), From, To, and Update, with Export to CSV and Print beside the title. Changing Report Basis or Customer reloads the list at once.

The Customer Sales table lists Customer Name and Amount with a TOTAL row. For March: Cedar Schools $5,680.00, Harbor Clinic $7,000.00, Lumen Labs $7,520.00, Maple Dental $3,700.00, TOTAL $23,900.00. Click a customer row to open Sales by Customer Detail with the same filters, listing Customer, Date, Type, No., and Amount. Sales by Consultant Summary lists Consultant, Hours, Hourly Rate, and Amount, from posted invoices only.

Step 2

Run accounts receivable aging

Open Reports › Customers and Receivable › A/R Aging Summary, titled Accounts Receivable Aging Summary. Report Basis is fixed at Accrual and greyed out. Date Range offers Financial year to date (the default), This Month, Last Month, This Quarter, Last Quarter, and Custom; with Custom, set From and To, then press Update. Days late are counted from each invoice's due date to the To date.

Cards show Total Outstanding, Customers with Balances, and Report Basis. The table lists Customer / Vendor, Current, 31–60 Days, 61–90 Days, 91–120 Days, Older, and Total. Run to 04/30/2026, Cedar Schools has $2,680.00 in 31–60 Days and Lumen Labs $7,520.00 in Current. A paid invoice drops out, and a partial payment leaves only the remainder. A/R Aging Detail lists each invoice with its No. and Due Date.

Step 3

Open a customer statement

Customers Statement opens a page titled Customer Statement. Its filters are Current View (All, Summary, or Detail), Customer (All or one customer), Date Range, From, To, and Update; picking a view or a customer reloads it. Choose Cedar Schools and Customer Details appear beside Company Details, with Statement Date and Statement Period.

The Summary box shows Opening Balance, Total Invoices $5,680.00, Total Payments $3,000.00, Total Prepayments, and Closing Balance $2,680.00, and the Amount Receivable card shows $2,680.00. The table lists Date, Type, No., Description, Charges, Credits, and Balance. Click Export or Print to share it. Customer Transaction History, with Report Basis, Customer, and dates, lists that customer's invoices and payments.

Step 4

Keep sales and aging separate

The two reports answer different questions. Sales by Customer Summary shows everything you billed in the dates, paid or not. A/R Aging Summary shows only what is still unpaid on the To date, sorted by how late it is. Maple Dental has $3,700.00 in sales and no aging row, because RP-301 paid INV-1050 in full on 03/10/2026.

A large aging balance means the invoice is posted and the cash has not arrived. Lumen Labs shows $7,520.00 in both reports, because neither INV-1056 nor INV-1057 is paid yet. Tracker is the daily follow-up list; aging is the report you export or print.

More detail on this screen

Reports Home calls the detail report A/R aging, and the sidebar calls it A/R Aging Detail. An open invoice is both a sale and a receivable. In the current version a credit memo or a customer prepayment does not reduce what a customer owes. Only Receive Payment does, including any amount you type in its Credits column. A/R Aging Detail lists one row per open invoice with Customer / Vendor, Date, Type, No., Due Date, the aging buckets, Overdue, and Total. On the Customer Statement, Total Credit Notes appears in the Summary box only when there are credit notes, and the Export button saves a CSV file.

8.4 Payables and purchase reports

Accounts payable aging groups what the company still owes vendors by how late the bills are. Purchase reports show how much was bought, including bills already paid.

  1. 1Go toReports › Vendors and payables
  2. 2DoA/P Aging Summaryor A/P Aging Detail
  3. 3ClickUpdate
  4. 4DecideBills still open?YesVendors StatementNoNothing to pay
  5. 5ClickPay BillVendors › Pay Bills · Save and Close
  6. 6Go toReports › Purchaseor dashboard quick links
  7. 7ResultExport to CSV / Print
Step 1

Run accounts payable aging

Open Reports › Vendors and payables › A/P Aging Summary, titled Accounts Payable Aging Summary. Report Basis is fixed at Accrual. Date Range offers Financial year to date (the default), This Month, Last Month, This Quarter, Last Quarter, and Custom; with Custom, set From and To, then press Update. Export to CSV, Print, and Help sit beside the title. A/P Aging Detail lists each open bill instead.

When bills are open, cards show Total Outstanding, Vendors with Balances, and Report Basis, and the table lists Vendor, Current, 31–60 Days, 61–90 Days, 91–120 Days, Older, and Total. After checks 2204 and 2205 nothing is open on 03/31/2026, so the page shows No vendors matched the selected filters. The $89.00 draft DS-9012 is not counted.

Step 2

Open the vendor statement

Vendors Statement opens a page titled Vendor Statement. Its filters are Current View (All, Summary, or Detail), Vendor (All or one vendor), Date Range, From, To, and Update, with Export and Print beside the title. Pick Deskline Supplies to show Vendor Details next to Company Details, with Statement Date and Statement Period.

The Summary box lists Opening Balance, Total Bills $306.40, Total Payments $306.40, Credit Memos/Refunds, and Closing Balance $0.00, beside the Amount Payable card. The table lists Date, Type (Bill or Payment), No., Description, Charges, Credits, and Balance. Vendor Transaction History lists the same vendor's bills and payments. Check it before Pay Bill so the payment matches the open bills.

Step 3

Pay what aging shows

Open Vendor › Pay Bills › Pay Bill. Vendor Name, Pay From, and Payment Method are required. Pay From lists Cash, Bank, Credit Card, and Paypal accounts, and Bank Balance (USD) shows that account's balance. Date and No sit at the top; with Payment Method Check you also get TO BE PRINTED and Check No. Filter by bill due on or before narrows the Bills due list.

Tick each bill to pay. Bills due shows Due Date, Bill No, Status, Original Amount(USD), Amount Due(USD), Credits(USD), and Payments(USD). Vendor credits are not pulled in, so Unused Credits (USD) stays at $0.00. Leave Credits at 0: Total (USD) adds Credits to Payments, so a typed credit raises what leaves the bank. Click Save and Close, or Save and New. Paying does not record the expense again.

Pay Bill appears only when Managing Bills is set to Yes in Company Preferences.

Step 4

Use purchase reports for volume

Purchase by Vendor Summary has Report Basis, Vendor (All Vendors or one vendor), Date Range (starts on All), From, To, and Update, with Export to CSV and Print. The Vendor Purchases table lists Vendor Name and Amount with a TOTAL row, and it counts bills already paid. Click a vendor row to open Purchase by Vendor Detail with the same filters.

For March, Deskline Supplies shows $306.40 and City Parking $240.00, though both are at $0.00 in aging. Purchase by Item Summary splits purchases into Item, Consultant, and Account tables. Open these reports from Reports › Purchase in the sidebar or from the dashboard quick links, because Reports Home hides that group.

More detail on this screen

A partial payment leaves the remainder in aging. A bill that is still a draft or an unaccepted portal submission is not in the total. A vendor can have large purchases and a zero balance because every bill was paid. Run aging on the pay date, and run purchase reports for the period you are reviewing. On Pay Bill, the arrow beside a bill opens its lines, and Write comments for this Pay Bill holds a note. The Vendor Statement's Export button saves a CSV file. Purchase by Item Summary has the same filters as Purchase by Vendor Summary.

8.5 Inventory, budget, asset, banking, and payroll reports

Payroll reports read your recorded pay runs, and budget reports read posted income and expense. In the current version the inventory, banking, and depreciation reports show example rows, not your company's data.

  1. 1Go toReports HomeInventory, Budget, Banking, Payroll
  2. 2DoPick report, Date Range
  3. 3ClickUpdate
  4. 4DecideBudget or Payroll report?YesReads company dataNoSample rows shown
  5. 5DoCompare with source screenPayroll History, budget
  6. 6ResultExport to CSV / PrintTax Summary is not filing
Step 1

Pick the question, then the report

Reports Home groups these reports by question. Inventory holds Inventory Valuation Summary, Inventory Valuation Details, Inventory Stock Status By Item, and Inventory Details, for what stock is worth and how much you have. Budget holds Budget Overview and Budget vs Actuals, which compare a plan with posted results. Fixed Asset holds Depreciation Report, which opens a page titled Depreciation Schedule.

Payroll holds Pay Slip Summary, Pay Slip Detail, Pay Head Summary Report, Pay Head Detail Report, Pay Sheet, and Tax Summary Report. Inventory Valuation Summary filters by Warehouse and dates and lists Item Name, Description, On Hand, Avg Cost, Assets Total, Sales Price, and Retail Value.

In the current version, Inventory Valuation Summary, Inventory Valuation Details, Inventory Stock Status By Item, Inventory Details, and Depreciation Report show example rows, not your company's data.

Step 2

Read banking reports with care

The Banking group holds Check Detail, Missing Checks, Reconciliation Discrepancy, and Deposit Detail. Check Detail has Report Basis, Date Range, From, To, and Update, with Export to CSV and Print, and lists Type, Date, Number, Name, Check Number, and Check Amount.

In the current version these reports show the same example rows every time, such as check C-1001 to John Doe for $1,200.00 dated 05/01/2024, not your company's data. They will not show Northline's checks 2204 and 2205, so they will not match your register. Use the account's transactions and saved statements on Banking › Bank Accounts instead.

Step 3

Read payroll reports from recorded runs

The payroll reports read recorded pay runs. Pay Slip Summary sits under the line Payroll · Fulltime & 1099 and has Report Basis, Date Range, From, and To; its button is Run report, not Update. Cards show People paid, Fulltime, 1099, and Total net pay. The table lists Employee, ID #, Acct #, Bank, Branch, Net amount, and Email; click a row to see Earnings, Employee Deductions, and Net pay to employee.

For pay run #12 (PR-12) it shows 10 people and $19,053.00 net: $19,008.00 of earnings plus $165.00 of reimbursements, less $120.00 of deductions. Staged batch SP-14 is not included. Tax Summary Report lists Emp. deductions and Employer deductions recorded on payroll; it is not a filing.

Step 4

Compare the report with its source

Payroll reports should match Payroll History for the same dates: pay run #12 on 03/27/2026 shows 10 employees and Net $19,053.00, the same as Total net pay on Pay Slip Summary. If figures differ, check Date Range, From, and To first.

Budget vs Actuals has two filters, Budget and Show Accounts (All Accounts, Revenue, Cost of Goods Sold, or Expenses), then Update, with Export to CSV, Print, and Help. With a budget it shows Total Actual, Total Budget, and Budget Variance cards and a table of Category, Actual, Budget, Over, and Remaining. With no saved budget, the Budget list reads No budgets found and the page shows No budget data available for the selected filters.

In the current version, New Budget does not save a plan, so Budget vs Actuals may have no budget figures to show.

More detail on this screen

Pay Slip Detail lists each slip, Pay Sheet shows the run as a sheet, and the pay head reports total or list earnings and deductions by pay head. Pay Slip Summary and Pay Sheet also offer Report Basis. Budget vs Actuals has Budget and Show Accounts filters, then Update. The example-row reports still show date filters, Update, Export to CSV, and Print. Every payroll report uses Run report instead of Update. Pay Slip Detail filters by Employee and lists Earnings, Withholdings, Employer-Responsible Tax, and Net Value; Pay Head Summary Report filters by Pay Head; Pay Head Detail Report filters by Employee and Pay Head; Tax Summary Report filters by Tax Type. Budget Overview has Budget and View filters and shows each Category by month and Total.
