1. Start and company onboarding

1.1 Client sign-up and sign-in

The start page offers two doors: Client for the people who keep a company's books, and Vendor for suppliers you invite later. Signing in identifies you; opening a company card comes next.

  1. 1Go toStart page › ClientWelcome to AnnaBooks
  2. 2DecideHave an account?YesEmail, password, LOGINNoSign up Anna Books
  3. 3DoVerify your emailSend OTP, then Verify
  4. 4ClickSIGN UPName, password, company, website
  5. 5Not posted yetApproval may be pendingOnly if manager approval on
  6. 6Go toYour CompaniesClick one card to open
Step 1

Open the start page and choose Client

Welcome to AnnaBooks asks you to choose how you want to sign in, and shows two cards. Client, marked Company books and accounting, opens the client sign-in page where the people who keep a company's books work. Vendor, marked Bills, payments, and portal, opens a separate portal for suppliers you invite later; it never shows your books.

Below the cards, Don't have an account? Create account goes straight to the sign-up form. Use it the first time; after that, Client is the door you use every day.

Step 2

Sign in, or start signing up

Sign in to your account has two required fields, marked with a red star: Email address and Password. The eye button at the end of Password shows or hides what you typed. Tick Remember me to keep your sign-in details filled in on this browser, then click LOGIN; the button reads Signing in... while AnnaBooks checks your details.

If you are new, click Sign up Anna Books under Don't have an account?. Create your account asks for First Name, Last Name, and Email address. Click Send OTP, type the 6-digit code from the email into the code box, and click Verify; the label then shows Verified. Vendor? Vendor sign in at the bottom is the supplier door, not yours.

Step 3

Finish the sign-up form

Create your account states that all fields are mandatory. After the email is verified, enter Password and type the same value in Confirm Password; each has its own show or hide button. Then fill in Company Name and Company Website, starting the website with https://. Click SIGN UP.

Company Name and Company Website describe your account only. No books exist yet: you create the company itself, with its own chart of accounts, on the next pages. Already have an account? Sign in at the bottom takes you back to the sign-in page.

If your AnnaBooks service requires manager approval, you see Account Pending Approval and can sign in only after a manager approves your account.

Step 4

Land on Your Companies

After LOGIN you land on Your Companies, which lists only the companies you created or were invited to. Each card is one separate set of books, and a badge such as Owner tells you how you are connected to it.

A brand-new account has no cards yet and shows No Companies Found. Click Create New Company in the top bar to start the setup wizard described in Full company setup. Once a card exists, clicking it opens that company; everything you save afterwards goes into the company you opened.

More detail on this screen

Forgot your password? on the sign-in page starts a password reset. Remember me keeps your sign-in details filled in on this browser. Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Support are public pages and do not touch your books. Your sign-in password is separate from any company encryption password. You can change it later under Settings › User Management › Change password.

1.2 Companies home

Your Companies is the page you see after sign-in, and the Companies item in the top bar brings you back to it. Each card is one separate set of books.

  1. 1Go toYour CompaniesShown right after LOGIN
  2. 2DoRead card badgesOwner · Using now
  3. 3DecideSwitching companies?YesYes, switch companyNoCard opens
  4. 4DecideCompany encrypted?YesEnter password › UnlockNoOpens directly
  5. 5Go toLands on OverviewTop bar 'Home' page
  6. 6DoCheck company nameProfile card, sidebar bottom
  7. 7DoCreate New CompanyOnly for a new entity
Step 1

Read each company card

Your Companies shows one card per company. Each card has the company name and its badges, the legal name in brackets, then the organization type, industry, city, state, and country, email, phone, and website; a missing value reads N/A. Owner means you created the company. Using now marks the company that is already open, and the green banner above the cards reads Now using: with its name.

The strip at the top of each card reads Open, or Continue on the company in use. If more than one account owner invited you, the page first reads Select an account owner: each owner card is marked Invited you and lists the companies you can access. Click one to see its companies; Back returns you to the owners.

Step 2

Open the company you mean to update

Click a card, or press Enter on it. If a different company is in use, a window asks Switch to Harbor Books LLC? with Cancel and Yes, switch company; Cancel keeps the current company open. If the company has a company password, Enter company password opens next, with the company name and one Password box.

Type the password and click Unlock, or press Enter. Unlock reads Checking… while AnnaBooks verifies it, and a wrong password shows Invalid password. Cancel closes the window and opens nothing. When the checks pass, Loading Company... and Preparing your dashboard... appear, and a message confirms Switched company. Now using Harbor Books LLC. New records will be saved here.

Step 3

Confirm the company name

The company opens on Overview, the page the Home item in the top bar returns to. The top bar also has Dashboard, Companies, Tracker, Reports Home, and Company Preferences. The left sidebar lists Consultant (ITConsulting companies only), Customer, Vendor, Inventory, Banking, Company, Reports, and Settings, and your profile card at its bottom shows your name with the open company's name under it.

Check that name before you save a payment or an invoice, because entries save only to the open company. To change companies, click Companies in the top bar; you return to Your Companies, where the open company's card is marked Using now. The first time you open a company on this browser, a short celebration plays before Overview loads.

Step 4

Add another company only for a new entity

The top bar of Your Companies has Create New Company, Import, Encryption, Delete Account, and Logout. Create New Company opens Welcome to AnnaBooks, where you choose Quick Setup or Start Setup. Use it only for a separate business: the new company has its own accounts, settings, and optional company password, and reports never mix two companies.

Import opens Import company from backup, which restores a Data Export ZIP or Anna JSON backup as a new company. Delete Account removes your whole account and every company, not one company, and asks you to tick I understand that this action cannot be undone first. Logout signs you out. With no cards yet, the page shows No Companies Found.

Invited users see only Logout in this top bar; Create New Company, Import, Encryption, and Delete Account are hidden.

More detail on this screen

Invited users see the heading Companies you can access instead of Your Companies, and only Logout in the top bar. Customers, vendors, invoices, and the bank register stay inside the company that was open when they were saved. Import walks through Source, Preview, and Confirm, and Confirm & import creates the new company; Delete Account stays grey until you tick the confirmation box. A card reads N/A where the company has no email, phone, or location. The first time you open a new company, a Set Up Your Chart of Accounts window appears, described in What the open company remembers.

1.3 Full company setup

Create New Company opens Welcome to AnnaBooks, where Start Setup on the New to AnnaBooks? card runs the full eight-step wizard. The company is created only when you click Go to Dashboard at the end.

  1. 1Go toCreate New CompanyCompanies page top bar
  2. 2DoStart Setup'New to AnnaBooks?' card
  3. 3DoCompany Info, IndustryIndustry + organization type required
  4. 4DoTerminology, products, accountsNext on each step
  5. 5DoAnswer Bill TrackingChoice not saved; tools on
  6. 6DoSetup EncryptionOptional company password
  7. 7ClickGo to DashboardCreates company, back to list
Step 1

Choose New to AnnaBooks?

From Your Companies, click Create New Company. Welcome to AnnaBooks says Let's get started with setting up your company and shows two cards. Experienced User? offers a streamlined 4-step setup through its Quick Setup button. New to AnnaBooks? guides you through the setup step by step; click Start Setup on it to run all eight steps.

The back arrow returns to Your Companies without saving anything. In the wizard, a step bar across the top lists Company Info, Industry & Org., Terminology, Services/Products, Accounts, Bill Tracking, Encryption, and Finish, and you can click a step to jump to it. Each step has Cancel and Next, and every step after Company Info also has Back. Cancel asks Cancel setup?; Yes leaves without saving, No keeps you in the wizard.

Step 2

Enter company info and industry

Company Information marks its required fields with a red star: Company Name, Legal Name, Federal ID, Phone Number, Website (starting with http:// or https://), Country, State, City, Postal Code, and Address Line 1. Email Address, Fax, and Address Line 2 have no star. Timezone stays grey until you pick a Country, then picks a zone from the state; for Oregon, check that it reads UTC-08:00 Pacific Time (US & Canada). Click Next; an empty required field turns red with a message such as Federal ID is required.

Industry & organization has two required lists, each with a search box: Industry Type and Organization type (legal entity). Pick ITConsulting if you place consultants; only that industry adds the Consultant menu. Click Next.

Go to Dashboard at the end also needs Email Address and Timezone, and a company name you have not used before; otherwise it shows Email is required, Timezone is required, or A company with this name already exists.

Step 3

Set terminology, services, and accounts

Customize Your Terminology asks How do you refer to your customers? (Customer, Client, or Patient) and How do you refer to your vendors? (Vendor, Supplier, or Provider), Do you want to create Quotes in Anna Books? (default No), Select Currency (default USD), Fiscal Year Start (default January), Enable multi-currency, and a Cash Flow Management yes or no. Services & Products Setup asks What do you offer?: Services Only, Products Only, or Both Services and Products. Enable Inventory Tracking adds warehouse, unit, and Inventory Scheme options; Charge or Track Tax adds tax options.

Review Income & Expenses lists boxes under Cost of Goods Sold, Expenses, and Other Income, such as Labor, Travel Expenses, and Interest Income. None of these three steps has a required field; click Next on each.

In the current version, these account ticks are stored as preferences only, and Restore Recommendations and Select All do nothing. The chart of accounts itself is offered when you first open the company.

Step 4

Answer bill tracking and encryption, then finish

Managing Bills you owe? explains that knowing what you owe helps you manage cash flow, and asks Do you want to track bills you owe?, with Yes chosen by default; click Next. Setup Encryption offers the optional company password described in Optional company encryption password; leave its box unticked to skip it, then click Next.

Congratulations! says Your company has been successfully set up and shows one button, Go to Dashboard. Only this click creates the company: Company created successfully appears and you return to Your Companies, where you click the new card to open it. If a required value is missing, a red message names it and you stay on this step; click that step in the step bar, fix it, and come back.

In the current version, the bill-tracking answer is not stored; bill tools stay on.

More detail on this screen

The bill tools are Create Bill, Drafts & Captured, Bill Mail Setup, and Pay Bill under Vendor, and Vendor Bill is always available. Industries other than ITConsulting still have customers, vendors, banking, inventory, and reports. Cancel asks Cancel setup? and warns that none of your information will be saved; Yes leaves, No stays. Clicking a step in the step bar skips the checks that Next runs, so a missing field may only show up when you click Go to Dashboard. Enable Inventory Tracking adds Have multiple warehouses?, Enable Units, and Inventory Scheme (Average); Charge or Track Tax adds Tax item in transactions (One per transaction or One per detail line) and Enable Tracking Tax Paid. Setup does not create customers, vendors, items, or an opening bank balance; you enter those on their own screens.

1.4 Short setup for an experienced user

Quick Setup on the Experienced User? card runs four steps: Company Info, Industry & Org., Encryption, and Finish. Afterwards you set the skipped choices in Company Preferences and Settings.

  2. 2DoQuick Setup'Experienced User?' card
  3. 3DoCompany Info, IndustryNext after each
  4. 4DoSetup EncryptionOptional, then Next
  5. 5ClickGo to DashboardCreates company, back to list
  6. 6Go toCompany › Company PreferencesManaging Bills, Client & Sales
  7. 7Go toSettings › Account ConfigurationSales tax toggle
Step 1

Choose Experienced User?

From Your Companies, click Create New Company. On Welcome to AnnaBooks, click Quick Setup on the Experienced User? card, which offers a streamlined 4-step setup. The step bar then shows only Company Info, Industry & Org., Encryption, and Finish.

Quick Setup skips Terminology, Services/Products, Accounts, and Bill Tracking and fills in their defaults for you: Customer and Vendor as the terms, USD, a January fiscal year start, no quotes, Both Services and Products, inventory and tax off, every suggested account ticked, and bill tracking on. The result is still a full set of books, and you can change these choices later in Company Preferences.

Step 2

Enter company info and industry

Company Information and Industry & organization are the same screens as in the full setup. Fill in every starred field: Company Name, Legal Name, Federal ID, Phone Number, Website, Country, State, City, Postal Code, and Address Line 1. Also fill in Email Address and check Timezone, because Go to Dashboard will not create the company without them, then click Next.

Choose Industry Type and Organization type (legal entity); both lists have a search box, and an empty one shows Industry type is required or Organization type is required when you click Next. Choose ITConsulting if you need the Consultant menu. Click Next to reach Setup Encryption.

Company Details has no industry field, so you cannot change the industry after setup.

Step 3

Choose encryption and finish

Setup Encryption shows one checkbox. Leave it unticked for no company password, or tick it and fill in Company Password, Confirm Password, and an optional Password Hint, as described in Optional company encryption password. Click Next.

Congratulations! has a single button, Go to Dashboard, which creates the company. Company created successfully appears and you return to Your Companies; click the new card to open it. No customers, vendors, items, or opening bank balance are created, and the first time you open the company it offers to load the chart of accounts.

Step 4

Set the skipped choices afterwards

Company Preferences, in the top bar and under the Company menu, has a left list: Company Details, Managing Bills, Client & Sales Preferences, Location Tracking, Class Tracking, and Job Tracking. Managing Bills Settings asks Do you manage bills you owe? and has Enabled Price level, Enable Purchase Order, Expense and product/service tracking by customer, and Use billable expenses and products/services. Click Update to save; Reset undoes unsaved edits and Close returns to Overview.

Settings › Account Configuration has the Sales tax enable switch, plus Bonus enable, Discount enable, and Create draft bill when creating invoice; click Save configuration after a change. Add accounts with Company › New Account, or review them in Company › Company Lists › Accounts List.

In the current version, the Managing Bills answer does not show or hide the bill tools; they stay on.

More detail on this screen

Client & Sales Preferences covers products and services, aging, discounts, quotes, shipping, and the rounding account. Company Details, the first item in Company Preferences, holds the company information from setup, Organization type (legal entity), and the Data encryption section. Only the company owner can open Company Preferences; an invited user sees Access restricted. Reset stays grey until you change something, and leaving with unsaved edits asks whether to stay or leave. Save configuration on Account Configuration also stays grey until you flip a switch.

1.5 Optional company encryption password

Setup Encryption lets you add an optional company password. In the current version it works as a lock on opening the company; names, notes, and amounts are stored as usual.

  1. 1Go toSetup Encryption stepLast step before Finish
  2. 2DecideEnable encryption?YesEnter password twiceNoNo company password
  3. 3DoCompany Password, ConfirmOptional Password Hint
  4. 4ClickGo to DashboardPassword stored hashed
  5. 5DoUnlock on every openEnter company password › Unlock
Step 1

Decide on the encryption step

Setup Encryption has one checkbox, whose label begins Encryption enables your company data to be encrypted on the server. Below it, the screen says financial numbers are not encrypted and lists examples of data that is encrypted, such as customer names, vendor names, account names, and memos. Leave the box unticked to skip the company password, and click Next.

Tick the box to show Company Password and Confirm Password, both required, and Password Hint, which is optional. A warning box below them says you cannot open the company without this password and that losing it will render all your data useless.

In the current version, names, addresses, and notes are not encrypted; the password only guards opening the company.

Step 2

Enter and confirm the company password

Type the company password in Company Password; it must be at least 8 characters. Type the same value in Confirm Password. Both boxes check as you type, and Password must be at least 8 characters or Passwords do not match shows in red until they agree. Password Hint is optional; write something that reminds you of the password without giving it away.

Click Next; the wizard stays on this step while either message shows. On Congratulations!, click Go to Dashboard to create the company with its password. This password is separate from your sign-in password.

The warning on this step says you will be asked for this password when you change your account password, but in the current version Change password never asks for it.

Step 3

Keep the password somewhere safe

You need this password every time you open the company from Your Companies. AnnaBooks stores it in a form it cannot show back to you, and the Enter company password window has no reset link and does not show your hint. Keep the password outside AnnaBooks, for example in a password manager, and do not put it in an invitation email.

Your sign-in password and the company password are separate, and changing one never changes the other. If you have several companies, each has its own setting and its own password. The hint is visible later only in Company Preferences › Company Details › Data encryption.

Step 4

Unlock the company when you open it

Clicking the card of a company with a password opens Enter company password, showing the company name and a Password box. Type the password and click Unlock, or press Enter. Unlock stays grey while the box is empty and reads Checking… while AnnaBooks verifies it; a wrong password shows Invalid password. Cancel closes the window without opening the company.

After unlocking, the company opens and works as usual: a saved invoice still raises what the customer owes. To change or remove the password, open Company Preferences › Company Details › Data encryption. Untick Enable data encryption to turn it off, or type a new Company encryption password and Confirm password, then click Update.

More detail on this screen

Each company has its own setting and its own password. In Data encryption, Enable data encryption can also turn the setting off. Leave the new password fields blank there to keep the current password; while one is on file, the section reads A company encryption password is on file. The Encryption button in the Your Companies top bar opens Page Not Found in the current version, so use Data encryption instead. Only the company owner can open Company Preferences.

1.6 What the open company remembers

After setup, the company keeps settings that later screens follow. The chart of accounts groups every report, industry decides the Consultant menu, and Account Configuration decides sales tax.

  1. 1DoLoad Chart of AccountsFirst open; or Skip for Now
  2. 2Go toCompany › Company PreferencesCompany Details, Managing Bills
  3. 3DecideIndustry is ITConsulting?YesConsultant menu shownNoConsultant menu hidden
  4. 4Go toSettings › Account ConfigurationSales tax on or off
  5. 5ClickSave configuration
  6. 6Not posted yetDrafts stay unpostedExcluded from Tracker balances
Step 1

Load the chart of accounts

The first time you open a new company, Set Up Your Chart of Accounts appears over Overview with the heading Welcome to Your New Company!. It states how many predefined accounts it will create, lists what you get, such as Pre-configured asset, liability, and equity accounts and Revenue and expense categories ready to use, and warns that without a chart you cannot track your finances or produce accurate reports.

Click Load Chart of Accounts; a Loading Chart of Accounts window counts the accounts as they are created, and a message confirms how many were made. Skip for Now, or a click outside the window, asks Skip Chart of Accounts Setup? with Yes, Skip Anyway and Go Back. The window returns on every visit until the chart is loaded.

Step 2

Check industry

The Industry Type chosen during setup decides one menu. When it is ITConsulting, the left sidebar starts with Consultant, which opens Consultant Center, New, Consultants, Received Payments, Consultant Time Sheet, Consultant Invoice, Payroll Management, and more, and Quick Access shows Consultant links. Every other industry hides the Consultant menu and those links.

Customer, Vendor, Inventory, Banking, Company, Reports, and Settings are the same for every industry, and the Overview map shows its consultant row for every industry. Your Companies shows each company's industry on its card. Organization type (legal entity) can be changed later in Company Details.

Company Details has no industry field, so choose the industry carefully during setup.

Step 3

Check bill tools and sales tax

Under Vendor › Bills, Vendor Bill is always listed, with Create Bill, Drafts & Captured, and Bill Mail Setup below it; Pay Bills holds Pay Bill. These bill tools are on for every company.

Sales tax lives in Settings › Account Configuration, on the page titled Account configuration. Sales tax configuration has one switch, Sales tax enable, with On or Off beside it; when on, sales tax is calculated and tracked on your invoices and transactions. The same page has Bonus enable, Discount enable, and Create draft bill when creating invoice, all off for a new company. Save configuration stays grey until you flip a switch; click it to keep the change.

In the current version, neither the setup answer nor Managing Bills turns the bill tools off.

Step 4

Save or accept before totals change

Only a saved document changes the books, and closing the browser posts nothing. A draft bill does not raise what you owe the vendor: Deskline Supplies' DS-9012 for $89.00 waits in Vendor › Bills › Drafts & Captured until you open it and save it as a bill. Tracker leaves drafts out of its outstanding totals.

Other items also wait on their own review screens until you act: captured mail in Drafts & Captured, staged payroll in Consultant › Payroll Management › Staged Payroll, and staged imports, such as the $64.20 card file, in Company › Review Staged Imports. A saved invoice, such as INV-1052 for Cedar Schools, is in the books as soon as you save it.

More detail on this screen

Company › Company Lists › Accounts List shows every account, and Company › New Account adds one. Accounts List also has Load Chart of Accounts, which reads Load Remaining with a count when only some predefined accounts are missing; existing account numbers are skipped. Review Staged Imports is under the Company menu, and Staged Payroll is under Consultant › Payroll Management. With Create draft bill when creating invoice on, saving an invoice also creates draft vendor bills for eligible C2C timesheet lines.
