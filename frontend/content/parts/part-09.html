9.1 Company preferences and account configuration

Company Settings in the Settings menu and Company Preferences in the top bar open the same page, which only the company owner can use. Account Configuration is a separate Settings page for sales tax, bonus, discount, and draft bills.

  2. 2DoCompany Details
  3. 3DecideManage bills you owe?YesAnswer savedNoAnswer saved; bill menus stay
  4. 4DoClient & Sales PreferencesType, Inventory Tracking, aging
  5. 5ClickUpdateSaves Company Details and this page
  6. 6Go toSettings › Account Configuration
  7. 7ClickSave configurationsales tax, bonus, discount, draft bill
Step 1

Open Company Preferences

Company Preferences opens on Company Details. The list on the left has four groups: Company (Company Details), Vendor & Purchases (Managing Bills), Client & Sales (Client & Sales Preferences), and Categories (Location Tracking, Class Tracking, Job Tracking). Click a name to show that page on the right. These settings belong to the company, not to you. An invited user sees Access restricted instead, and OK returns to the dashboard.

Three buttons sit under every page. Update saves and takes you to the dashboard; it reads Updating... while it works. Reset stays grey until you change something, then asks you to confirm and reloads the saved values. Close goes to the dashboard without saving.

Update saves Company Details plus the one page on screen. Press Update before you click another page in the left list, or the edits on the page you leave are lost.

Step 2

Set Managing Bills

Managing Bills Settings has one question, Do you manage bills you owe?, with Yes and No; a new company shows Yes. Under it are four checkboxes, all ticked to start: Enabled Price level, Enable Purchase Order, Expense and product/service tracking by customer, and Use billable expenses and products/services. Northline keeps Yes and leaves the boxes as they are.

Press Update to store the answer with Company Details. In the current version the answer is only stored. Create Bill, Drafts & Captured, Bill Mail Setup, and Pay Bill follow a separate bill-tracking setting that is on for every new company, so choosing No here does not hide them. Vendor Bill and Vendor Payments are always shown.

Step 3

Set Client & Sales Preferences

Client & Sales Preferences opens on Product and Services: Type is Both services and products (the default), Products Only, or Services Only, and ticking Inventory Tracking adds Have multiple warehouses?, Enable Units, and Inventory scheme. Aging and Selling Details sets Aging basis to Aging for due date (the default) or Aging for transaction date, and has an Enable Sales Order box.

Further down, Track Discount, Tracking Quotes, Use Delayed Charges, Do you do shipping, and Enable to allow Rounding Amount for Invoice and CashSale are boxes; some open more fields when ticked. They only store the preference. Press Update to save this page together with Company Details, and you land on the dashboard. Client and sales preferences in full covers every field.

Step 4

Save Account Configuration separately

Settings › Account Configuration shows four sections, each with one switch and an On or Off label: Sales tax enable, Bonus enable, Discount enable, and Create draft bill when creating invoice. All four start Off. The last one makes saving an invoice also create draft vendor bills for eligible C2C consultant timesheet lines. Northline leaves every switch Off.

Save configuration stays grey until you change a switch; pressing it shows Account configuration saved successfully! and you stay on the page. With Sales tax enable off, invoices post the line amounts only. This page is separate from Company Preferences, so the Update button there does not save these switches.

More detail on this screen

Update saves Company Details and the page on screen, shows Company settings updated successfully!, and returns you to the dashboard. Reset asks Are you sure you want to reset all changes? before reloading the saved values, and Close leaves without saving. Update needs Company Name, Email Address, Country, and Primary Currency filled in. Leaving with unsaved edits opens Unsaved Changes with Stay on Page and Leave Page. New companies start with bill tracking on, which keeps the bill menus visible; in the current version the Managing Bills answer does not change that. Preferences do not post a transaction; they change which screens and fields later transactions see. In the current version, the Location, Class, and Job Tracking switches only store a preference.

Connect email for sending

Settings › Email Setup, titled Email Service Configuration, has two cards: Gmail Integration and Outlook Integration. Each shows Connected or Not Connected and, until connected, four setup instructions. Press Connect Gmail (or Connect Outlook), sign in on the Google or Microsoft page, and allow access. You return here and the card reads Gmail Successfully Connected with the mailbox address.

Once connected, Send Test Email sends a test message to that same mailbox, and Disconnect Gmail removes it. Then open Settings › Email Signature, fill in Signature Details on the Signatures tab, type a Signature Name, and press Save Signature. Neither page sends an invoice.

Step 2

The Tracker reminders tab on the SMS page turns on Send Tracker payment reminders and sets the daily send time and Timezone. On each page press Save changes, and a Success window confirms. Invalid email addresses are skipped when saving. A message does not post anything or collect cash.

Step 3

Set branding; the check layout cannot save

Settings › Document Branding has one tab, Template Settings. In Branding Configuration, pick a theme in Select Theme (Professional to start), pick a logo in Select Logo or press Upload Logo (PNG or SVG, max 2MB), and set Primary Color, Secondary Color, Accent Color, Heading Font, and Body Font. A sample invoice beside the settings shows the result.

Update Theme stays grey until you change something; Reset returns the theme to its default values. Settings › Check Print Setting shows Check details (Bank account, required, and Authorised signatory), a Check preview, and a Page formats table of Top, Left, and Width in centimeters. In the current version its only button is Cancel, so nothing on it is kept.

Step 4

Send or print a posted document

Test one email to yourself before a large run. When you send a reminder from Tracker, the envelope button opens Send Invoice Email. From is the connected mailbox and To is the customer's address. You can edit the Subject, pick a saved Signature or No Signature, and change the Email Body before sending.

Send Email sends the message and Cancel closes the window without sending. Emailing or printing a saved invoice or check does not post it again, and branding never changes an amount. Printing check 2204 uses the payment that Pay Bill already recorded.

More detail on this screen

Dunning Letter, also under Settings, holds the wording for overdue reminders; see Dunning letters. Email Signature can keep several named signatures under My Signatures: the star sets one as the default and the bin deletes it, and Standard Layout is the only layout template. Document Branding also sets Company Logo Placement (Top Left or Top Right), Show Bill To Section, Show Payment Terms, Show Order Number, Show Due Date, Show Tax Breakdown, and Show Comments Section, and Create New Theme saves a copy of a base theme under a Theme Name with Save New Theme. Disconnect Gmail or Disconnect Outlook removes the mailbox from the company.

9.3 Data export, data import, and leaving a company

Data Export downloads a copy of company data, and Data Import brings a reviewed file in. Only the company owner sees these two pages.

  1. 1Go toSettings › Data Exportowner only
  2. 2DoCategory, Datasets, Format
  3. 3ClickExport N as FMTdownloads a copy
  4. 4Go toSettings › Data Importowner only
  5. 5DoSource › Review › Policy
  6. 6ClickValidatedry-run, nothing written
  7. 7ClickStart importwrites to live company
Step 1

Export a copy when you need one

Settings › Data Export appears only for the company owner. Pick a Data category (Master data, Transaction data, Reports, or Custom selection), set any Filters, tick the Datasets you want (Select all and Clear help), and choose a File format: Excel (.xlsx), PDF (.pdf), CSV (.csv), or JSON (.json). For a backup, Northline picks Custom selection, ticks Customers and Customer Invoices, and chooses JSON.

The button at the bottom names your choice, such as Export 2 as JSON, and stays grey until a dataset is ticked. A progress window runs, the file downloads, and OK closes it. The file is a copy, and nothing is removed from the company. In the current version, Delete company in the Settings menu does not open a page, so the company stays.

Step 2

Import only a file you have reviewed

Settings › Data Import, also owner only, has an Import and History toggle at the top right. The Import side is a five-step wizard: Source, Review, Policy, Validate, and Import. On Source, pick Data Export ZIP, Anna JSON backup, Excel workbook, or CSV file, then drop the file on the box or click to browse. A CSV file also needs a Target dataset for CSV.

Press Parse file, then Next on Review, pick a conflict policy, and press Validate. Validate is a dry run that writes nothing. Continue to import, then Start import and its Start import? confirmation, writes to the live company and cannot be undone automatically. Back returns one step.

Step 3

Keep posting in the open company

An exported file is a snapshot. When Cedar Schools pays the $2,680.00 still open on INV-1053, record it in the open company with Customer › Receive Payment: choose Received From, Payment Method, Amount Received (USD), Deposit In, and Cheque/Ref No, then type the Payment on the invoice line.

Save and Close posts the payment, and the exported file does not change. Export and import do not file tax and do not close the year. Run a fresh export afterwards if you want the copy to include the new payment.

Step 4

Keep the encryption password safe

Company Preferences › Company Details has a Data encryption section. Ticking Enable data encryption shows Company encryption password, Confirm password, and Password hint. The password is required the first time, must be at least 8 characters, and must match Confirm password. Once one is on file, the box says New password (optional); leave it blank to keep the current one.

Press Update to save. Keep the password where the owner can find it. Data Export does not ask for it, and the Company Profile dataset leaves secrets out of the file, so a restore through Data Import does not bring the password back.

More detail on this screen

A bad import creates duplicate customers or invoices, which then have to be corrected on the live records, because the merge pages do not combine them yet. Choose the conflict policy carefully: Skip existing keeps current records and only creates new rows, Update existing overwrites matched records with the file values, and Create only never updates. Duplicates are matched on business keys such as Customer ID, Invoice No., and Account Number. Reports can be exported as Excel or PDF only. Import From File on the company menu is a different, staged import.

9.4 One connected week in the client books

A normal week starts on the Companies page with the card marked Using now, or a switch to the right company. Add customers, vendors, and items first so every later line has an account.

  1. 1ClickCreate InvoiceCustomer › New · from timesheets
  2. 2ClickReceive PaymentCustomer menu
  3. 3ClickMake Depositonly if Undeposited Funds
  4. 4ClickCreate Billor open draft · Save and Close
  5. 5ClickPay BillVendors › Pay Bills
  6. 6DoUpload bank statementMark as Recorded per line
  7. 7ResultProfit And Loss, Balance Sheetposted entries only
Step 1

Bill the time and bring money in

Enter the week's hours on the consultant's time sheet and press Create Time Sheet. On Customer › New › Create Invoice, pick the customer and press Add Timesheet; the hours come in at the consultant's Sales Price. Priya Shah's 40 hours for Harbor Clinic make INV-1048 for $3,400.00. Save and Close posts it to Accounts Receivable and Consulting Income.

When Harbor Clinic pays, open Customer › Receive Payment, choose Received From, Payment Method, Amount Received (USD), and Deposit In, type each invoice's Payment, and press Save and Close. Harbor's $7,000.00 went to Undeposited Funds, so finish with Banking › Make Deposit: Add RP-302 and Save and Close deposit D-78 into Checking.

Step 2

Accept bills, then pay them

Vendor › Drafts & Captured has Drafts, Split, Inbox, Captured, and History tabs. A draft or captured bill, such as Deskline's DS-9012 for $89.00, is not posted. Open it, check it on Create Bill, and press Save and Close to post it; for a bill a vendor submitted through the portal, press Create Bill on it first. Until then it stays out of Accounts Payable.

Then open Vendor › Pay Bills › Pay Bill. Choose Vendor Name, Pay From, Payment Method, and Check No, type each bill's payment, and press Save and Close; check 2204 paid Deskline's $306.40. A vendor credit goes in Credits(USD). Otherwise leave it at $0.00, because an amount typed there is added to what leaves the bank.

Step 3

Match the bank statement

On Banking › Bank Accounts, press Upload on the Checking Account row, choose the file where it says Drop your bank statement here, and press Process Statement. Check the Statement Information, Summary, and Extracted Transactions, then press the import button. View Saved Statements opens the statement with each line, its amount, and whether it is recorded.

Click an unrecorded line to open Link Payment, pick the matching record under Select payment to link, and press Mark as Recorded. The amounts must match; if they do not, the window says Can't link and offers Record New Payment instead. Cedar's $3,000.00 deposit links to RP-303.

The current version has no reconcile-to-zero screen, and Reconciliations History does not list past results yet.

Step 4

Read the statements

Open Reports › Company And Finance › Profit And Loss. Set Report Basis (Northline uses Accrual), pick a Date Range or type From and To (03/01/2026 to 03/31/2026), and press Update. March shows Total income $23,900.00, Total expenses $19,872.40, and Net income $4,027.60. Summary and Detail switch the view, and Export to CSV and Print make copies.

Balance Sheet uses Apply instead of Update, and the A/R and A/P aging reports show who owes what. Anything still in drafts, captured mail, staged payroll, or a staged import is not in these statements, so post it first.

More detail on this screen

A payroll run from Create Payroll posts wage expense to payroll clearing, and the bank side is recorded from the statement line with Create Payroll Journal. Card charges are entered from Vendor › Record Expenses › Credit Card and leave checking only when you use Pay Credit Card. Record Loan Payment splits principal and interest. A journal entry is only for a correction the other screens cannot make. In the current version, cash sales, credit memos, customer prepayments, and customer refunds do not post to the books.

Location, class, and job are optional switches on Company Preferences under Categories. In the current version they only store a preference, and no invoice, bill, or check offers these tags yet.

Step 1

In Company Preferences, click Location Tracking, Class Tracking, or Job Tracking under Categories. All three open one page, Categories Preferences, and scroll to the section you clicked. Location Tracking explains that it sorts transactions by business location; Class Tracking and Job Tracking each have one checkbox. All three boxes start unticked.

Only the company owner can open this page; an invited user sees Access restricted. Nothing here posts money. Reset puts back the saved values, and Close leaves without saving.

Expect no tag field yet

Open Customer › New › Create Invoice after saving. In the current version, no invoice, bill, or check screen shows a Location, Class, or Job field, so you cannot tag a transaction yet. Lumen Labs' INV-1056 for Ethan Brooks's 40 hours posts $4,400.00 to Accounts Receivable and Consulting Income as usual.

Turning a switch on now only records your plan. Northline leaves all three switches off and follows each customer and consultant through the normal invoice lines and reports.

9.6 Dunning letters

A dunning letter is the reminder email for an invoice that is coming due or already late. Settings › Dunning Letter stores the categories that choose the wording.

  1. 1Go toSettings › Dunning Letter
  2. 2DecideList empty?YesLoad default categoriesNoEdit or add

  4. 4ClickSave

Step 1

Open Dunning Letter

Settings › Dunning Letter opens Dunning letter categories. With no categories, it shows No categories found and two buttons: Load default categories and Add new category. Load default categories adds four: a before-due reminder and three overdue notices. Each category card then shows its name, an Active or Inactive badge, Days overdue, Letter template, Priority, and Description.

On a card, the envelope icon is Preview email, the pencil is Edit category, and the bin is Delete category, which asks Delete Category? before removing it. Add new category opens a window with the same fields as editing. Load default categories disappears once the list has any category.

Step 2

Set the days and the template

Edit category, and the Add new category window, has Category name and Days overdue (both required), Priority, Letter template, Description, and the Active category box. Days overdue −3 means three days before the due date, 0 is the due date, and 3, 10, and 11 are days after it.

Priority is Low, Medium, High, or Urgent. Letter template is Default Template, Friendly Reminder, Formal Notice, Final Notice, or Legal Notice. Leave Active category ticked, because only active categories are used for sending, and press Save; Cancel drops the edit. A Success window then says Category updated successfully!

Step 3

Preview the email

Press the envelope icon on a card to open Email preview, with the category name in the title. The top shows Subject, Template, and Priority. Below is the message as it would look, with From set to the company email and To set to the sample address customer@example.com. For the 10+ Days Overdue category the subject is Final Notice - Immediate Payment Required.

Close, or the X in the corner, shuts the preview. Closing does not send the message, and the preview never uses a real customer. Real reminders go out from Tracker.

Step 4

Send it from Tracker

On Tracker, each invoice row has an Action column. The envelope button, Send Email, appears on an overdue unpaid invoice, and before the due date only when a before-due category matches. With no categories at all, it appears only on overdue invoices, and a paid invoice has none. Cedar Schools' INV-1053, due 03/24/2026, still has $2,680.00 open.

The button opens Send Invoice Email with the matching Dunning Letter Category and its priority, plus From, To, Subject, Signature, and Email Body. Press Send Email; it needs Email Setup connected. After a send the button reads Email already sent today. The open balance stays until Receive Payment.

More detail on this screen

The default categories are Before 3 Days (Reminder Before Due Date) (−3 days, Low, Friendly Reminder), 1 to 3 Days Overdue (Soft Reminder) (3, Low, Friendly Reminder), 3 to 10 Days Overdue (Strong Reminder) (10, High, Formal Notice), and 10+ Days Overdue (Final Notice / Very Strong) (11, Urgent, Final Notice). Zero days means the due date. A card's Letter template badge shows the short name in capitals, such as FINAL or DEFAULT. Only an active category is used when Tracker matches an invoice. Delete Category? warns that the action cannot be undone. A paid invoice has no Send Email button.

Integration Keys shows Client information and Company information with a copy button for each key, and a key does not post. Journal Entry Guide answers questions about a scenario and does not post a journal. Users lists the people invited to the company, and Invite a User opens the invite form.

In the current version, Check Print Setting has no save button, so check positions cannot be stored.

More detail on this screen

Users lists invited people, and the invite asks for Email, First name, Last name, and User role, then saves with Save and close or Save and new. Email Signature can hold a name, job title, phone, email, website, address, social links, images, and a disclaimer, and the star on a saved signature makes it the default. Integration Keys shows client and company keys you can copy, and a key does not post. Journal Entry Guide answers questions about a scenario and does not post a journal. Session Alert's Reset to default (1 min) returns the alert lead time to one minute.

9.10 Data export and data import screens

Data Export and Data Import are two owner-only Settings pages, separate from Import From File on the company menu. Export downloads a copy, and the open company keeps every posted transaction.

  1. 1Go toSettings › Data Export
  2. 2DoData categoryMaster, Transaction, Reports, Custom
  3. 3DoFilters, Datasets, File formatExcel, PDF, CSV, JSON
  4. 4ClickExport N as FMTa copy; live data unchanged
  5. 5Go toSettings › Data ImportImport tab
  6. 6ClickValidatedry-run counts, nothing written
  7. 7ClickStart importthen History › Refresh
Step 1

Choose what to export

Data export has four panels. Data category is Master data, Transaction data, Reports, or Custom selection, each with a count. Filters shows only what the chosen datasets support: From date and To date (MM/DD/YYYY), Status, Search, and Active records only (master data), ticked by default. Datasets lists checkboxes such as Customers, Vendors, and Chart of Accounts, with Select all and Clear.

File format offers Excel (.xlsx), PDF (.pdf), CSV (.csv), and JSON (.json); reports can use Excel and PDF only. The bar at the bottom counts the datasets ready, and the button reads, for example, Export 3 as Excel. Press it to download the file.

Step 2

Keep the live company

While the export runs, a progress window titled Exporting data shows Currently exporting with each dataset as it is built, then the file name when it is done; OK closes it. The file is a copy. The company inside AnnaBooks keeps every balance, so the Balance Sheet still shows Accounts Receivable $10,200.00 and Checking Account $16,030.60.

The spreadsheet will not receive the next payment. Record new activity in the open company, and export again later if you need a fresh copy. A JSON export is the backup that Data Import can bring back.

Step 3

Validate before you import

After Review, the Policy step shows Conflict policy, which matches duplicates on business keys such as Customer ID, Invoice No., and Account Number. Skip existing keeps current records and creates only new rows, Update existing overwrites matched records with the file values, and Create only never updates. Pick one and press Validate.

Validation results is a dry run that writes nothing. It shows totals for Would create, Would update, Would skip, Link later, and Errors, then a table per dataset, with lists that explain each skipped row and error. Continue to import, then Start import, is the step that writes.

Start import cannot be undone automatically, so keep a fresh Data Export backup first.

Step 4

Check history after the run

Switch the toggle at the top right from Import to History. Import history lists each run with Date, File, Status, Source, Policy, Created, Updated, Skipped, Errors, and Imported by. Click a row to open its per-dataset detail with Rows, Created, Updated, Skipped, Link later, and Errors, plus the reasons for skipped rows and errors.

Press Refresh to reload the list; with no runs yet it says No imports yet. After a finished import, the Import side also offers View import history and Start new import. Duplicates from a bad file stay until you correct them on the live records.

More detail on this screen

Sources are Data Export ZIP, Anna JSON backup, Excel workbook, or CSV file. An Excel workbook needs one sheet per dataset, named like the dataset, and a CSV file holds a single dataset, so it needs a target dataset. On Review, Select all and Clear apply to the datasets in the file, and Preview shows sample rows. Back returns to the previous step. Link later means a row imports but a reference, such as a parent account, is left empty to map afterwards. The merge pages do not combine duplicate names yet. Export and import do not file tax and do not close the year.
