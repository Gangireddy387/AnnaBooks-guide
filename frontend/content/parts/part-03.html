3. Customers and sales

3.1 Customer center, new customer, and the customer list

A customer is the person or organization you sell to. Customer Center shows one customer's invoices, received payments, and balance.

  1. 1Go toCustomer › New › New Customer
  2. 2DoEnter ID & nameBasic Information tab
  3. 3DoPick Payment TermsPayment & Settings tab
  4. 4DoAdd contact & addressNext through each tab
  5. 5ClickSave and CloseOn Documents tab
  6. 6Go toCustomer › CustomersRow › View to edit
  7. 7Go toCustomer › Customer CenterInvoices & received payments
Step 1

Open New Customer

Customer › New › New Customer opens a form with five tabs: Basic Information, Payment & Settings, Contact Details, Address Information, and Documents. On Basic Information, Customer ID and Customer Name are required; for Cedar Schools enter C-1002 and Cedar Schools. Both are checked as you type, and a taken value shows This Customer ID already exists in this company. Active is ticked and Customer Since is today's date. Opening Balance (USD), Balance as of, Fax, Web page address, and Notes are optional, and Balance (USD) is read-only.

Click Next to move to the next tab, or Cancel to leave without saving. Next stays greyed out while a required field on the tab is empty. Saving a customer does not create income; income starts when you post an invoice.

Step 2

Set terms, then contact and address

Payment & Settings has three required fields: Payment Method, Payment Terms, and Invoice Frequency. For Cedar Schools pick Check, Net 15 (invoices fall due 15 days after their date), and Weekly. Sales Person, Customer Group, Credit Limit(INR), Bank Name, Bank Account Number, and Bank Branch are optional. The lists end with Add New Sales Person, Add New Payment Term, Add New Group, or Add New Invoice Frequency, which open a small form without leaving the page.

Click Next. On Contact Details, Add Contact opens a card with Contact Name, Title, Business Phone, Email, and Primary Contact; click Save on the card. Address Information works the same way with Add Address, Address Type (Billing or Shipping), Address Line 1, Address Line 2, Country, State, City, and Postal Code.

Step 3

Save, then find them on the list

The last tab, Documents, has Add Document for uploading files such as a signed contract; click a file in the list to preview it on the right. On this tab Next is replaced by Save and New and Save and Close. Click Save and Close; the message Customer created successfully. asks Stay on this page to view the details?. Yes opens the customer's edit page, and No takes you back.

Customer › Customers shows the Customers List with Current View (All, Active, Inactive), a Search box, a column for each month, Year to Date, and Balance. Click a row to open its preview, or the pencil Edit Customer button to edit it. Editing the name keeps the invoices already posted to that customer.

Step 4

Check Customer Center before invoicing

Customer › Customer Center opens with Status set to Active and Customer Scope set to Selected Customer. The left list shows each customer's Name and Balance, with a Search customer... box; click Cedar Schools. The right side then reads Cedar Schools | C-1002 with Balance: $2,680.00, which is what its invoices still have unpaid. Create Invoice and Receive Payment open those screens with Cedar already picked, and Preview opens the Last Invoice and Last Receipt cards and the Customer Information and Notes tabs.

Under Transactions, filter by Current View, Type, and Date, type in Search, or click Export to CSV. Each row shows Date, Type, Customer, No, Memo, Due Date, Amount, and Status, and the eye button opens it. Switch Customer Scope to All Customers for combined totals.

Current View offers Cash Sales, Customer Credit Notes, and Customer Refunds, but Customer Center loads only invoices and received payments, so those views stay empty. Prepayments are not listed either.

More detail on this screen

Payment terms come from the Payment Term list, and you can add a new term from the dropdown. The Opening Balance (USD) field is saved with the customer but does not post a journal entry. Contacts and addresses are optional, but if you add only one contact it must be marked Primary Contact. Each saved contact or address card has Edit and Delete. Manage Custom Fields on Payment & Settings adds fields of your own. Customer › Customers also has Import and Export buttons for bulk lists, and a table or grid view; grid cards show Balance, Open invoices, Email, Phone, and a View button. A customer exists only inside the open company, so a second company needs its own record. If the same customer was entered twice, use Company › Merge Accounts › Merge Customers.

3.2 Sales items

An item is something you sell, linked to the accounts it posts to. Customer › Customers Items lists every item.

  1. 1Go toCustomer › New › New Sales Item
  2. 2DoPick item typeSelect Item Type › Continue
  3. 3DoSet Income Accountplus Sales Price(USD)
  4. 4DecideInventory item or assembly?YesAdd COGS & AssetsNoIncome account only
  5. 5ClickSave and Close
  6. 6Go toCustomer › Customers ItemsNo posting on save
Step 1

Choose the item type

Customer › New › New Sales Item opens the Select Item Type box with five choices: Service Item, which is selected when the box opens, Inventory Item, Non Inventory Item, Inventory Assembly, and Add New Consultant. Use Service Item for time and fees, Inventory Item for goods you count on a shelf, and Non Inventory Item for goods you sell without tracking stock. Inventory Assembly defines a finished item built from parts.

Click Continue to open the matching form, or Cancel or the X to close the box. Add New Consultant opens the new consultant form instead of an item form. The Add a New Item button on Customer › Customers Items opens the same box.

Step 2

Set the price and accounts

The service form, New Customer Service Item, has two tabs. Basic Information asks for the required Service Name, for example Consulting hour, an optional Category (with Add New Category), and three tick boxes: Subitem of, which then asks for a required Parent Item, Commission item, and Active, which starts ticked. Click Next to reach Sales & Purchase Details.

I sell this service starts ticked, so Sales Price(USD) and Income Account are required; pick 4002 - Consulting Income - Income. Description on Sales Transaction is optional, and Standard Cost(USD) is always required, even if 0.00. Tick I buy this service to add Purchase Price(USD), a required Expense Account, and Description on Purchase Transaction. An Inventory Item or Inventory Assembly also needs a Cost of Good Sold account and an Assets Account.

Step 3

Save the item

On Sales & Purchase Details the buttons are Cancel, Save and New, and Save and Close. Save and Close shows Service item saved successfully! Click OK to go back. Save and New shows the same message ending Click OK to create another item. and clears the form. A missing or wrong value turns the field red with a message such as Service Name is required. or Sales Price must not be less than Purchase Price.

Saving an item posts nothing to the books. The item then appears on Customer › Customers Items, titled Items, which has a Status filter (All, Active, or Inactive), Export to CSV, and Add a New Item. Its columns are Active, Item Name, Description, Type, On Hand Quantity, Average Cost, and Sales Price.

Step 4

Use the item on an invoice

Outside the consulting industries, the New Invoice screen has an Items table with Name, Description, On Hand, Quantity, Unit Price, and Total. Pick the item in Item, check On Hand for a stock item, then type Quantity and Unit Price; Total is Quantity times Unit Price. Add New Item adds a row and Remove deletes one. Below the table, type any Tax Amount, and Sub Total and Total (USD) update.

Saving the invoice lowers on-hand quantity for a stock item. In consulting industries such as Northline's, invoices bill timesheets instead, so the Items table does not appear.

The invoice posts to Consulting Income unless you answer Yes when asked to use the item's own income account. The invoice does not post cost of goods.

More detail on this screen

Inventory Assembly defines a finished item built from components, and the definition does not move stock. Inventory › Build Assembly is the step that uses up parts and adds finished quantity. The Inventory Item form also has Inventory Name, I sell this product, Preferred Vendor, Vendor Product No, On Hand Quantity, Total(USD), As of, and Reorder Point. The Non Inventory Item form asks for Product Name, Items Group, and Weight, and has I sell this product and I buy this product boxes. On the service form, picking Add New Account in an account list shows a Go to Add New Account button. The same inventory item can be bought on a vendor bill, which raises its quantity. Editing an item does not rewrite invoices already saved.

3.3 Customer invoice

Customer Invoice, titled New Invoice on screen, bills a customer and posts the receivable. Create Invoice is the other screen, where you add consultant rows one at a time.

  1. 1Go toCustomer › New › Customer Invoice
  2. 2DoPick Customer Nameand Contact
  3. 3DoPick Payment TermsDue Date fills in
  4. 4DoAdd linesTimesheet Records or Items
  5. 5ClickSave and Closeor Save and New / Email
  6. 6ResultA/R ↑ · Income ↑INV-JE posted; tax → liability
  7. 7Go toCustomer › InvoicesStatus shows Sent
Step 1

Open Customer Invoice

Customer › New › Customer Invoice opens a page titled New Invoice. The Invoice Details card shows Date, Invoice No, and Order Number at the top right. Invoice No is filled in for you and cannot be typed over; Order Number is optional, for the customer's purchase order. The gear Settings button opens a panel where you connect Gmail or Outlook, choose an Email Theme, and switch Sales Tax, Bonus, Discount, and Draft Bill on or off.

In consulting industries, such as Northline's, the lower section is Timesheet Records; until you pick a customer it reads Select a Customer. In other industries it is an Items table. Attachments with Attach a file sits at the bottom for files that go with the invoice.

Step 2

Choose the customer and terms

The header has three columns. Customer Name, Payment Terms, and Contact are required, marked with a red star. Pick Cedar Schools in Customer Name: its Payment Terms (Net 15), Bill To address, and primary contact load from the customer record, so Contact shows Ruth Alvarez (ap@cedarschools.example). Email and Phone number fill in from the contact and cannot be typed over. Due Date is read-only and is worked out from Date and Payment Terms: 03/09/2026 plus 15 days is 03/24/2026.

Bill To is a free-text box you can edit, such as Cedar Schools, 410 Oak Avenue, Portland, OR 97205. Sales tax rows appear only when Sales Tax is turned on, in Settings › Account Configuration or with the gear on this screen.

Step 3

Add the lines

For Northline, the Timesheet Records heading counts Available timesheets and Added to invoice. Click Add Timesheet, pick Noah Kim in Consultant, then his 03/02/2026 to 03/06/2026 sheet in Timesheet. Hours (40) and Rate (USD) ($70.00, his Sales Price) fill in, and Total (USD) shows 2,800.00. Description is optional, a paperclip badge opens any file attached to the sheet, and Remove deletes the row.

Sub Total and Total (USD) sit under the table, with Comments beside them. If the customer has no open sheets you see No Timesheets Available and a Create New Timesheet button. In other industries, add rows in Items with Item, Quantity, and Unit Price, and Add New Item adds another row.

Step 4

Save and find it on Invoices

The bottom bar has Cancel, Save and Close, Save and New, and Save and Email. Each save stores the invoice as Sent and then posts its journal entry in the background, while a progress box ticks off Save invoice and Create journal entries, plus Send email for Save and Email. For INV-1052, accounts receivable and consulting income each rise by $2,800.00. Save and Email asks which email service to use, or opens Settings if none is connected.

Save and New leaves you on a fresh New Invoice. Cancel with unsaved changes asks Yes, Cancel or Continue Editing. The invoice then appears on Customer › Invoices and in Customer Center.

This screen has no draft button. If the journal step fails, the invoice is still saved and the progress box says the journal entries could not be created.

More detail on this screen

The journal debits Accounts Receivable and credits Consulting Income, plus Sales Tax Payable when tax is added. If an item or consultant points at a different income account, an Income Account Mapping question asks whether to use it; answering No keeps Consulting Income. Selling a stock item lowers on-hand quantity, but no cost of goods is posted. When Sales Tax, Bonus, or Discount is on, each consultant gets a small table with Add Sales Tax, Add Bonus, and Add Discount; Amount takes a percent or a number, such as 5% or 500. The Draft Bill switch in Settings creates a draft vendor bill when you create an invoice. On Customer › Invoices, the Status filter offers Over-Due, Open, Voided, All, Drafts, and Received. The invoice logo and layout come from Settings › Document Branding.

3.4 Create invoice from consultant time

Create Invoice bills a customer from timesheets already entered, one consultant row at a time. The consultant section appears when your company's industry is a consulting type.

  1. 1Go toConsultant › Consultant Time SheetEnter hours first
  2. 2Go toCustomer › New › Create Invoice
  3. 3DoPick Customer & terms
  4. 4DoAdd Consultant › TimesheetHours & Rate (USD) fill
  5. 5ClickSave and Closeor Save and New / Email

Step 1

Enter the timesheet first

Hours must exist before you can bill them. Consultant › Consultant Time Sheet lists every sheet with Consultant ID, Consultant Name, Customer, Vendor, From Date, To Date, Hours, Attachments, Timesheet Source, and Invoice Status, and you can filter by Customer, Consultant, Status, and Date. Click Add New Time Sheet to enter one. Aisha Rahman's sheet for Cedar Schools runs 03/02/2026 to 03/06/2026 with 36 hours, and its Invoice Status reads Time Sheet Created.

Aisha's Sales Price, $80.00 an hour, is the bill rate the invoice uses. Her Pay Rate, $50.00, is a different number used only for payroll.

Step 2

Open Create Invoice

Customer › New › Create Invoice has the same Invoice Details card as Customer Invoice: Date, a read-only Invoice No, an optional Order Number, and the required Customer, Payment Terms, and Contact. Pick Cedar Schools in Customer (the list also offers + Create Customer); Net 15 sets the read-only Due Date to 03/24/2026. Here Phone Number and Email can be typed over. Type CS-PO-2207 in Order Number.

Under Itemize by Consultant Hours, click Add Consultant, pick Aisha Rahman in Consultant, then her 03/02/2026 to 03/06/2026 sheet in Timesheet. Hours and Rate (USD) fill in from the sheet and her Sales Price. If the sheet is missing, pick + Create New Timesheet in the Timesheet list.

Step 3

Save the invoice

The line total is hours times rate: 36 × $80.00 = $2,880.00, shown in Total (USD). Sub Total and Total (USD) sit under the table. Bonus Amount (From Bonus entries), Discount Amount (From Discount entries), and Tax Amount (From Sales Tax entries) appear only when those options are on, along with Add Sales Tax, Add Bonus, and Add Discount under the consultant row. Comments and Attach a file are optional.

Click Save and Close, Save and New, or Save and Email. Saving stores INV-1053 as Sent and posts accounts receivable and consulting income the same way Customer Invoice does, with the same progress box.

Step 4

Collect it later

INV-1053 now waits on Customer › Receive Payment. Pick Cedar Schools in Received From and the invoice is listed under Due for payment with an Inv Amt and Due of 2,880.00 and a Sent status, next to INV-1052. It stays open until a payment is applied; Cedar paid $200.00 of it on 03/19/2026, leaving $2,680.00 (see Receive payment).

Payroll is a separate step that uses Aisha's Pay Rate, $50.00, not the $80.00 Sales Price. Do not bill the same timesheet again on another invoice.

More detail on this screen

Consultant › Consultant Invoice is a read-only list of invoice details with filters; it does not create invoices. If a sheet is missing, choose + Create New Timesheet in the Timesheet list to add one without leaving the screen; the Consultant list likewise ends with + Create Consultant. Sales Tax, Bonus, and Discount rows appear under a consultant only when those options are turned on, either in Settings › Account Configuration or with the gear Settings button on this screen. One customer can have several consultants, so add one row per consultant and sheet; Remove deletes a row. Companies outside the consulting industries see an Items table here instead, with a plain Tax Amount box.

3.5 Cash sale

New Cash Sale is meant for a sale the customer pays for at once. In the current version, its save buttons do not store the sale yet.

  1. 1Go toCustomer › New › New Cash Sale
  2. 2DoPick Customer & Deposit InBasic Information › Next
  3. 3DoAdd product linesName, Quantity, Unit Price
  4. 4DoPick TaxRequired field
  5. 5ClickSave and Close
  6. 6Not posted yetNot savedNot connected to server yet
Step 1

Open New Cash Sale

Customer › New › New Cash Sale has two tabs, Basic Information and Lines & Totals. At the top, No starts empty for you to type and Date shows the sale date. Customer and Deposit In are required. Payment Method is also required but starts as Cash; choosing Cheque adds a Cheque No box preset to TO BE PRINTED. Contact, Phone, and Bill To are optional. Deposit In lists your accounts as number, name, and type, plus Add New Account.

Click Next to go to Lines & Totals, or Cancel to leave. If Customer or Deposit In is empty, a message lists Customer is required. or Deposit account is required. and you stay on the tab.

Step 2

Enter the lines and tax

Lines & Totals has two sections that fold open or shut when you click their headings. Itemize by Account has Name, Description, Amount(USD), Total, and Taxable, and Add New Account adds a row. Itemize by Product/Service has Name, Description, Quantity, Unit Price, Total, and Taxable, and Add New Item adds a row. In both, Name is typed text, not a pick list of your accounts or items, and Delete removes a row.

Tax is required: pick GST 18%, VAT 5%, or No Tax. Amount includes tax is a tick box. Net Amount (USD) and Total (USD) add up all rows; the tax choice does not change them. The comments box is labeled Write comments for this New Cash Sale.

Step 3

Save and Close does not store it

On Lines & Totals the bottom bar has Cancel, Save and New, and Save and Close. In the current version neither save button stores the sale. Each first checks Customer, Deposit In, and Tax, then shows Save and Close is not yet connected to the server. or the Save and New version; Save and New also clears the form and returns you to Basic Information.

Make it recurring and Save as draft, above the bar, only show messages that they are not implemented yet. No income, bank balance, or stock quantity changes, and nothing appears in Customer Center or on any report.

Step 4

Record cash sales with Make Deposit

To record a sale paid on the spot today, go to Banking › Make Deposit. Under Deposit Information pick the bank in the required Deposit To; Payment Method, Deposit Date, and No are optional. Under Deposit Items, click Add New Payee if you need a row, pick the Payee, and set Deposit From to an income account such as Consulting Income. A No prior invoice tick box then appears in the Cash Sale column; tick it, and type a Description and the Total.

Click Save and Close. Until Deposit To and one complete row are filled in, the page shows Form Incomplete and the save buttons stay off.

Make Deposit records the income and the bank deposit only. It does not lower stock quantity or post cost of goods.

More detail on this screen

The sample values on these screens are for illustration; Northline recorded no cash sales in March. Payment Method on New Cash Sale offers Cash, Cheque, Credit Card, Direct Deposit, Master card, Online Banking, Standing Order, Switch/Maestro, and Paypal. Do not also create an invoice for a sale you record through Make Deposit, or the income counts twice. If the customer will pay later, use Customer Invoice and then Receive Payment. Make Deposit posts its journal entry when saved, but the bank account's register line appears only after the matching bank-statement transaction is imported and linked. Accounts Receivable is not allowed as a Deposit From account.

Open New Credit Memo

Customer › New › New Credit Memo opens a page titled New Credit Note with two tabs, Basic Information and Lines & Consultants. At the top, Credit No starts empty for you to type, next to Date. Customer Name is the only required field; for Maple Dental's price correction, pick Maple Dental. Contact, Bill To, Payment Terms, Order Number, Phone number, and Email are optional.

Each checks that a customer is picked, then shows Save and Close is not yet connected to the server. or the Save and New version. Because the credit is not stored, the original invoice stays fully open in Customer Center and on Receive Payment. On Receive Payment, Maple Dental's INV-1050 shows an Inv Amt and Due of 3700.00 under Due for payment. When you receive payment, type only the cash that actually arrives in Payment; any unpaid remainder stays open.

A credit memo is not a payment and does not move the bank. Customer › Customer Refund is the separate screen that pays money back. Picking a consultant in the Consultant Name table fills in its Description and Hourly Rate, which you can still change. Customer Center's Current View has a Customer Credit Notes choice, but it stays empty because no credit note is stored.

3.7 Customer prepayment

Customer Prepayment records money received before an invoice exists, such as a deposit or retainer. In the current version it raises the bank account's balance only; it posts no journal entry and does not change the customer balance.

  1. 1Go toCustomer › Customer Prepayment
  2. 2DoPick Customer NameEnter Amount(USD)
  3. 3DoPick Deposit Inand Payment Method
  4. 4ClickSave and Close
  5. 5ResultBank balance ↑ onlyNo journal; customer unchanged
Step 1

Open Customer Prepayment

Customer › Customer Prepayment is a single page with no tabs. Use it when money arrives before any invoice exists, so Receive Payment has nothing to apply it to. Date, set to today, and the required No sit at the top right. No starts at 1, so type your own number, such as PP-4, and change Date to the day the money arrived, 03/14/2026.

The left column holds the payment fields, and the right column shows the Address of the customer you pick, read-only. Comments is optional. The bottom bar has Cancel, Save and New, and Save and Close.

Step 2

Enter the customer and amount

Lumen Labs pays $500.00 by check 7781 ahead of Ethan Brooks's invoice. Pick Lumen Labs in the required Customer Name and enter 500 in the required Amount(USD). Payment Method is also required and starts empty; pick Cheque, and a Cheque/Ref No box appears for 7781. The other choices are Cash, Credit Card, Direct Deposit, Master card, Online Banking, Standing Order, Switch/Maestro, and Paypal.

Customer Balance(USD) and Bank Balance(USD) are read-only and are there for reference: the customer's current balance and the stored balance of the Deposit In account. Lumen Labs shows 0.00 because its invoices are not posted until 03/20/2026.

Step 3

Choose Deposit In and save

Deposit In is required and lists Bank, Cash, and Other current Asset accounts as name, number, and type; pick Checking Account (1010) - Bank. Bank Balance(USD) then shows Checking's stored balance. Add New Account at the end of the list opens the new-account form instead.

Click Save and Close: Prepayment recorded successfully appears and you return to the page you came from. Save and New saves and clears the customer, account, amount, and method for the next one. Checking's stored balance rises by $500.00, and Lumen Labs's customer balance does not change.

No journal entry is posted, so journal-based reports such as the General Ledger do not include this $500.

Step 4

Know what happens at invoice time

There is no screen that applies a prepayment to an invoice yet. When Ethan's INV-1056 for $4,400.00 is posted on 03/20/2026, it stays open for the full $4,400.00, and the $500.00 is not subtracted automatically.

On Receive Payment, Lumen Labs then lists INV-1056 and INV-1057 under Due for payment, and Customer Balance (USD) reads 7520.00. The Credits column can be typed by hand, but it is not linked to the prepayment record, and it does not post to the journal.

More detail on this screen

Receive Payment has a Credits column, but you type it by hand and it is not linked to prepayment records. Deposit In lists Bank, Cash, and Other current Asset accounts, and Add New Account opens a form for a new one. Prepayments do not appear in Customer Center; Company › Transactions Center › Customers lists them under Client Payments. If the prepayment saves but the screen cannot refresh the customer or account, a warning says Prepayment saved, but failed to update customer info. or the deposit account version.

3.8 Receive payment

Receive Payment collects money against one or more open invoices for a customer. Saving posts a journal entry that lowers accounts receivable and raises the account you deposit into.

  1. 1Go toCustomer › Receive Payment
  2. 2DoPick Received FromLists 'Due for payment'
  3. 3DoEnter Amount & Deposit Inand Payment Method
  4. 4DoTick invoices, enter PaymentWrite Off / Credits optional
  5. 5ClickSave and Close
  6. 6ResultA/R ↓ · Deposit In ↑RCPT-JE posted
  7. 7DecideDeposited to Undeposited Funds?YesBanking › Make DepositNoBank already up
Step 1

Choose who paid

Customer › Receive Payment shows Date and No at the top right; No is filled in for you and read-only, here RP-302. Received From is required: pick Harbor Clinic, and the read-only Customer Balance (USD) shows 7000.00. If you came from the Receive Payment button in Customer Center, or the Receipt button on Invoices, the customer is already picked and a Pre-selected note appears under the field.

The Due for payment table then lists Harbor's open invoices, INV-1048 for 3400.00 and INV-1049 for 3600.00, both due 03/24/2026. Its columns are Exp, Invoice, Due (date), Inv Amt, Due (amount still unpaid), Write Off, Credits, Payment, and Status. The arrow in Exp expands an invoice to show its consultant lines.

Step 2

Enter the amount and Deposit In

Payment Method, Amount Received (USD), and Deposit In are required. Harbor paid with one check, so pick Check; a Cheque/Ref No box appears for the check number. Enter 7000 in Amount Received (USD). Deposit In lists Bank and Other current Asset accounts, as the note under Comments says, plus Add New Account. Comments is optional.

Choose a bank account, such as Checking Account (1010) - Bank, if the money is already in the bank, or Undeposited Funds if you will group checks on one deposit slip. Harbor's $7,000.00 goes to Undeposited Funds and reaches Checking later through Make Deposit.

Step 3

Apply it and save

Tick each invoice, or the box in the header row to tick them all, and type its Payment: 3400 and 3600 clear both Harbor invoices, and each Status adds → Will be marked as Paid. A line's Payment cannot exceed what is still owed on it. If all Payments add up to more than Amount Received (USD), you see Total payment cannot exceed Amount Received (USD). and the save buttons turn off.

A partial amount leaves the rest open: Cedar's $3,000.00 clears Noah's $2,800.00 and puts $200.00 on Aisha's INV-1053, which shows Will be marked as Partially Paid and keeps $2,680.00 open. The strip below shows Total Write Off (USD), Total Applied Credits (USD), and Total Payment (USD). Click Save and Close.

Saving does not raise income again, because that happened when the invoice was posted. Do not receive the same invoice twice.

Step 4

Make Deposit for undeposited receipts

If Deposit In was Undeposited Funds, go to Banking › Make Deposit. The Undeposited Customer Payments table lists each waiting payment with Date, Payment No, Customer, and Amount. Click Add next to RP-302 for Harbor Clinic to copy it into Deposit Items. Under Deposit Information, pick Checking Account (1010) in Deposit To, set Deposit Date to 03/18/2026, and type D-78 in No.

Click Save and Close. Deposit D-78 moves the $7,000.00 into Checking as one lump sum, matching the line on the bank statement. It does not add income again.

More detail on this screen

If Deposit In was a bank account, the bank rises when you save the receipt and no Make Deposit is needed. Saving posts the payment's journal entry in the background, with a progress box like the one on invoices. Make Deposit posts its journal entry when saved, but the bank register line appears only after the matching bank-statement transaction is imported and linked. Write Off and Credits lower what an invoice shows as due, but they are not recorded in the journal, so accounts receivable in the ledger does not drop for them. The gear button beside each Status opens Invoice Settings, where Bonus and Discount can be switched on; the Bonus and Discount columns, Add Bonus, and Add Discount then appear, and they post to bonus income or sales discounts.

Step 1

Know why money goes back

Maple Dental paid INV-1050 in full and is owed $50.00 back after a price correction. In the current version the credit memo for that $50.00 cannot be saved, so Customer Center still shows Maple Dental | C-1004 with Balance: $0.00.

Its Transactions list shows the 03/09/2026 invoice INV-1050 as Received for $3,700.00 and the 03/10/2026 received payment RP-301 for the same amount. Nothing in AnnaBooks records that Maple is owed money, so you pay the $50.00 back yourself on Customer Refund.

Step 2

Open Customer Refund

Customer › Customer Refund has Date, set to today, and No at the top right; No starts at 1, so type RF-0001 and set Date to 03/26/2026. Pick Maple Dental in the required Pay To. The read-only Customer Balance(USD) then shows 0.00, and the Address box on the right shows Maple's address.

Enter 50 in the required Amount(USD). Payment Method is required and starts as Cash; Northline pays by check, so pick Cheque. That adds a TO BE PRINTED tick box and a Cheque No box preset to TO BE PRINTED; type 1001. Write comments for this Customer Refund. is an optional note.

Step 3

Choose Pay From and save

Pick the required Pay From account, Checking Account (1010) - Bank; the list holds Cash, Bank, and Paypal accounts, plus Add New Account. Bank Balance(USD) then shows that account's stored balance. If Amount(USD) is higher, the field shows Amount cannot exceed Bank Balance. and nothing saves.

Click Save and Close: Customer refund saved successfully! appears and you return to the page you came from. Save and New saves and clears the form for the next refund. Checking's stored balance drops by $50.00, but Maple's customer balance does not change.

No journal entry is posted, so journal-based reports such as the General Ledger do not show this refund.

Step 4

Review the Invoices list

Customer › Invoices opens the Invoices List. Filter by Customer, Status (Over-Due, Open, Voided, All, Drafts, Received; All at first), Date, From Date, and To Date, or search by Invoice No. The Summary shows Total Invoice Amount, Total Balance, and Amount Collected, plus Invoice Counts, a Financial Summary with the Collection Rate, and a Status Breakdown.

Columns are Invoice No, Date, Customer Name, Due Date, Total Amount, Balance, Status, and Voided. Click Details to see a row's creation logs, journal entries, and timesheets, or PDF to open the invoice PDF. Sent invoices also have Receipt, which opens Receive Payment for that invoice. Draft rows are not open receivables. Export to CSV, Print, and Add a New Invoice sit at the top.

More detail on this screen

Refunds do not appear in Customer Center or in the bank register; Company › Transactions Center › Customers lists them under Client Refunds. If you pay by Cheque, a Cheque No field appears, preset to TO BE PRINTED, and switching back to another method resets it. Pay From cannot use an Other current Asset account such as Undeposited Funds. On Invoices, Export to CSV and Print use the filtered rows and are greyed out when no invoice matches, and Add a New Invoice opens Customer Invoice. A partly paid invoice shows the raw status partially_paid on this list and has no Receipt button.
