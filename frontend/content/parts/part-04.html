4. Consultants, time, and payroll

4.1 Consultant center and a new consultant

The Consultant menu appears only when the company industry is ITConsulting. A consultant is a person you place with a customer, set up as Fulltime, 1099, or C2C.

  1. 1Go toConsultant › New › New ConsultantMenu shows for ITConsulting industry
  2. 2DecideMultiple service agreements?YesMulti form (4.2)NoSingle Service Agreement
  3. 3DoBasic InformationConsultant Type, Customer
  4. 4DoService InformationSales Price = bill rate; Pay Rate
  5. 5ClickSave and CloseShown on last tab (Next)
  6. 6Not posted yetNothing postedNext: Consultant Time Sheet
Step 1

Confirm the Consultant menu is visible

The Consultant menu sits at the top of the left sidebar, but only when the company industry is ITConsulting. Other industries, including Professional Consulting, do not show it. Click Consultant to open its list: Consultant Center, New, Consultants, Received Payments, Consultant Time Sheet, Timesheet Summary, Consultant Invoice, Consultant Contract, Benefits, and Payroll Management.

New, Consultant Contract, Benefits, and Payroll Management have an arrow: clicking one opens a short sub-list instead of a page. Consultant Center shows one consultant at a time, with Customer, Status, Consultant Scope, and Consultant Type filters and the buttons Export to CSV and Add New Consultant.

Add New Consultant on Consultant Center opens the single-agreement form straight away. To choose between a single and multiple agreements, use Consultant › New › New Consultant.

Step 2

Open New Consultant and pick the type

Choose Consultant › New › New Consultant. The Select Consultant Type dialog asks whether this consultant has multiple service agreements or a single service agreement. Click Single Service Agreement, then Continue; Continue stays greyed out until a card is picked, and Cancel closes the dialog. The form opens on Basic Information.

Consultant ID, Consultant Name, Email, Phone, and Consultant Type are required. For Priya enter NL-101, Priya Shah, her email and phone, and pick Fulltime - Client. An ID already in use shows This Consultant ID already exists in this company. Pick Customer: Harbor Clinic; Invoicing Frequency and Payment Terms can then fill in, marked Automatically selected from customer. Timesheet Frequency, Commission item, and Active are optional. Click Next. Nothing posts yet.

Step 3

Set Sales Price and Pay Rate

Service Information starts with I sell this service ticked; I buy this service appears only for C2C. Under Sell Service Configuration, Sales Price (USD) is required and is the hourly bill rate the customer pays: $85.00 for Priya. Income Account is required: pick 4002 - Consulting Income. Standard Cost (USD) is also required and starts equal to the Sales Price; you can change it. Description on Sales Transaction is optional invoice text.

Fulltime and 1099 consultants also get Pay Rate, with the hint This is what you pay the consultant at payroll time. Enter $55.00; the box also accepts a percentage of the Sales Price, such as 80%. Tick Fixed Salary instead to enter Fixed Salary (USD). Click Next.

C2C consultants get both boxes ticked and a Buy Service Configuration with Purchase Price (USD), Expense Account, and Preferred Vendor instead of a Pay Rate. They are paid by vendor bill, not through payroll.

Step 4

Save, then enter time

Click Next through Address Information, Payroll Details, and Benefits to Contract Information, the last tab. To record the placement contract, enter Contract Number CT-201 and Start Date 01/05/2026, both required once you start a contract, plus End Date 12/31/2026, Status Active, and Auto Renew. Contract Type follows the Consultant Type and cannot be changed here.

On the last tab, Next is replaced by Save and New and Save and Close. Click Save and Close; the message reads Consultant saved successfully! Click OK to go back to the previous page. Save and New saves and clears the form; Cancel leaves without saving. Priya then appears in Consultant › Consultants and Consultant Center. Nothing posts: income waits for the invoice, and pay waits for a processed payroll run.

More detail on this screen

The tabs run Basic Information, Service Information, Address Information, Payroll Details, Benefits, and Contract Information; Payroll Details and Benefits appear only for Fulltime consultants. Consultant Type offers Fulltime - Client, Fulltime - Company, 1099, and C2C. Fulltime - Company staff work for your own company, so they have no customer, no Service Information tab, and no Contract Information tab; you enter their Pay Rate, or tick Fixed Salary and enter Fixed Salary (USD), on Basic Information. Address Information holds up to two addresses, added with Add Address. Dropdowns such as Customer, Payment Terms, and Income Account end with an Add New option, so you can create a missing record without leaving the form. The Income Account on Service Information is where invoiced hours post. A new Sales Price applies to invoices you create afterward; posted invoices keep their rate. To change someone later, open them from Consultants and click Save Changes.

The form opens as New Consultant (Multiple Service Agreements), with the tabs Basic Information, Service Agreements, and Address Information, plus Payroll Details and Benefits for Fulltime.

Basic Information asks for the same required fields as a single consultant: Consultant ID, Consultant Name, Email, Phone, and Consultant Type. For Grace Liu that is NL-109 and Fulltime - Client. Step 4

On her time sheet row, the Create Invoice for this timesheet icon opens a new invoice for that agreement's customer, with Customer Name filled in and a line showing Consultant, Timesheet, Hours, and Rate (USD). More detail on this screen

The Select Consultant Type dialog opens every time you click New Consultant, so this is a choice, not a separate menu item. When I sell this service is ticked, the card's Invoicing Frequency, Payment Terms, and Timesheet Frequency sit inside Sell Service Configuration; C2C agreements also tick I buy this service and add Purchase Price(USD), Expense Account, Preferred Vendor, and Vendor Service No. Saving without a primary agreement shows At least one service agreement must be set as Primary Agreement. This form has no Contract Information tab; contracts for these consultants are handled from Consultants. The time sheet list marks these people with a Multiple Agreements icon, and Timesheet Summary can filter its consultant list with the Multiple and Single chips. Contract Details lists one contract per agreement.

Consultant › Consultant Time Sheet lists every sheet. The buttons are HR Timesheets, Bulk Actions (n), and Add New Time Sheet. Click Add New Time Sheet; the Add A New Time Sheet window opens.

Step 2

The sheet appears in the list with Timesheet Source Manual Entry and Invoice Status Time Sheet Created. It does not create the customer invoice and does not pay Priya. While the status is Time Sheet Created, the Edit timesheet icon reopens it as Edit Time Sheet, where Save Changes stores your fixes. Once the sheet is invoiced, the edit icon disappears.

Step 4

Invoice it, then pay it in a run

In the Invoice Actions column, the Create Invoice for this timesheet icon opens a new customer invoice for Harbor Clinic with the sheet attached: 40 hours at Rate (USD) $85.00 = $3,400.00. Saving it as INV-1048 changes the sheet to Invoice Done, and the icon becomes View Invoice. After Receive Payment the status reads Payment Received, with a View Payment icon.

Pay is a separate step. Payroll Management › Create Payroll › Run picks up the same 40 hours at the Pay Rate: 40 × $55.00 = $2,200.00. Because an invoiced sheet can no longer be edited, a wrong posted invoice is corrected with a credit memo.

More detail on this screen

The Upload Document for Data Extraction switch adds a required Upload Time Sheet Document box: upload a PDF or image, click Extract Data, and the dates and hours fill in, marked Data Extracted. If reading fails, the form shows Data extraction was unsuccessful and a Retry Extraction button; you can enter the details by hand and the file stays attached. Once the sheet is invoiced its status changes to Invoice Done; a partial receipt shows Partial Payment Received and full payment Payment Received. For C2C consultants the list adds Bill Status and Bill Actions, with Enter Bill for this timesheet, because they are paid by vendor bill. The consultant's Timesheet Frequency controls the allowed range: Weekly must span exactly 7 days, Bi-Weekly 14 days, Semi-Monthly the 1st to the 15th or the 16th to month end, Monthly a full month, and Quarterly a full quarter, and changing From Date moves To Date to match. Bulk Actions (n) applies one action to several selected sheets.

Approved and Processed cards that carry notes show a notes icon that opens the approval notes. AnnaBooks also has an Import Rewa HR Timesheets screen that fetches sheets from the Rewa HR app by customer, dates, and consultant, but its button is currently hidden, so use HR Timesheets. Timesheet Summary also shows the customer rate and vendor rate behind each consultant's time, and C2C cards add a bill status.

More detail on this screen

A benefit applies only between its Effective Date and End Date. A contribution is a fixed Amount ($) or a Percent (%) of earnings, and Frequency (Weekly, Biweekly, Semimonthly, Monthly, Per Pay Period, or Annually) scales it to the pay period. You can add a new category, type, or plan straight from the consultant's Benefits tab, using Add New Benefit Type or Add New Benefit Plan, without leaving the form. The same plan cannot be given to one person twice for overlapping dates; the tab shows an error until you change the period or remove the duplicate. Benefit Category groups types and plans. The Tax Summary report lists tax amounts recorded on processed runs; it does not file tax.
