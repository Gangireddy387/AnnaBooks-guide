2. Inside the company

2.1 Company dashboard

The Dashboard has two tabs: Quick Access, a catalog of links, and Company Details, a board of widgets you can show or hide. A company opens on Overview, so reach the Dashboard from the top bar.

  1. 1Go toTop bar › Dashboardor Company › Dashboard
  2. 2DoQuick Access tabDefault tab; links by module
  3. 3DoCompany Details tabThe widget board
  4. 4DoConfigure › tick widgetsOK saves in this browser
  5. 5Do× Close widgetHides one widget
  6. 6DoAsk Anna (Ctrl+/)Reads only; never posts
Step 1

Open the Dashboard

Click Dashboard in the top bar, or open Company › Dashboard in the sidebar. The page has an Anna AI Agent button at the top right and two tabs: Quick Access, which is selected when the page opens, and Company Details.

Click Company Details to switch to the widget board. Its heading reads Company Details, with the line Overview of banking, sales, expenses, and key business metrics. Two buttons sit beside it: Configure chooses which widgets show, and Import From CSV opens Import From File. Switching tabs saves nothing and changes no records.

Step 2

Read the widgets

Company Details starts with every widget shown, in a fixed order: Profit & Loss and Cash Flow first, then Money Coming In, Money Going Out, Who Owes Me, and Who I Owe, with Bank Account last. Profit & Loss shows Net Profit with Total Income and Total Expenses below it. Who Owes Me lists Customer and Amount with a View all invoices link, and Bank Account lists each Account and its Balance.

Widgets read saved records only. A new company shows zero amounts, No outstanding invoices, or No bank accounts until the first save. With Northline's March records, Net Profit is $4,027.60 and Who Owes Me lists Lumen Labs and Cedar Schools. You cannot edit anything here; use a widget's link to open the full list.

Step 3

Show or hide widgets

Each widget has a × button at its top right, named Close widget. Click it to hide that widget at once. To change several at a time, click Configure. The Configure Widgets panel opens with a Select All box at the top, which reads Deselect All when every box is ticked, and one checkbox per widget, from Banking to Bills Status.

Tick the widgets you want and click OK. Cancel, or the × at the top of the panel, closes it without changes. Your choice is stored in this browser for this company only, so another browser or another user still sees the full board. Hiding a widget deletes no records; tick it again to bring it back.

Step 4

Use Quick Access and the assistant

Quick Access starts with a search box, Search menus, pages, reports...; press / to jump into it. Type part of a screen name, then click a match or press Enter. Recently Used lists the last shortcuts you opened, with a Clear button. Below come shortcut groups: Consultant for ITConsulting companies, then Customer, Vendor, Inventory, Banking, Settings, Company, and Reports, each showing how many shortcuts it holds.

Click a card, such as Receive Payment under Customer, to open that screen. Create Bill and Pay Bill appear under Vendor only when the company manages bills. Press Ctrl+/ or click Anna AI Agent to open the Anna assistant, which searches and answers questions about the open company but never saves or posts.

More detail on this screen

Company Details also has Import From CSV, which opens Import From File. Configure Widgets also lists Banking, Expenses, Account Balances, Top Customers By Sales, Top Items By Sales, Top Vendors By Expenses, Incomes, Income and Expense Account, Year Over Year Income, Year Over Year Expense, Invoices Status, Bills Status, Unpaid Bills, and Messages And Tasks. Quick Access remembers the shortcuts you use in this browser; Clear empties Recently Used, and a search with no hits shows No shortcuts match your search. The top bar offers Home, Dashboard, Companies, Tracker, Reports Home, and Company Preferences. Customer, vendor, and consultant pages also have an Anna AI Agent button.

2.2 Overview work map

Overview is the Home page in the top bar and the first page you see after opening a company. It maps three paths of daily work and never posts anything itself.

  1. 1Go toTop bar › HomeOverview; first page after opening
  2. 2DoSales rowInvoices › Customers › Receive Payment › Reconciliation
  3. 3DoConsultants & timesheets rowTimesheets › Consultants › Make Deposit
  4. 4DoPurchases rowBills › Vendors › Pay Bill
  5. 5Go toClick a nodeOpens screen; map never posts
Step 1

Open Overview

Click Home in the top bar, or the AnnaBooks logo at its left end; opening a company card also lands here. Overview is a map, not a form. Four headings run across the top, Documents, Entry Points, Payments, and Banking, and three rows run down the left side: Sales, Consultants & timesheets, and Purchases.

Each box shows a title and a short line saying what it does, and arrows show the order of the work. Point at a row to highlight its path. Clicking a box opens that screen; the map itself never saves or posts anything. Below the map, Supporting Modules links to other everyday screens.

Step 2

Follow Sales for customer invoices

The Sales row has four boxes. Invoices, Create and send invoices, opens the invoice list. Customers, Manage customer accounts, opens Customer Center. Receive Payment, Apply payments to invoices, opens the payment screen. Bank Reconciliation, Match bank transactions, opens the reconciliation history.

Read the row left to right: record what the customer owes, look after the customer, collect the money, then match it to the bank statement. For Northline, INV-1053 for Cedar Schools is listed under Invoices, and Cedar's $3,000.00 check, RP-303, is entered on Receive Payment. Clicking a box only opens the screen; nothing is created until you save there.

Step 3

Follow Consultants & timesheets

The middle row reads Timesheets, Create first; Consultants, C2C · 1099 · Full-time; and Make Deposit, Deposit funds to bank, followed by an arrow marked → Bank. Timesheets opens Consultant Time Sheet, Consultants opens Consultant Center, and Make Deposit moves money you received into a bank account.

Point at a box to read its tip. Consultants says Step 1: Select consultant. Next: create timesheet. Timesheets says Step 2: Create timesheet. Then create bills/invoices. So choose the consultant, enter the hours, invoice them, and deposit the payment. For Northline, Priya Shah's 40 hours for 03/02/2026 to 03/06/2026 start on Timesheets.

This row shows for every industry, but the Consultant menu exists only for ITConsulting companies.

Step 4

Follow Purchases when you owe a vendor

The bottom row reads Bills, Record vendor bills; Vendors, Manage vendor accounts; and Pay Bill, Pay outstanding bills, followed by → Bank. Bills opens Create Bill, Vendors opens Vendor Center, and Pay Bill opens the screen where you pay open bills.

Entering a bill records what you owe and raises Accounts Payable; the bank does not change yet. Paying the bill on Pay Bill is the step that lowers the bank. For Northline, Deskline Supplies' bill DS-8891 for $186.40 is entered under Bills on 03/06/2026 and paid with check 2204 on 03/13/2026.

More detail on this screen

Below the map, Supporting Modules groups its links: Inventory has Items; Banking has Accounts, Write Check, and Transfer Funds; Reports has Reports; Journal Entries has Journal Entries; and Company has Chart of Accounts and Transactions. Each link only opens its screen. Use the Dashboard for balances and Tracker for due dates.

2.3 Tracker

Tracker is the working list of invoices and bills by due date, opened from Tracker in the top bar. It helps you collect and pay on time; it is not a financial statement.

  1. 1Go toTop bar › Tracker
  2. 2DoInvoices or Bills & PaymentsPick a tab
  3. 3DoSet Status, Due daysDate range too, then Apply
  4. 4DoOpen a rowDetails only; no cash moves
  5. 5DecideInvoice row?YesCustomer › Receive PaymentNoVendor › Pay Bill
  6. 6Not posted yetDraft bills not payableOpen it, Save and Close first
Step 1

Open Tracker and pick a tab

Click Tracker in the top bar. Two tabs sit at the top, each with a record count: Invoices, for customer invoices, and Bills & Payments, for bills, expenses, and vendor payments. Export to CSV and Print sit above the filters. Under the filters, a Summary box shows the tab's totals.

On Invoices the Summary shows Total Invoiced Amount, Overdue Amount, Amount Paid, and Outstanding Balance, with Total Invoice Count, Overdue Invoice Count, Paid Invoice Count, and Open Invoice Count below. On Bills & Payments it shows Total Bills, Amount Paid to Vendors, and Outstanding Balance, with Bills Count, Payments Count, and Unpaid Bills Count. For Northline's March invoices, Outstanding Balance is $10,200.00. Switching tabs saves nothing.

Step 2

Filter by date, status, or due days

The filter bar starts with Date range, set to All, which also offers This week, This month, Last week, Last month, financial year and quarter choices, and Custom. From and To take dates as MM/DD/YYYY. On Invoices, Status offers All, Received, Sent, Draft, Partially Paid, Overdue, and Cancelled. Due days offers Due today, Due in next 7 days, Due in next 30 days, Not yet due, Overdue (any), and bands from Overdue 1–30 days to Overdue over 90 days.

Search, with Search no, name, status..., finds a number or a name. Status, Due days, and Search narrow the list as soon as you change them. Changing Date range, From, or To reloads the list, and Apply reloads it on demand. Filters only change what you see.

Step 3

Open the row you will act on

The Invoices table shows SNO, Consultants, Customers, Invoice, InvoiceDate, DueDate, Due Days, Overdue Days, aging columns from 0-30 days to >90, Total, OverDue, Status, and Action. Click a heading such as DueDate to sort by it. The Consultants cell, for example 1 line(s), opens the invoice's lines inside the table.

Click anywhere else on an invoice row to open its details window. It shows Invoice date, Due date, Status, Invoice total, Paid, and Balance, with tabs for Line items, Consultant & timesheets, and Journal entries. On Bills & Payments, clicking a bill or expense row expands its details instead. Opening a row does not collect or pay anything.

Step 4

Receive the payment or pay the bill

Tracker has no payment button. The Action column holds a Send Email button, shown for unpaid invoices that are overdue or due for a dunning reminder, and a Notes button for notes on the invoice or bill. Emailing a reminder or adding a note changes no balance.

To collect INV-1053, go to Customer › Receive Payment, choose Cedar Schools, and apply the payment to the invoice. To pay a bill, go to Vendor › Pay Bills › Pay Bill. A draft bill is not posted yet and cannot be paid until you open it on Create Bill and click Save and Close. Return to Tracker afterwards to see the new Outstanding Balance.

More detail on this screen

Invoice statuses are Received, Sent, Draft, Partially Paid, Overdue, and Cancelled. Bill statuses are Draft, Open, Paid, Overdue, Voided, Bill, and Expense. Drafts, voided, and cancelled records are left out of outstanding totals. The Bills & Payments table adds Vendor Name, Original Amount, Balance, and Paid Amount columns. Headings with sort arrows reorder the list, and the pager at the bottom sets how many records show per page. An empty list reads No data found for the selected date range. Once a reminder has gone out that day, Send Email shows a check mark and the tip Email already sent today.

2.4 Owner invites users

The owner can share one company with other people without copying its books. Invitations are managed in Settings › User Management › Users.

  1. 1Go toSettings › User Management › Users
  2. 2DoInvite a User
  3. 3DoEmail, first, last nameEmail they will sign in with
  4. 4DecideSet a User role?YesPick a labelNoNot specified
  5. 5ClickSave and closeor Save and new
  6. 6Not posted yetInvite pendingEmail with Accept Invitation link
Step 1

Open Users and click Invite a User

In the sidebar, open Settings › User Management › Users. The heading reads Users, with the line Manage company members, invitations, and sign-in access. The owner also sees two tabs, Members and Log email schedule. On Members, the list on the left shows each person who joined, with (owner) beside the company owner, followed by invitations not yet accepted.

Click a name to see its details on the right: First name, Last name, Email, User role, and Status, plus Availability and Last seen at for members. Click Invite a User at the top right to open the invitation form. Invited users do not see this button.

Step 2

Enter the email and name

Invite a user opens with the line Send an invitation with account details and an optional user role. Under Account details, which notes The invited user will use the email to sign in., are three boxes. Email is required and shows name@company.com until you type. First name and Last name are optional and appear on the invitation and on the accept page.

Type the exact address the person will sign in with. If Email is empty, saving stops with Email is required. A typo sends the invitation to the wrong inbox, so check it before you save. Nothing is sent until you click a save button at the bottom of the form.

Step 3

Choose a role label if you want one

User role, marked Optional job title for this person., lists five choices, each with a short description: Not specified (No formal role label.), Accountant (Books, reconciliations, and accounting operations.), Finance manager (Reporting, budgets, and cash-flow oversight.), Payroll manager (Pay runs and payroll-related records.), and Auditor (Review and verification of financial records.). Not specified is selected at first.

Click one choice. The label appears in the invitation email, on the accept page, and as a badge on the person's details in Users. It does not limit what the person can open or change in the company, so invite only people you trust with the books.

Step 4

Save, then they accept

The bottom of the form has Cancel, Save and close, and Save and new. Save and close saves the invitation, sends the email, and returns you to Users. Save and new does the same but clears the form for the next person. The button reads Saving… while it works, and Cancel returns to Users without saving.

The email has an Accept Invitation button, and a person with no AnnaBooks account also gets a Temporary password. Until they accept, the invitation stays in the Users list with Status Invite sent. After they accept, they appear as a member, and the company card opens the same Northline books you see, not a copy.

If company email sending is not set up, the invitation is still saved, but a warning says the email was not sent.

More detail on this screen

Select a pending invitation in the Users list and click Edit to open Edit invitation, change Email, the names, or User role, and click Save changes. No new email is sent, and the existing link shows the updated details. Delete asks Delete invitation for that address; click Yes, continue. For a member who joined, Remove asks Remove member and takes the company off their list, and Sign-in access offers Allow sign in and Block sign in; a blocked user cannot log in until you allow it again. The owner can click View logs on a member, and Company › Company Lists › Users Activity Log shows who changed what. Inviting a vendor is a different screen, Vendor › Invites & Submitted, and opens only the vendor portal.

2.5 Accept invite

Accept invitation is the page the email link opens, outside the company sidebar. Accepting signs you in and adds the shared company to your list.

  1. 1Go toEmail › Accept InvitationOpens Accept invitation page
  2. 2DoReview company and roleSign-in email is fixed
  3. 3DoConfirm first, last name
  4. 4ClickACCEPT INVITATIONCreates account if new; signs in
  5. 5Go toCompanies you can accessOpen the shared card
Step 1

Open the email link

Click Accept Invitation in the email. The page opens outside the company, with You're invited on the left and the heading Accept invitation on the right. Under it, a new user reads Confirm your details to join this company account.; someone who already has an account reads Confirm your details to add this company to your account.

Three greyed-out boxes cannot be changed: Company, the shared company's name; User role, shown only if the owner chose one; and Sign-in email, the address the invitation went to. If the link is broken or has expired, a red message and a Go to login button appear instead of the form.

Step 2

Confirm your name

First name and Last name come filled in from the invitation. If the owner left them blank, the boxes show First name and Last name in grey. Check the spelling and correct it if needed. Both are optional; a box you leave empty is not sent, so the name already on file stays.

You do not sign in or sign up first; this page does both. Below the button, After you submit explains later sign-ins: use Anna Books login, the email address shown, and either your existing password, which will not change, or the temporary password from your invitation email.

Step 3

Click ACCEPT INVITATION

Click ACCEPT INVITATION; it reads Saving… while AnnaBooks works. If the email has no AnnaBooks account, accepting creates one; an existing account keeps its password. A screen then shows You're all set!, Invitation accepted, and Opening your Anna Books dashboard…, and you are signed in.

If something goes wrong, a red message appears above the form and nothing changes; try again, or ask the owner for a new invitation. Already accepted? Go to login, under the button, takes you to the sign-in page instead. Accepting shares the owner's books with you; it does not copy them.

For later sign-ins, use the temporary password from the invitation email, or your existing password if you already had an account.

Step 4

Open the shared company

You land on your companies page, titled Companies you can access. Each shared card shows the company name, a line such as Invited by Maya Chen, and the company's type, industry, location, email, and phone. A card you own yourself carries an Owner badge and stays separate from the shared one.

Click the card, which shows Open at the top, to enter the company. Confirm the name before you post, because what you save changes the owner's books. Back on this page later, the banner Now using: names the company you are working in. If several owners invited you, Select an account owner comes first; click an owner card marked Invited you to see its companies.

More detail on this screen

Already accepted? Go to login, and the Sign in link at the foot of the page, both return you to the client sign-in page. The invitation email suggests changing a temporary password after your first sign-in, under Settings › User Management › Change password. If more than one owner invited you, Select an account owner lists each owner with Invited you and the number of companies you can access; Back returns to that list. Accept invitation does not open the vendor portal; vendors use Vendor on the start page.

2.6 Profile, password, and session

Three screens under Settings › User Management belong to you, not to the company: Update profile, Change password, and Session Alert. They never change customers, vendors, or the company's books.

  1. 1Go toSettings › User ManagementThree personal screens
  2. 2ClickSave changesUpdate profile; email locked
  3. 3ClickUpdate passwordCurrent + new, 8+ chars
  4. 4ClickSaveSession Alert lead time
  5. 5DoKeep workingNo sign-out after password change
Step 1

Update your own profile

Update profile has three parts. Profile picture is optional: Upload picture takes a JPG, PNG, GIF, or WebP under 5 MB and opens a crop window so you can adjust it; Change picture and Remove appear once a picture is set. Personal details has First name and Last name, both required, and Email, which is locked and marked Email cannot be changed.

Company details has Company name and Company website, both required; the placeholder https://example.com shows the format. These fields belong to your account from sign-up, not to the books you open. Save changes stays greyed out until the required boxes are filled and something has changed. Discard changes puts back the saved values.

Step 2

Change the sign-in password

Change password says it updates the password for your AnnaBooks sign-in and that you must enter your current password first. Under New credentials are three required boxes: Current password; New password, with the hint At least 8 characters.; and Confirm new password, which must match New password exactly.

Click Update password. It stays greyed out until all three boxes are valid and reads Saving… while it works. On success a confirmation appears and the boxes clear. A wrong current password shows a red message above the form and keeps the old password. This changes your sign-in password only, not the company encryption password.

Step 3

Set the session warning

Session Alert opens Session expiry alert. Under Top bar timer, Show session timer in top bar is ticked by default; unticking it hides the countdown at once, with no Save needed. Under Alert lead time, Quick preset sets how long before your session ends the top-bar timer turns to warning colors, from 1 minute before expiry to 1 hour before expiry, or Custom… for your own value.

Custom… adds Hours and Minutes boxes, up to 24 hours in total, and a line such as Saves as 90 minutes before expiry. Click Save, which stays greyed out until you choose a different value. Reset to default (1 min) sets the lead time back to one minute and turns the timer back on. The last minute always shows red.

This setting is saved in the current browser only.

Step 4

Keep working in the open company

After Update password you stay signed in and the company stays open; use the new password the next time you sign in. The top bar shows Time remaining with a countdown and a Log out button. When the countdown reaches your Session Alert lead time, it turns to warning colors and Session ending soon opens, saying how much time is left.

In that window, Continue closes it so you can finish and save your work, and Log out signs you out now. Forms you have not saved stay unsaved. When time runs out, Session expired appears with Sign in again, which takes you back to the sign-in page.

More detail on this screen

Invited users can use all three screens for their own account. The profile card at the bottom of the sidebar shows your name and the open company; its menu has Personal Settings, which opens Users, and Logout. On Update profile, clicking the picture opens the crop window again. Both Session Alert settings are stored in this browser only. The company encryption password is changed only in Company Preferences › Company Details › Data encryption.
