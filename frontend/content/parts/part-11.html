11.1 Fields on a customer, a consultant, and a contract

New Customer and New Consultant hold the identity and rates that later invoices and bills use. Saving them stores the details, and no amount posts until an invoice, bill, or payment is saved.

  1. 1Go toCustomer › New CustomerTabbed form; Next between
  2. 2DoFill Customer ID, NamePayment Method, Terms, Invoice Frequency
  3. 3ClickSave and CloseOn the Documents tab
  4. 4Go toConsultant › New ConsultantSingle Service Agreement form
  5. 5DoSet sell and buySales Price, Purchase Price, Pay Rate
  6. 6DoBenefits and contract fieldsAdd Benefit; Start/End Date, Auto Renew
  7. 7ClickSave and CloseStores rates; nothing posts
Step 1

Fill the customer identity

Customer › New Customer opens on Basic Information. Customer ID and Customer Name carry a red star, and a value already in use shows This Customer ID already exists in this company or This Customer Name already exists in this company. Active is ticked and Customer Since is today's date. Opening Balance (USD), Balance as of, Fax, Web page address, and Notes are optional, and Balance (USD) is read-only. Next stays grey while the tab has an error.

Payment & Settings requires Payment Method (Check for Harbor Clinic), Payment Terms (Net 15), and Invoice Frequency (Weekly). Sales Person, Customer Group, Credit Limit(INR), Bank Name, Bank Account Number, and Bank Branch are optional. Contact Details and Address Information fill with Add Contact and Add Address. Save and New and Save and Close appear only on the last tab, Documents.

Opening Balance (USD) and Balance as of are stored on the customer record. Saving the customer posts no invoice.

Step 2

Set sell and buy on the consultant

Consultant › New Consultant › Single Service Agreement opens the form. On Service Information, Priya Shah is Fulltime - Client, so I sell this service is ticked and Sell Service Configuration shows. Sales Price (USD) is the bill rate, $85.00, and Income Account is where it posts; both are required. Standard Cost (USD) is required too and starts equal to Sales Price. Pay Rate, $55.00, is what you pay the consultant at payroll time and takes an amount or a percent such as 80%; ticking Fixed Salary swaps it for Fixed Salary (USD).

Description on Sales Transaction is optional. I buy this service appears only for C2C consultants such as Ravi Menon, with both boxes ticked and locked. Buy Service Configuration then requires Purchase Price (USD), $72.00, Expense Account, and Preferred Vendor, Bright Path IT LLC, plus optional Vendor Service No and Description on Purchase Transaction. Click Next.

Step 3

Fill the contract dates

Contract Information is the last tab for every type except Fulltime - Company. A contract is optional, but once you start one, Contract Number and Start Date are required: CT-201 from 01/05/2026 for Priya. Contract Type follows the consultant type and is greyed out. Status starts at Draft and also offers Active, Expired, Terminated, and Cancelled. End Date must be after Start Date, Renewal Date on or after it, and Signed Date between the two. Contract Title and Signed By are free text.

Sales Price (USD), Pay Rate, and Payment Terms record the agreed terms. Purchase Price (USD) appears on 1099 and C2C contracts, and Fixed Salary (USD) replaces Pay Rate for fixed-salary staff. Tick Auto Renew for a contract that should continue. Termination Clause, Notes, and Attach a file are optional. Save and Close stores the consultant and the contract; the invoice is what posts income.

Step 4

Add a benefit for full-time staff

The Benefits tab appears for Fulltime - Client and Fulltime - Company consultants, such as Noah Kim and Maya Iyer. Click Add Benefit for a new card. Pick the required Category first (Health), then the required Benefit Plan (Aetna PPO Gold). Status offers Active, Pending, Inactive, and Terminated. Frequency offers Weekly, Biweekly, Semimonthly, Monthly, Per Pay Period, and Annually. Effective Date and End Date limit when the benefit applies.

Employee Contribution Type and Employer Contribution Type each take Amount ($) or Percent (%); Noah pays 60.00 and Northline pays 0.00. Benefit Description holds coverage notes, and Remove appears once there are two cards. The plan itself is made in Add Benefit Plan, which requires Category, Benefit Type, Plan Name, and Provider and also has Status.

More detail on this screen

The customer form also holds Customer Since, Sales Person, Customer Group, Credit Limit(INR), Bank Name, Bank Account Number, and Bank Branch, and Manage Custom Fields on Payment & Settings adds fields of your own. Add Contact asks for Contact Name, Title, Business Phone, and Email; if there is only one contact it must be the Primary Contact. Add Address asks for Address Type (Billing or Shipping), Address Line 1, Address Line 2, Country, State, City, and Postal Code. The consultant's Basic Information tab requires Consultant ID, Consultant Name, Email, Phone, and Consultant Type (Fulltime - Client, Fulltime - Company, 1099, or C2C), and also has Timesheet Frequency, Customer, Invoicing Frequency, Payment Terms, Commission item, and Active; Invoicing Frequency and Payment Terms can show Automatically selected from customer. Fulltime - Company consultants have no Service Information or Contract Information tab and enter Pay Rate or Fixed Salary (USD) on Basic Information. Save and Close appears on the last tab. To add customers to an existing consultant, use Enable Multiple Customer Service Agreements on the Consultants list; the consultant must already have a customer. On the March payroll Noah and Maya each had $60.00 deducted for Aetna PPO Gold, so PR-12's net pay was $19,053.00.

11.2 Fields on an invoice, a credit, a cash sale, and a payment

Create Invoice and Customer Invoice share the invoice fields, and Save and Close posts the invoice with its journal. Receive Payment then records the cash against the open invoices.

  1. 1Go toCustomer › Create Invoice
  2. 2DoFill Invoice No, CustomerContact, Payment Terms, Due Date
  3. 3DoAdd linesAdd Timesheet or Add New Item
  4. 4DecideRequired fields and lines?YesSave and CloseNoErrors list missing
  5. 5ResultInvoice and journal postJournal auto-created after save
  6. 6Go toCustomer › Receive PaymentReceived From, Amount Received
  7. 7ClickSave and ClosePosts payment journal
Step 1

Enter the invoice header

Customer › Create Invoice starts with Date, Invoice No, and Order Number. Invoice No is filled in for you and read-only; Order Number is optional (CS-PO-2207). Customer, Payment Terms, and Contact carry a red star. Picking Cedar Schools loads Net 15, the Bill To address, and Ruth Alvarez as Contact. Due Date is worked out from Date and Payment Terms and cannot be typed. Phone Number and Email are optional.

For a consultant company the lines are Itemize by Consultant Hours: Consultant, Description, Timesheet, Hours, Rate (USD), and Total (USD). Add Consultant adds a row and Remove deletes one; Aisha Rahman's timesheet brings 36 hours at $80.00. Other industries get an Items table with Add New Item. The Customer Invoice screen (Customer › New Invoice) labels the field Customer Name and fills lines with Add Timesheet or Select All Timesheets.

Step 2

Add tax, bonus, or discount, then save

The gear button titled Settings opens Invoice Settings with Enable Sales Tax entries, Enable Bonus entries, Enable Discount entries, and Create draft bill when creating invoice. With any of the first three on, each consultant line gets Add Sales Tax, Add Bonus, or Add Discount. Each added row has a Description and an Amount, which takes a percent or a sum such as 5% or 500. The totals then show Bonus Amount, Discount Amount, and Tax Amount lines above Total (USD). Comments is optional.

Save and Close, Save and New, and Save and Email all save and post the invoice, and its journal entry is created right after the save; Save and Email first asks which email service to use. If a required field or line is missing, an error lists it. Cancel with unsaved changes asks Yes, Cancel or Continue Editing.

The invoice screens have no Save as draft or Make it recurring button.

Step 3

Receive the payment

Customer › Receive Payment shows Date and No, which is filled in and read-only (RP-303). Received From, Payment Method, Amount Received (USD), and Deposit In are required. Picking Check adds Cheque/Ref No for the check number, 5521. Deposit In lists bank, cash, and Other current Asset accounts; Checking Account (1010) puts the money in the bank at once. Customer Balance (USD) is read-only and shows what Cedar Schools owes, $5,680.00. Comments is optional.

Due for payment lists open invoices with Invoice, Due, Inv Amt, Due (the unpaid amount), Write Off, Credits, Payment, and Status; Bonus and Discount columns appear when turned on. Tick INV-1052 and INV-1053 and type 2,800.00 and 200.00. Total Payment (USD) cannot exceed Amount Received (USD), and Save and New and Save and Close stay grey until the payment is valid. Saving posts the payment journal.

Step 4

Use a refund only when cash goes back

Customer › Customer Refund has Date and No, then four required fields: Pay To (Maple Dental), Pay From (the bank the money leaves), Amount(USD), and Payment Method. Customer Balance(USD) and Bank Balance(USD) are read-only. The amount cannot be more than the bank balance; if it is, the screen shows Amount cannot exceed Bank Balance. Address is filled from the customer and cannot be edited, and the comments box is optional.

Picking Cheque adds TO BE PRINTED and Cheque No. Type 1001 for a check written now, or tick TO BE PRINTED and leave the number for printing. Save and Close stores RF-0001 for $50.00. It lowers the bank balance only: no journal entry posts and the customer balance does not change.

More detail on this screen

Select All Timesheets pulls every listed timesheet onto the invoice. On the Customer Invoice screen, Email and Phone number are read-only, and the line area reads Select a Customer until you pick one. Receive Payment shows Check in Payment Method, while Customer Refund shows the same method as Cheque. If you leave a half-finished invoice, the screen asks Yes, Cancel or Continue Editing. New Cash Sale and New Credit Memo show Make it recurring and Save as draft, but both buttons only show a 'not implemented yet' message. Receive Payment can add Bonus and Discount lines for each invoice through its Invoice Settings. Harbor's $7,000.00 receipt cleared INV-1048 and INV-1049, and Cedar's open $2,680.00 still waits for a receipt.

11.3 Fields on a vendor, a bill, a check, and an expense

Create Bill and Enter Bill are two screens with the same header and tabs, and Save and Close posts what you owe with its journal. Pay Bill then spends the bank, and Print Check prints only checks that Write Check has confirmed.

  1. 1Go toVendor › Create BillCreate Bill screen
  2. 2DoFill Bill No, Vendor NameBill Date, Payment Terms, Due Date
  3. 3DoItemize the billConsultant Hours or Product/Service
  4. 4ClickSave and CloseSaves bill, posts journal
  5. 5Go toVendor › Pay BillPay From, Payment Method
  6. 6ClickSave and CloseTO BE PRINTED for checks
  7. 7Go toWrite Check › Print CheckPrinter(s), Check Style, Print Check
Step 1

Enter the bill header and lines

Vendor › Create Bill starts with Bill Date and Bill No, then Vendor Name, the only field with a red star, plus Contact Name, Phone, Payment Terms, and Due Date. Bill Date must be filled too; saving without it lists Bill Date is required. Picking Deskline Supplies loads its Net 30 terms, so DS-8891 dated 03/06/2026 is due 04/05/2026.

Three tabs set how lines are entered. Itemize by Account has Account, Description, Amount(USD), and Total, with Add Account Row; DS-8891 is one line, Office Supplies Expense for $186.40. Itemize by Consultant Hours, for consultant industries, has Consultant, Description, Timesheet, Hours, Hourly Rate (USD), and Total (USD), with Add Consultant. Itemize by Product/Service has Item Name, Description, Quantity, Unit Price, and Total, with Add New Item.

Step 2

Save the bill

DS-8900 uses Itemize by Product/Service: pick Laptop sleeve, type Quantity 10, and Unit Price, which comes from the item and cannot be typed, gives a Total of $120.00. Save and Close or Save and New saves the bill and then posts its journal entry, and a progress window shows each step until you click OK. Cancel leaves without saving.

Draft bills such as DS-9012 for $89.00 come from Drafts & Captured and stay out of Accounts Payable and aging until you open them here and save. Vendor › Bills › Vendor Bill opens Enter Bill, which has the same header and tabs, shows Please Select a Vendor until you pick one, and has a Write comments for this Enter Bill. box.

Neither bill screen has Save as draft or Make it recurring. Add Account Row is on Create Bill only; Enter Bill has none.

Step 3

Pay from the bank

Vendor › Pay Bill shows Date and No, then Vendor Name, Pay From, and Payment Method, all required, plus optional Contact Name and Phone. Bank Balance (USD) is read-only and shows the Pay From account's balance. Choosing Check adds TO BE PRINTED and Check No: type 2204 for a check written now, or tick TO BE PRINTED to leave the number for printing. Filter by bill due on or before hides bills due later.

Bills due lists Due Date, Bill No, Status, Original Amount(USD), Amount Due(USD), Credits(USD), and Payments(USD). Tick DS-8891 and DS-8900; their payments total $306.40. The arrow on a bill opens its lines with Line payment boxes. Unused Credits (USD) and Total (USD) sit beside the buttons. Save and Close posts the payment, and Accounts Payable and Checking each drop by $306.40.

Step 4

Write the check, then print it

Write Check (Banking › Write Check) has Vendor and a read-only Bank Account found from the pay bill. Vendor Pay Bills lists Select, Pay Bill #, Date, Payment Method, Contact, Total Amount, Write Status, Print Status, and Bills Paid; tick 2204 for $306.40. Check Information shows Check Date, a read-only Check Number, Payment Amount, and Amount in Words, and an optional Memo. Confirm Check confirms the check, and Save Draft Check keeps it unconfirmed.

Print Check then lists confirmed checks that are not printed yet under Select Checks to Print, with Select All and Clear All. Under Print Options pick Printer(s), or click Refresh, and Check Style, US Business Pre-printed Check (Stubs Only), which prints only the stubs. Click Print Check. The books already hold the payment from Pay Bill.

A payment marked TO BE PRINTED does not appear in Print Check until Write Check is confirmed.

More detail on this screen

Create Bill appears under Vendor › Bills only when bill tracking is on for the company; Vendor Bill, which opens Enter Bill, is always there. Write Check opens from Home and from the vendor checks list in Transactions Center, and Save Draft Check keeps a check unconfirmed. Print Check also has Remittance Advice Stub Fields toggles with Select All, Deselect All, and Reset Defaults, and a Check Layout Preview. After printing, Print Job Completed asks whether the print finished and marks the checks as printed. The cash expense, card expense, and vendor credit screens show Make it recurring and Save as draft, but both buttons only show a 'not implemented yet' message. Deskline's bills were $306.40 on check 2204. City Parking's CP-310 for $240.00 was paid in full with check 2205.

New Bank Account adds the register and links it to a company bank account in the chart. A loan payment posts its own journal once the split matches the amount.

  2. 2DoAdd Credit Card or LoanLast 4 Digits, APR, Term
  3. 3Go toRecord Loan PaymentPay From Account, Amount (USD)
  4. 4DoFill from estimatePrincipal, interest, Escrow, Late Fee
  5. 5DecideBreakdown equals amount?YesSave and CloseNoError; fix split
  6. 6ResultLoan payment journal posts
  7. 7DoLink statement lineLink …, then Mark as Recorded
Step 1

Name the bank and the opening balance

Banking › New Bank Account has two panels. Under Bank Information, Bank Setup is required: Select Existing Bank shows Select Bank, and Create New Bank shows New Bank Name, here Harbor Community Bank. Under Bank Account Details, Account Type reads Bank, and Bank Account Type (Checking Account or Savings Account) and Company Account (Bank Type) are required. Picking 1010 - Checking Account fills and locks Account Number and Account Name, both required.

Bank Account Number is optional; enter the real number ending 4821. Opening Balance (USD) 10,000.00 and As of 03/01/2026 are stored and Current Balance (USD) copies them, but no journal entry is made; JE-1 records the $10,000.00. Comments is optional, Active stays ticked, and Is Sub-Account? adds a required Parent Account. Save and Close stores the account.

Step 2

Store the card or the loan terms

Banking › Credit Cards › Add Credit Card has Card Information and Billing Address tabs. Card Name / Nickname, Cardholder Name, Issuer / Bank, and Last 4 Digits are required; Last 4 Digits must be exactly 4 numbers and Expiration (MM/YY) a valid month. Card Category, Card Network, Company Account (Credit Card Type), Credit Limit (USD), Current Balance (USD), APR (%), Statement Closing Day, Payment Due Day, Minimum Payment Due (USD), and Notes are optional. Next: Billing Address leads to the address; then click Save Credit Card.

Banking › Loans › Add Loan first asks How do you want to track payments?: Amortization schedule or Track payments. Its steps are Loan Details (required Loan Name / Nickname, Lender / Financial Institution, Loan Type), Accounts (Loan Liability Account, Default Interest Expense Account), Terms (required Original Loan Amount, Current Outstanding Balance, Interest Rate (APR %), Term (Months), Start Date), Fees, Schedule, and Review.

Step 3

Split the loan payment

Banking › Loans › Record Loan Payment runs Loan & Payment, Breakdown, and Review. The first step requires Pay To (Loan), Pay From Account, Payment Date, and Amount (USD); No and Payment Method are optional. For the First Civic term loan on 03/20/2026, pay $420.00 from Checking Account by ACH / EFT. Click Next.

Breakdown holds Principal (USD) and Interest (USD), both required here, plus optional Escrow (USD), Processing / Service Fee, and Late Fee. Fill from estimate, shown for amortization loans, fills principal $380.00 and interest $40.00; change one and the other adjusts to the total. Interest Expense Account is required, and Escrow Account and Fees & Charges Expense Account take the extra amounts. On Review, Save and Close posts the payment and the loan balance drops by the principal.

Save and New and Save and Close stay grey until every part adds up to Amount (USD). Save as draft stores the payment as a draft instead.

Step 4

Link a statement line to a saved document

Recording a line from a saved bank statement opens Record Transaction. A badge names the kind of line, such as Customer deposit, Vendor payment, Fund transfer, Credit card payment, or Payroll payment, above its Date, Amount, and Party. Under Link to existing record choose Link Vendor Payment, Link Credit Card Payment, Link Fund Transfer, Link Receive Payment, Link Bank Deposit, Link Loan Payment, Organization Loan, or Link Journal Entry. Create Payroll Journal records a payroll line.

For Cedar's $3,000.00 deposit on 03/19/2026, click Link Receive Payment. Link Payment asks you to Select payment to link; choose RP-303. If the amounts differ, a Can't link message shows both amounts. Mark as Recorded points the line at that saved record and posts nothing new; Record New Payment creates a new record from the line instead.

More detail on this screen

New Bank Account can also take Select Bank or a New Bank Name, a Parent Account, Account Type, Bank Account Type, and Current Balance (USD). Add Credit Card's Billing Address tab has Street Address, Apt / Suite, Country, State, City, and Postal Code, and Back: Card Information returns to the first tab. Add Loan also takes Original Loan Amount, Current Outstanding Balance, Start Date, fees, and payment schedule fields across its Next steps. Track payments makes Interest Rate (APR %), Term (Months), and Start Date optional on Add Loan. Add Credit Card shows a message instead of saving when the required Card Information fields are empty. On Record Loan Payment, Make it recurring only says that recurring loan payments will come in a future release. A transfer between two of your accounts is still your cash.

Step 1

Set the item accounts and prices

Inventory › New Inventory Item has two tabs. Basic Information requires Inventory Name (Laptop sleeve) and has Category, Subitem of, Commission item, and Active; ticking Subitem of adds a required Parent Item. In the current version a sale lowers On Hand Quantity but posts no cost of goods.

Step 2

Build only what is on hand

Inventory › Build Assembly has Search and Add New Inventory Assembly at the top. Build Assembly Details requires Assembly Item (Client welcome kit); Quantity On Hand and Build Point are read-only, Date defaults to today, and Ref No. to 1. 11.6 What each report and activity screen offers

Report screens let you set the basis and the dates, update, and export to CSV or print. Opening a line shows the transaction history behind that total.

  1. 1Go toReports › Profit And Loss
  2. 2DoSet Report BasisAccrual or Cash; From, To
  3. 3DoClick UpdateIncome, Expenses sections refresh
  4. 4DoOpen a lineView transaction history
  5. 5DoExport to CSV or Print
  6. 6Go toPay Slip DetailEmployee, Date Range, Run report
  7. 7Go toUsers Activity LogFrom date, End date, Module, Actor
Step 1

Set the basis and the dates

Reports › Profit And Loss has Report Basis, Accrual or Cash, and opens on Accrual. Date Range opens on All and also offers This week, This month, Last week, Last month, This financial year, Last financial year, This financial quarter, Last financial quarter, Financial year to date, and Custom. Pick Custom, set From 03/01/2026 and To 03/31/2026, and click Update.

Export to CSV and Print give you a copy, and Help explains the report. The Summary and Detail switch sets how much is listed. Show Zero Balances adds accounts with no activity, and Compare to Last Year adds columns for the previous period and the year to date.

Step 2

Read the sections, then open a line

The Income section holds Income and Other Income, and the Expenses section holds Cost of Goods Sold, Expense, Other Expense, and Depreciation & Amortization. Each group ends in its own total, such as Total Income or Total Expense, and Total Expenses closes the section. The summary at the top takes Other income and Other expense into account and ends at Net income: $4,027.60 for March.

The clock button next to an amount, View transaction history, opens the entries behind it with Date, Entry Number, Description, Debit, Credit, Balance Change, and Running Balance. Use Export to CSV or Print when you need a copy. Only Income and Expense accounts appear; Balance Sheet accounts never do.

Step 3

Read pay from a completed run

Payroll Reports › Pay Slip Detail filters by Employee, which starts at All Employees, and Date Range; From and To can be typed only when Date Range is Custom. Click Run report. Each row shows Employee, Earnings, Withholdings, Employer-Responsible Tax, and Net Value. PR-12 recorded no withholding, so Noah Kim shows $1,800.00 earned and $1,740.00 net after his $60.00 benefit.

Click a row to open its breakdown: Earnings, Withholdings when there are any, Employee Deductions (not tax withholdings, taken from net pay), Employer-Responsible Tax when there is any (a company cost, not taken from net pay), and Net Value. Export to CSV and Print copy the list. Net Value for PR-12 totals $19,053.00 after $165.00 of reimbursements and $120.00 of deductions, and staged run SP-14 is not shown.

Step 4

Filter the activity log

Users Activity Log filters by Date range (Today, This week, This month, or Custom; it opens on Today), From date, End date, User type (All users, Client (owner), or Invited users), Module (All modules or one area, such as Company or Customer), and Actor (All actors or one person). The table lists Logged At, Actor, Company, Action, Module, Record, Details, and Status.

Export to CSV saves a copy of the list. Ticking rows swaps that button for Delete Logs with the count, such as Delete Logs (2), which asks Delete activity logs? before removing them. Clear unticks the selection.

The log email and Send test email are on the Users settings page, under Log email schedule.

More detail on this screen

Profit And Loss can also switch between Summary and Detail, Show Zero Balances, and Compare to Last Year. Its totals are Total Income, Total Other Income, Total Cost of Goods Sold, Total Expense, Total Other Expense, and Total Depreciation & Amortization. Pay Slip Detail can also export to CSV or print. Payroll reports read completed runs only. When Pay Slip Detail finds nothing it shows No payroll payments found for this period. On Users Activity Log, Clear unticks the selected rows; it does not reset the filters.
