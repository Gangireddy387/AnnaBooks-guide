6. Banking, cards, and loans

6.1 Bank accounts and the register

Banking › Bank Accounts lists your banks and their accounts. Banking › New Bank Account adds an account and links it to a Bank-type account in your chart of accounts.

  1. 1Go toBanking › New Bank Account
  2. 2DoSelect BankExisting or new bank
  3. 3DoBank Account TypeChecking or Savings
  4. 4DoLink Company AccountBank-type chart account
  5. 5ClickSave and CloseOpening balance; no journal
  6. 6Go toBanking › Bank Accounts
  7. 7DoView TransactionsOr Transaction History icon
Step 1

Open New Bank Account

Banking › New Bank Account has two panels. Under Bank Information, Bank Setup is required: Select Existing Bank, or Create New Bank and type the New Bank Name, here Harbor Community Bank. Under Bank Account Details, pick the required Bank Account Type, Checking Account, and the required Company Account (Bank Type), 1010 - Checking Account. Account Number and Account Name then fill in from that chart account and lock.

Bank Account Number is optional; enter the real number ending 4821. Opening Balance (USD) 10,000.00 with As of 03/01/2026 is stored on the account, and Current Balance (USD) copies it, but no journal entry is made: Northline's books get the $10,000.00 from journal entry JE-1. Leave Active ticked and click Save and Close to land on Bank Accounts.

Step 2

Add savings the same way

Click Save and New instead of Save and Close, or open Banking › New Bank Account again. Save and New keeps the bank you last used, so Bank Setup shows Select Existing Bank and Select Bank shows Harbor Community Bank. Choose Bank Account Type Savings Account and Company Account (Bank Type) 1011 - Savings Account. 1010 is no longer listed, because each chart account can link to only one bank account.

Enter the Bank Account Number ending 4839 and leave Opening Balance (USD) at 0.00, since savings starts empty. Click Save and Close. Money you later move between checking and savings is a transfer, not income; savings ends March at $2,000.00 after transfer TR-3.

Step 3

Open the account's transactions

Banking › Bank Accounts lists your banks with Bank Name, Bank Code, Routing Number, Phone, Email, and Status. Click the Harbor Community Bank row to expand it. Its Bank Accounts table shows Account Name, Account Number, Account Type, Bank Account Number, Bank Account Type, and Current Balance, then a Transactions button, a History button, and icons for Edit Account, Upload, and View Saved Statements.

Click Transactions on the Checking Account row. Bank Transactions shows Opening Balance and Current Balance, Add Single Transaction and Upload CSV File, AnnaPay and Plaid connection tabs, and an Account Statement list you can filter by Date Range, Transaction Type, Amount Range, and Search. History shows the same rows with a Running Balance column. Back to Accounts returns you here.

These lists show bank rows, not every entry you post. Read your book balance on the Balance Sheet.

Step 4

Read the ending balance

Reports › Company And Finance › Balance Sheet shows your book balances. Choose a Report Basis, set Date Range to Custom, type From and To (03/01/2026 to 03/31/2026), and click Apply. The Assets side lists each account with Account, Account #, and Balance: Checking Account 1010 at $16,030.60 and Savings Account 1011 at $2,000.00 under Current Assets. See 8.6 for the full report.

Bank Accounts shows $16,480.60 for checking because its Current Balance is a stored bank figure: the $500.00 prepayment and the $50.00 refund changed it without a journal entry. If a book balance looks wrong, look for a draft or an entry posted to another account. The history button on each row opens that account's entries.

More detail on this screen

Each chart account can link to only one bank account; accounts already linked are hidden from the list. Is Sub-Account? creates an account under a Parent Account and then requires an Opening Balance; the bank is then set from the parent. Until the required fields are filled, a Form Incomplete message shows and Save and New and Save and Close stay greyed out. Bank Accounts can Export to CSV and switch the Current View between Active and Inactive. The pencil on a bank row opens Edit Bank Details (Bank Name, Bank Code, Routing Number, Phone, Email, Website, Address, Active, then Update Bank); the pencil on an account row opens Edit Bank Account Details with Link to Chart of Accounts and Update Account. The Upload and View Saved Statements icons on the row handle bank statements (see 6.4).

6.2 Make deposit

Make Deposit moves customer payments from Undeposited Funds into a bank account. One deposit can hold several payments so it matches the lump sum on your bank statement.

  1. 1DoReceive customer paymentsHeld in Undeposited Funds
  2. 2Go toBanking › Make Deposit
  3. 3DoClick Add on paymentsUndeposited Customer Payments list
  4. 4DoDeposit To, Deposit Date
  5. 5ClickSave and Close'Save as draft' does not post
  6. 6ResultBank ↑ · Undeposited ↓See Deposit Detail report
Step 1

Receive the payments first

Customer › Receive Payment records the money before it goes to the bank. Pick Harbor Clinic in Received From, set Date 03/18/2026, and check that No shows RP-302. Choose Payment Method Check and type 7,000.00 in Amount Received (USD). In Deposit In, choose Undeposited Funds so the payment waits to be grouped into a deposit. Customer Balance (USD) shows what Harbor still owes.

The invoice table lists INV-1048 for $3,400.00 and INV-1049 for $3,600.00 with the Payment applied to each. Click Save and Close. Income was already recorded on the invoices, so this payment only lowers Accounts Receivable and raises Undeposited Funds; checking does not change yet.

Step 2

Open Make Deposit

Banking › Make Deposit opens with two notes on how posting works, then Undeposited Customer Payments with Date, Payment No, Customer, and Amount. That list appears only while payments are waiting. Click Add on RP-302: a Deposit Items row fills in Payee Harbor Clinic, Deposit From Undeposited Funds, Description Payment RP-302, and Total 7,000.00. Clicking Add twice only shows a message that the payment is already on the deposit.

Under Deposit Information, Deposit To is required: pick Harbor Community Bank | 1010 (Checking). Payment Method is optional (Cheque here), Deposit Date is 03/18/2026, and No shows the next number, D-78. Comments is optional. Add New Payee adds a blank row and the trash icon removes one.

Step 3

Save the deposit

Check the Deposit Items table: every row needs a Payee, a Deposit From account, and a Total above zero. Cash Sale shows a No prior invoice box only when Deposit From is an Income account, and that box must be ticked to save. Deposit Summary shows Total (USD) $7,000.00. Until Deposit To and every row are complete, a Form Incomplete message shows and Save and New and Save and Close stay greyed out.

Click Save and Close. Checking rises by $7,000.00 and Undeposited Funds falls by the same amount; income does not change. Save and New posts and clears the form for the next slip. Save as draft keeps the deposit without posting, and Make it recurring only says recurring deposits are not available yet.

Step 4

Match the statement

Reports › Banking › Deposit Detail lists each deposit. Choose a Report Basis, pick a Date Range (or Custom and type From and To), and click Update. Each line shows Type, Number, Date, Name, Account, Debit, and Credit, so you can find D-78 and the $7,000.00 it moved into Checking Account. Export to CSV and Print copy the same rows.

Harbor Community Bank's statement shows one DEPOSIT line for $7,000.00 on 03/18/2026, and it should equal D-78. When you upload that statement (see 6.4), link the line with Link Bank Deposit. If a deposit went to the wrong account, one account is short and the other is long.

More detail on this screen

Payments appear in the list only if they were deposited to an Other current Asset account named Undeposited Funds; create that account if the list is empty. Accounts receivable can never be used as a deposit source. A deposit row can also bring in owner money from an Equity account, or a direct cash sale from an income account with No prior invoice ticked under Cash Sale. Deposit To lists only active bank accounts and ends with + Add a New Account, which opens New Bank Account and brings you back. Payment Method offers Cash, Credit Card, Direct Deposit, Master card, Online Banking, Standing Order, Switch/Maestro, and Cheque. Posting updates your books; the bank register line is created when you import and link the matching statement line. Do not fix a misplaced deposit with a journal entry that adds income.

6.3 Transfer funds

Banking › Transfer Fund moves money between two of your bank accounts. It is not income, an expense, a customer receipt, or a vendor payment.

  1. 1Go toBanking › Transfer Fund
  2. 2DoTransfer From, Transfer To
  3. 3DoAmount (USD), Date
  4. 4ClickSave and Close'Save as draft' does not post
  5. 5ResultFrom ↓ · To ↑No income or expense
  6. 6DoCheck both registersBank Accounts › View Transactions
Step 1

Open Transfer Fund

Banking › Transfer Fund has three panels. Transfer Details holds Date and No; Account Information holds the required Transfer From, Transfer To, and Amount (USD); Additional Information holds Comments. Both account lists show your active bank accounts as bank name, account number, and type. Pick Harbor Community Bank | 1010 - Checking in Transfer From and Harbor Community Bank | 1011 - Savings in Transfer To.

The account chosen in one list is hidden from the other, so money cannot move to the same account. + Create New Account at the bottom of each list opens New Bank Account. Both accounts must be linked to the chart of accounts; if one is not, a red message asks you to link it before posting.

Step 2

Enter the amount and the date

Type 2000 in Amount (USD) and set Date to 03/12/2026. No shows the next transfer number, TR-3; you can change it. Comments is optional; Northline writes Reserve for April payroll so the reason is on record.

Transfer Summary shows Available in Transfer From, which is the linked chart account balance ($28,700.00 for checking that day), and Total (USD) $2,000.00. If the amount is more than the available balance, a red message says it cannot exceed it and the save buttons stay off. Total cash does not change; only the account holding it changes.

Step 3

Save the transfer

Until Transfer From, Transfer To, and an Amount (USD) above zero are filled, a Form Incomplete message shows and Save and New and Save and Close stay greyed out. Click Save and Close to post TR-3: Savings Account rises $2,000.00 and Checking Account falls $2,000.00, with no income or expense. A message confirms the save and you return to the page you came from.

Save and New posts the transfer and clears the form for another one. Save as draft keeps it without posting; you can post it later from Transfer Funds. Make it recurring only says recurring transfers are not available yet. Use the loan screens, not a transfer, for loan draws and payments.

Step 4

Check both accounts

Company › Transactions Center › Banking › Transfer Funds lists every transfer with Transaction Date, Number, Transfer To, Transfer From, Amount, and Status (Posted, Draft, or Voided). Filter with Current View, Date, From, and To, then click Apply or Reset. Cards above the list show Total records, Posted, Drafts, and Total amount; Export CSV, Refresh, and Transfer Fund sit at the top.

A draft row has Post; any row not yet voided has Void, which asks you to confirm and then reverses the bank and chart of accounts entries. Both accounts show TR-3 in the General Ledger on 03/12/2026. The line on each account's Bank Transactions list appears only after you import the statement and link it with Link Fund Transfer.

More detail on this screen

Transactions Center shows each transfer as Posted, Draft, or Voided, and Current View can show All, Open (Posted), Drafts, or Voided. Post on a draft asks you to confirm, then moves the money. Voiding a draft transfer deletes it. Transfer Fund skips inactive bank accounts and sub-accounts. Do not edit only one side of a transfer; void it and enter it again. Each account's statement is matched separately in 6.4.

6.4 Reconciliation

AnnaBooks matches the books to a bank statement line by line: you upload the statement, then mark each line as recorded. There is no screen that asks for a statement ending balance and finishes at a $0 difference.

  1. 1Go toBanking › Bank Accounts
  2. 2DoUpload statementUpload icon on account row
  3. 3ClickImport TransactionsAfter Process Statement
  4. 4Not posted yetStatement importedLines not recorded yet
  5. 5DecideLine already in books?YesLink, Mark as RecordedNoCreate it, then link
  6. 6DoStatus shows processedEvery line recorded
Step 1

Upload the statement

In Banking › Bank Accounts, expand Harbor Community Bank and click the Upload icon on the Checking Account row. Upload Bank Statement opens below the table: Drop your bank statement here, for PDF and image files (PNG, JPG, JPEG). Click Choose file and pick the March statement; Remove clears a wrong file. Click Process Statement and a progress bar shows each reading step.

Then check Statement Information (Bank, Account Number, Account Type, Statement Period) and the Summary of instances and amounts, from Beginning Balance $10,000.00 down. Extracted Transactions (14) groups the lines in tabs; a Validation Mismatch warning flags a tab whose count or total differs from the Summary, and Add row fixes a missed line. Click Import 14 Transactions.

Step 2

Open the saved statement

Click View Saved Statements on the Checking Account row, or click the row itself. Saved Statements lists Bank, Statement Period, Transactions, Status, and Uploaded Date. Filter by Status (All Status, Imported, Processing, Processed) or Statement Period; Clear Filters resets them and Close hides the list. Click the March row to open Statement Details below it.

Statement Details shows Bank, Account Number, Statement Period, and Status, a Summary with Instances and Amount from Beginning Balance to Ending Balance ($16,480.60), and Transactions (14) in tabs such as Deposits and Additions, Checks Paid, and Electronic Withdrawals. A new statement starts as imported: no line is recorded yet, and importing posts nothing to your books.

Step 3

Mark each line as recorded

Click an unrecorded line, for example DEPOSIT $7,000.00 on 03/18/2026. Record Transaction shows its Date, Amount, and Party, then Link to existing record: Link Vendor Payment, Link Credit Card Payment, Link Fund Transfer, Link Receive Payment, Link Bank Deposit, Link Loan Payment, Organization Loan, and Link Journal Entry. Cancel and Create Payroll Journal sit at the bottom. Click Link Bank Deposit.

Link Payment opens. Under Select payment to link, choose D-78 and click Mark as Recorded. The amounts must match; if they differ, a message gives the difference and the button stays off. Record New Deposit creates the record from this line instead. Check 2204 uses Link Vendor Payment the same way.

If a line is not in your books yet, create it first (for example with Pay bill or Create bill on a vendor line), then link it.

Step 4

Finish when every line is recorded

A recorded line shows a badge such as Deposit Recorded, Payment Recorded, Transfer Recorded, or Journal Recorded; click it to view the linked record. The statement Status changes to processing while some lines are still open and to processed when every line is recorded. Use the Status filter on Saved Statements to find statements still processing.

Banking › Reconciliations History has Select Bank Account and columns Reconciliation Date, Reconciliation Period, Open Balance, and Closing Balance, but it does not list results yet: it shows No reconciliations to show for this account. Read progress on the statement Status instead.

Northline's March statement stays processing: line 5 (the $500.00 Lumen Labs prepayment) and line 13 (the $50.00 refund check 1001) have no journal entry to link.

More detail on this screen

Record Transaction can also link a credit card payment, fund transfer, receive payment, loan payment, organization loan, or journal entry, and Create Payroll Journal handles payroll lines. Each link screen has its own Record New button (Record New Payment, Record New Fund Transfer, Record New Pay Credit Card, Record New Loan Payment, or Create Journal Entry) for lines not yet in your books. Link Journal Entry lists only posted entries that are not tied to an invoice, bill, payment, deposit, transfer, payroll, or loan. On customer and vendor lines, Map links an unknown name to an existing customer or vendor. While uploading, Add category and Remove category manage extra tabs, and rows can be moved between tabs. Each bank account has its own statements, so savings is matched against the savings statement. Do not change an opening balance to make lines agree; find the missing entry instead.

6.6 Loans from a lender

Banking › Loans tracks money you owe a lender. Saving the loan posts the proceeds, and Record Loan Payment splits each payment into principal and interest.

  1. 1Go toLoans › Lenders › Add LenderThen Save Lender
  2. 2Go toBanking › Loans › Add Loan
  3. 3DoLiability and Pay FromNext through wizard steps
  4. 4ClickSave and Close
  5. 5ResultBank ↑ · Loan liability ↑Proceeds post on save
  6. 6Go toLoans › Record Loan PaymentSplit Principal and Interest
  7. 7ResultLoan ↓ · Interest ↑ · Bank ↓After Save and Close
Step 1

Add the lender and the loan

Banking › Loans › Lenders › Add Lender requires only Lender Name; Lender Code, Phone, Website, and Notes are optional, and Active Lender is ticked. Enter First Civic Bank and click Save Lender to land on Lender Details. Then open Banking › Loans › Add Loan. Under How do you want to track payments?, Amortization schedule builds a payment schedule from rate, term, and start date; Track payments records each payment without one.

Keep Amortization schedule. The wizard runs Loan Details, Accounts, Terms, Fees, Schedule, and Review. Loan Details requires Loan Name / Nickname (First Civic term loan), Lender / Financial Institution (First Civic Bank), and Loan Type (Term Loan). Loan / Account Number FC-55810 and Loan Purpose are optional, and Active Loan is ticked.

Step 2

Save to post the proceeds

On Accounts, pick Loan Liability Account 2200 - Bank Loan Payable (blank creates one) and Default Interest Expense Account 5100 - Interest Expense. Terms requires Original Loan Amount 10,000.00, Current Outstanding Balance 10,000.00, Interest Rate (APR %) 4.80, Term (Months) 24, and Start Date 03/03/2026. Fees are all optional; First Civic has none. On Schedule, choose Payment Frequency Monthly, Payment Amount (USD) 420.00 (or click Calculate payment), First Payment Date 03/20/2026, and Default Pay From Account 1010 - Checking Account.

Review shows Comments, the Loan Summary, and the Origination Journal Entry posted when you save. Click Save and Close: checking and the loan liability each rise $10,000.00, with no separate deposit step.

If Default Pay From Account is blank, the proceeds post to Opening Balance Equity instead of the bank.

Step 3

Record a payment and split it

Banking › Loans › Record Loan Payment is a three-step wizard: Loan & Payment, Breakdown, Review. On the Loan & Account tab, pick Pay To (Loan) First Civic term loan and Pay From Account 1010 - Checking Account; a line confirms the outstanding $10,000.00. On Current Payment, set Payment Date 03/20/2026, Amount (USD) 420.00, and Payment Method ACH / EFT. No fills with a generated LP number; type LP-1.

Click Next. Breakdown asks for Principal (USD) 380.00 and Interest (USD) 40.00; when you leave one field, the other adjusts so the parts equal the payment. Fill from estimate copies the scheduled split. Escrow (USD), Processing / Service Fee, and Late Fee are optional. Interest Expense Account is required: 5100 - Interest Expense.

Step 4

Read the remaining balance

Click Next to reach Review. It has Comments and a Payment Summary: Available in Pay From ($36,153.60, the Chart of Accounts balance), Principal $380.00, Interest $40.00, Balance After Payment $9,620.00, and Total Payment (USD) $420.00. The Payment Journal Entry below it lists the lines posted when you save.

Click Save and Close. The loan liability falls $380.00 to $9,620.00, interest expense rises $40.00, and checking falls $420.00. Save and New posts and clears the form; Save as draft keeps the payment without posting. Banking › Loans › Loans Details then shows Outstanding $9,620.00 and Next Payment 04/20/2026.

More detail on this screen

Lender Details lists Lender Name, Code, Phone, Website, and Status, with Current View, Search, and Add Lender; a lender opens with Edit Lender. Loans Details lists Loan Name, Loan #, Lender, Type, Linked Account, Original Amount, Outstanding, Interest Rate, Term, Next Payment, and Status, filtered by Current View (Active, All, Paid Off) and Loan Type, with Record Payment, Add Loan, and Edit Loan. Add Loan can also record origination fees, and Fee Treatment (Expense, Add to Principal, or Amortize) decides how they post. With Track payments, rate, term, and start date are optional and no schedule is built. Prior Payments Completed counts payments made before you started tracking; they create no journal entries. Fill from estimate on Record Loan Payment suggests the principal and interest split, and Escrow, fees, and Late Fee use their own accounts. Do not use Transfer Fund for a loan draw or payment.

Banking › Organization Loans › Org Loan Payment opens Record Organization Loan Payment. Under Loan & Accounts, pick the Organization Loan, Westline bridge loan; only loans you borrowed are listed. Step 4

Use Org Collect Loan only when repaid

Org Collect Loan opens Record Organization Loan Collection, the same form for loans you lent: Deposit To (Bank / Cash), Interest Income Account, Collection Date, Collection #, and Collection Method, with a Post Collection button. Posting raises the bank and lowers the loan receivable, and any interest goes to income. Its Organization Loan list shows only loans you lent, so Northline's list is empty.

Organization Loans › Loans Details has two tabs, We borrowed (they lent to us) and We lent (we gave to them), with Outstanding balance and Principal received cards, Current View, Search, Record Payment, and Add Loan. The Westline bridge loan shows Principal received $5,000.00, Principal repaid $500.00, and Outstanding balance $4,500.00: Northline still owes Westline $4,500.00.

More detail on this screen

Record as Add funds to an existing loan posts more principal on a loan you already have: pick the Loan type and Existing loan, then enter Additional principal received (or advanced) and click Post additional principal; no new loan is created. A loan you lent uses Disburse from (bank / cash), Organization receiving the loan, and a Loan Receivable Account. The Loan Payable or Receivable Account field is optional; left blank, it creates one named for the organization. Interest Rate (APR %) and Due Date are optional. Interest on a payment goes to the Interest Expense Account; on a collection it goes to the Interest Income Account. Principal cannot exceed the outstanding balance. Organization Loans › Loans Details shows each loan; opening it lists draws and repayments with Balance After and the journal, plus Record Payment (or Record Collection) and Edit Loan.
