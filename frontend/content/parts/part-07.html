Step 1

Add a warehouse if you track locations

Open Company › Manage Support Lists › Warehouses List. The Warehouses page has a Search box, a Status filter (All Warehouses, Active, Inactive), and a table with Name, Description, Address, Contact, and Status. Click Add New Warehouse to open a small window of the same name. Name is required, marked with a red star: type Main office. Description is optional, for example Shelf in the Portland office.

Click Save to add the warehouse as Active, or Cancel to close without saving. An empty Name gives Warehouse name is required. New Inventory Item has no warehouse field; you choose the warehouse later on a stock adjustment.

Step 2

Add the inventory item

Open Inventory › New › New Inventory Item. The Basic Information tab asks for Inventory Name (required: Laptop sleeve) and an optional Category, with Subitem of, Commission item, and Active (ticked) boxes; ticking Subitem of adds a required Parent Item. Click Next. On Sales & Purchase Details, I sell this product is ticked, which makes Sales Price(USD) and Income Account required; Standard Cost(USD) and Description on Sales Transaction are optional. Under Purchase Information, enter Purchase Price(USD) 12.00 and pick the required Cost of Good Sold and Assets Account (Finished Goods Inventory); Preferred Vendor, Vendor Product No, and Description on Purchase Transaction are optional.

Inventory Information holds On Hand Quantity, Total(USD) (Standard Cost times quantity, read-only), As of, and Reorder Point. Leave the quantity at 0; the bill brings the stock in. Save and New and Save and Close appear only on this tab, and Purchase Price must be lower than Sales Price. After the success message, click OK to go back.

Step 3

Define an assembly without building it

Open Inventory › New › New Inventory Assembly. Assembly Details starts with Inventory Assembly, the required name: Client welcome kit. Category, As of, and Active sit beside it, then the required Assets Account, Reorder Point, On Hand Quantity, and a read-only Total(USD). Purchase Information has I buy this product, Description on Purchase Transaction, Standard Cost(USD), and the required Cost of Good Sold. Sales Information has I sell this product, Description on Sales Transaction, Sales Price(USD), the required Income Account, Taxable, and Commission item.

In Inventory Assembly Item Table, click Add New Item, pick Laptop sleeve, and type 1 in Quantity Needed; On Hand Quantity, Average Cost(USD), and Total fill the row, and Total value of materials cost adds the rows. Sales Price cannot be below Standard Cost or the materials cost. Save and Close opens Build Assembly; saving uses no parts.

Quantity Needed cannot be more than the part's On Hand Quantity, so define the assembly after the sleeves have arrived.

Step 4

Purchase, sell, or build

Deskline's bill DS-8900 brings in 10 Laptop sleeves at $12.00, so the quantity on hand rises to 10. An invoice for 2 sleeves lowers it to 8. Check the result on Inventory › Inventory items: the Inventory Items page has Search and Add a new inventory item buttons, Reorder Point (All, Safe, Reached) and Current View (Active, Inactive) filters, and a line that repeats the filters with the item count. Its columns are Active, Item Name, Description, Type, On Hand Qty, Reorder Point, Sales Price, and Purchase Price.

These screens change quantities only. The bill's journal entry decides which account holds the $120.00; on DS-8900 that is Finished Goods Inventory. Build Assembly, in the next section, is the third way a quantity changes.

In the current version, selling an inventory item does not post cost of goods sold, and the item's Assets Account is not used automatically. Check the account on the bill's journal entry.

More detail on this screen

A bill adds stock only after it leaves Draft, Submitted, or Cancelled status. An invoice cannot sell more than the quantity on hand. Inventory Items filters by Reorder Point (All, Safe, Reached) and Current View (Active, Inactive). On New Inventory Item, Category offers Add New Category, the account lists offer Add New Account (which opens New Account), and Preferred Vendor offers Add New Vendor. The item form also checks that Standard Cost sits between Purchase Price and Sales Price when you fill it in. Settings › Client and Sales stores an inventory scheme of Average, FIFO, LIFO, or Specific Identification, but the current version does not use it to cost a sale. Stock status and inventory valuation reports are under Reports.

Build Assembly turns component quantities into finished units, and Stock Adjustment sets an item's quantity to what you counted. In the current version, neither screen posts a journal entry.

  2. 2DoPick Assembly ItemComponents show Quantity On Hand

Step 1

Open Build Assembly

Open Inventory › Build Assembly. In Build Assembly Details, Assembly Item is required: choose Client welcome kit, or Create new assembly item… to define one. Quantity On Hand (kits already built) and Build Point (the kit's reorder point) are read-only. Date defaults to today and Ref No. to 1. Assembly Item Components then lists each part with Name, Description, Quantity On Hand, Quantity Needed, Average Cost (USD), and Total.

Write comments for this Build Assembly. takes an optional note. Maximum number you can build from the quantity on hand is worked out from the parts: 8 sleeves at 1 each allows 8. Type 1 in Quantity to Build; a larger number is cut back to the maximum with Quantity to Build cannot exceed maximum buildable quantity. Search at the top only shows a 'coming soon' message, and Add New Inventory Assembly opens the assembly form.

Step 2

Build and check the quantities

Click Build and Close or Build and New. Each part drops by Quantity Needed times Quantity to Build, and the kit rises by the number built, so after the earlier sale 7 sleeves and 1 kit remain. The app shows Assembly built successfully!. Build and Close then opens Inventory Items, where you can read both quantities; Build and New clears the form for another build.

With no Assembly Item you see Please select an assembly item., and a Quantity to Build of 0 gives Please enter a valid quantity to build.. Cancel goes back without building. Each build is listed in Transactions Center under Inventory › Build Assembly.

Building changes quantities only. No journal entry is posted and no value moves between accounts.

Step 3

Count the shelf

Open Inventory › New › Stock Adjustment. Two required lists sit at the top. Warehouse: pick Main office, or Add New Warehouse to open the warehouse window. Adjustment Account: pick the account for the difference, shown as number, name, and type, or Add a New Account. Northline counts 6 sleeves because one is damaged.

In the Stock Adjustment table, choose the Item; Current Qty fills in with the app's figure, 7, and Current Value and Sales/Purchase Rate fill in from the item's price. Type the counted figure, 6, in Adjusted Qty and add a Comment such as 1 damaged. Add New Item adds a row, and the bin icon removes one.

Adjusted Qty cannot be more than Current Qty; a higher figure shows Adjusted Qty cannot reduce below zero or exceed current quantity!. An item that has already been adjusted once no longer appears in the Item list.

Step 4

Save only a real difference

Click Save and Close. The item's quantity on hand is replaced with Adjusted Qty, so the sleeves now read 6, and you land on Inventory › Stock Adjustments. Save and New saves and clears the form for the next count. The app stops you with Please select a warehouse., Please select an adjustment account., or Please add at least one item with adjusted quantity. (an Adjusted Qty of 0 counts as empty), and it refuses a count that would drop an item below its Reorder Point.

Stock Adjustments lists each saved line with Date, Warehouse, Item Name, Comment, Current Qty, Adjusted Qty, Current Value, and Rate, plus Edit and Delete; Warehouse, Date Range, From, To, and Update filter it. Use an adjustment for breakage or a miscount, never in place of a bill or an invoice.

In the current version, a stock adjustment does not post to inventory value or to the Adjustment Account. Only the quantity changes.

More detail on this screen

If Quantity to Build is above the maximum, the screen caps it and shows an error. Build Point on Build Assembly is the kit's Reorder Point. Each build is logged with its Date, Ref No., and comments, and appears in Transactions Center under Inventory › Build Assembly. Saved adjustments are listed under Inventory › Stock Adjustments, where you can Edit or Delete them, and under Inventory Adjustments in Transactions Center. Editing or deleting a saved adjustment does not change the quantity on hand again. On the adjustment form, Current Value is filled from the item's Sales Price.

Step 1

Open Accounts List

Open Company › Company Lists › Accounts List. Three filters sit at the top: Search Account picks one account (All Accounts by default), Account Type narrows the list to one type such as Bank or Expense (All Types by default), and Current View switches between Active and Inactive. The table shows Active, No, Name, Type, and Balance, then three icons under Register: Register, Preview, and Edit.

Preview opens Account Details with Close and Edit Account; Edit opens the account form. If accounts from the standard chart are missing, a Load Chart of Accounts or Load Remaining button appears beside Export to CSV. Click Add New Account to add an account before you need it on a line.

Step 2

Add or edit an account

Add New Account, or Company › New Account, opens a form whose title follows the type, such as Expense Account. Account Type starts at Income; pick Expense. Account Number is required but read-only: the app fills in the next free number for that type. Account Name is required: Software Subscriptions. As of defaults to today, Opening Balance (USD) is optional, and Current Balance (USD) is read-only. Is Sub-Account? adds a required Parent Account, Active is ticked, and Is Depreciation marks a depreciation expense.

Bank adds Bank Name, Bank Account Type, and Bank Account Number; Credit Card adds Credit Limit (USD) and Card or Loan Number; Paypal adds PayPal Email. Comments is optional. Save and Close returns to Accounts List, and Save and New clears the form. When editing, the button reads Update and Close; changing the type of an account with history regroups that history.

Step 3

Post daily work on its own screen

Most entries need no journal, because each everyday screen posts its own balanced entry. Settings › Journal Entry Guide shows which screen does what. Pick a Scenario, such as Customer invoice, Customer receipt, Vendor bill, Pay bill, Bank deposit, Transfer funds, Pay credit card, or Customer prepayment, and the page names the screen that records it with a one-line description.

An invoice records income, a bill records what you owe, Receive Payment and Pay Bill settle them, and the banking screens move cash. Do not use a journal for a customer payment or a bill payment, or the invoice or bill stays open. Open ANNA AGENT answers other questions; its answers are a guide only and post nothing.

Step 4

Use a journal only for corrections

Open Company › Journal Entry. Date is required and defaults to today; No is required, read-only, and filled in for you. Under Entries, each row has Account (shown as name, number, and type), Memo, Debit, and Credit, plus a Delete button that stays off while only two rows remain; Add Row adds another. Comments is optional, and the ? button opens Journal Entry Help.

JE-8 on 03/16/2026 debits Office Supplies Expense $100.00 and credits Software Subscriptions $100.00. Debit Total ($) and Credit Total ($) show under the table with Totals must be equal or Balanced. Save and New and Save and Close stay off until it is balanced and every row has an account; Save and Close shows Journal entry JE-8 saved successfully. Profit and the bank do not change. Make it recurring shows a 'coming soon' message.

More detail on this screen

Settings › Journal Entry Guide shows sample scenarios and when a journal is the right tool. The journal No is filled in for you, and each line takes either a debit or a credit. The app rejects an entry with fewer than two lines, a line without an account, a line with both a debit and a credit, or totals that differ (Totals must be equal. Debit: …, Credit: …). Leaving with unsaved lines asks whether to stay or leave. Make it recurring on Journal Entry shows a 'coming soon' message and does not store a repeat. On New Account, Is Depreciation places an expense account on the depreciation line of Profit and Loss.

Step 3

Each row's Action decides what happens. On an invoice batch, Compare & Review sets Staged (File) beside Existing (Database) for Invoice #, Customer, Hours, and Amount, and Action offers Place (add as new), Update (overwrite the match), or Skip. Card rows use Apply selected (3). It opens Apply to Credit Card Statement: choose the required Credit Card (Harbor Visa) and Statement, then click Add to statement. The rows land on that statement as unrecorded. Without a card you see No credit cards found. Add a credit card under Banking first.

An applied card charge does not change the card balance or expenses until you record it on Banking › Credit Cards › Credit Cards Details.

More detail on this screen

The invoice review compares Staged (File) with Existing (Database) and tags each row with its match and differences; its filters are All, New, Conflicts, Duplicates, Errors, and DB only, which shows invoices that exist only in the database. Rows with errors cannot be selected. On the card review, compare Running Balance with the Balance column in your file. Skipped rows stay out of profit, aging, and the card balance. Import is not a live bank feed; each batch still needs a person.

Duplicates happen when the same customer, vendor, account, or item is created twice. The merge pages let you pick the pair, but in the current version Merge does not combine records yet.

  2. 2Go toCompany › Merge Accounts› Customers/Vendors/Accounts/Items
  3. 3DoPick From and ToFrom = record merged away
  4. 4DecideFrom and To differ?YesMerge runs checkNoError toast
  5. 5ClickMerge
  6. 6Not posted yetNothing merged yet'coming soon' toast
Step 1

Find the duplicate

A list or an aging report shows the same customer, vendor, account, or item twice, such as Harbor Clinic and Harbor Clinic (copy) on Customer › Customers. The list shows Active, Customer ID, Customer Name, and Balance for each.

Before you treat them as one, open both and compare the address, contact, open invoices, and balance. Two records with different addresses may be two real customers.

Step 2

Open the matching merge page

Open Company › Merge Accounts, then Merge Customers, Merge Vendors, Merge Accounts, or Merge Items. Merge Customers has two panels: Customer From, the source record to merge away, and Customer To, the target record to keep. Each panel has a required Customer list, with read-only Customer ID, Active, and Balance below it.

The plan is to put the copy in Customer From and the keeper in Customer To.

In the current version, the lists on all four merge pages hold fixed example names (on Merge Customers: Acme Corp, Beta LLC, Gamma Inc, and Delta Ltd), not your own records, so Harbor Clinic cannot be picked yet.

Step 3

Compare, then click Merge

Once both sides are chosen, each panel shows Customer ID, Active, and Balance so you can compare them; on the example list, Beta LLC is C-002, inactive, with $350.00, and Acme Corp is C-001, active, with $1,200.00. Click Merge, or Cancel to go back.

Merge first checks your choice: Please select both Customer From and Customer To. if one is empty, and Customer From and Customer To must be different. if they match.

In the current version, Merge then shows Merge customers coming soon!. Transactions do not move and balances do not combine yet.

Step 4

Keep using one name for now

Until merging works, use the keeper, Harbor Clinic, on every new invoice, payment, and note, and leave the copy unused so its balance stays at $0.00. Customer › Customers still lists both until merging is available.

Open invoices on each record still need Receive Payment, and open bills still need Pay Bill. Never treat two truly different bank accounts as duplicates.

More detail on this screen

Merge Vendors shows Vendor ID, Active, and Balance on each side. Merge Accounts shows Account Number, Account Name, and Balance. Merge Items shows Item Type, Active, and Sales Price instead of a balance. All four pages fill their lists with example records in the current version and end with a 'coming soon' message. Cancel returns to the previous page.

7.10 Company dashboard and the company lists

Company Dashboard has two tabs, Company Details and Quick Access. Configure chooses the cards on Company Details, and Import From CSV is the upload shortcut on that page.

  1. 1Go toCompany › Dashboard
  2. 2DoConfigure › toggle cardsConfigure Widgets, then OK
  3. 3Go toCompany Lists › Accounts ListSearch, Account Type, Current View
  4. 4DoRegister or Add New AccountBalance column = posted
  5. 5Go toCompany Lists › ItemsCurrent View · Add a New Item
  6. 6ClickSave and CloseSales Persons › Add New Sales Person
Step 1

Choose the dashboard cards

Company › Dashboard opens on the Quick Access tab, a searchable menu of client pages. Click the Company Details tab to see the cards, with Configure and Import From CSV at the top right. Click Configure to open Configure Widgets: a Select All box, then one box per card, such as Profit & Loss, Cash Flow, Money Coming In, Who Owes Me, Who I Owe, Invoices Status, Bills Status, and Unpaid Bills.

Tick the cards you want and click OK, or Cancel to keep the current set. The X on a card closes it; that only hides it. Your choice is kept in this browser for this company. Who Owes Me and Unpaid Bills read saved invoices and bills.

Step 2

Open Accounts List

Company › Company Lists › Accounts List filters by Search Account, Account Type, and Current View, and Balance shows each account's balance. The Preview icon opens Account Details with Account Number, Account Name, Account Type, Current Balance, and Status, plus Close and Edit Account. The Edit icon opens the account form, and Add New Account opens a blank one.

For Checking Account, Preview shows 1010, Bank, and Active. For the full list of entries behind a balance, run Reports › Company And Finance › General Ledger Report.

In the current version, the Register icon on each row has no action.

Step 3

Open the Items list

Company › Company Lists › Items opens Items List, which shows inventory, service, and non-inventory items for customers and vendors. Columns are Active, Item Name, Description, Type, On Hand Quantity, Sales Price, and Purchase Price, and Current View offers Active, Inactive, and All.

Add a New Item opens Select Item Type: Service Item, Inventory Item, Non Inventory Item, Inventory Assembly, or Add New Consultant. Pick one and click Continue to open that form, or Cancel.

In the current version, Export to CSV on Accounts List, Items, and Sales Persons has no action and does not download a file.

Step 4

Add a sales person only when needed

Sales Persons List shows Active, Sales Person, Address, City, State, Zip Code, Phone, and Fax, with Current View (Active, Inactive, All), Export to CSV, Search, and an Edit icon. Click Add New Sales Person. New Sales Person requires Sales Person Name, Email, and Phone; File as, Job Title, Website, Fax, Expense Account, Gender, Active, Date of Birth, Date of Hire (today), Date of Last Review, Date of Release, and Address are optional.

Save and Close shows Sales person saved successfully! and returns to the company dashboard; Save and New clears the form for the next person. Saving does not create a consultant or a user.

More detail on this screen

Company Details can show cards such as Banking, Expenses, Who I Owe, Recent Transactions, Account Balances, Top Customers By Sales, Profit & Loss, Cash Flow, and Bills Status. Quick Access is a searchable menu of client pages. On Items, Active is both a column and a Current View choice. An empty required field on the sales-person form shows Please fill in all required fields. Save and New on the sales-person form keeps you on the form for the next person.

7.11 Every support list page

Each support list is its own page under Company › Manage Support Lists, and other screens pick a row from it. Payment Term List is where Northline keeps Net 15.

  1. 1Go toCompany › Manage Support Lists
  2. 2DoPayment Term › Add NewNet due in + discount fields
  3. 3ClickSaveRow added to the list
  4. 4DoGroup & frequency listsTimesheet list: Load defaults
  5. 5DoWarehouses ListAdd New Warehouse; Deactivate icon
  6. 6DoNote Tag/Type/Priority listsAdd New; never posts money
  7. 7DoSelect on live recordCustomer, vendor, item, invoice
Step 1

Add the payment term

Click Add New on Payment Term List to open Add payment term. Payment term is the required name, such as Net 15. Term type has two choices. Fixed Number of Days asks for Net due in (required, a whole number of days: 15), Discount percentage is, and Discount if paid within. Date Driven asks instead for Net due before (required), Discount percentage is, and Discount if paid before.

Click Save to add the row, or Cancel. An empty name gives Payment term name is required., and a missing or non-positive Net due in gives a matching message. Harbor Clinic uses Net 15.

Step 2

Add groups and frequencies

Customer Group List and Vendor Group List store a Name. Item Group List opens a page titled Item Categories with Name and Item Type columns and an Item Type filter (All types, Product, Service, Inventory); Add New opens New Category, or New Inventory Category when the filter is set to Inventory. A category made with All types shows for every item type.

Invoice Frequency List and Timesheet Frequency List store how often documents repeat. On Timesheet Frequencies, Load defaults fills in the standard frequencies and reports how many it added. Every list has Delete on each row.

Step 3

Add a warehouse or a shipping method

On Warehouses List, Add New Warehouse asks for a required Name and an optional Description. Each row has four icons: Edit, Deactivate (shown while active) or Activate (shown while inactive), and Delete. Deactivate keeps the name and its history but marks the row Inactive; the Status filter can show All Warehouses, Active, or Inactive.

Preferred Shipping Method List is a separate page, Preferred Shipping Methods, with Name, Description, and Delete. Add New opens Shipping Method, where Name and Description are both required, then Save.

Step 4

Classify notes without posting money

Note Tag List shows Name, Color, Related To, and Actions (Edit, Delete), with a Related To filter. Add New opens Note Tag: Name (such as Follow-up), Related To (Customer, Vendor, or Consultant), and a tag colour. Note Type List and Note Priority List each have Name and Actions, and Add New asks only for a Name, such as Meeting or High.

Customer Notes List and Vendor Notes List are read-only search pages; type in Search to find a note by title, name, or text. You add notes with Add Note in Customer Center or Vendor Center. A note never collects cash.

More detail on this screen

Payment Term List shows Name, Description, and Due Days, and Delete removes a row after you confirm. Note Tag List has a Related To filter. A group, frequency, warehouse, shipping method, or note never posts money. Invoices already saved keep their due date, and new invoices use the term you select. Adding a frequency shows how many you now have in the list.

Step 1

Fill in the budget form

New Budget has three fields. Start With offers Start from Scratch or Copy from Existing Budget. Select fiscal year is required and lists years such as FY 2025-26, with the current one marked (Current); + Create New Year shows a New fiscal year box with Confirm and Cancel. Budget Name is required, such as March plan.

In the current version, Save and Close and Save and New do not store the budget yet. A year added with + Create New Year lasts only while the page is open.

Step 2

Read the reminders page

Reminders List has a tick box column and Name, Transaction Date, Transaction Type, and Amount columns, a Current View filter (All, Active, Inactive), and Export to CSV and Search buttons. Below the table, the page count and Previous and Next arrows move through pages.

In the current version the list stays empty, so Create Selected and Skip Selected stay greyed out; with a row ticked they would show 'coming soon'. Pay Bill still pays Deskline and City Parking.

Step 3

Read the recurring page

Recurring Transactions List shows Name, Type, Transaction Type, Interval, Prev. Schedule, Next Schedule, and Amount for each repeat. Current View (All, Active, Inactive), Date (All, Prev. Schedule, Next Schedule), From, and To sit above the table with an Update button.

In the current version the list stays empty, and Use, Update, and Export to CSV show 'coming soon'. The repeat stored on a customer invoice does not appear here.

Step 4

Filter the activity log

Narrow Users Activity Log with Date range (Today, This week, This month, Custom), User type (All users, Client (owner), Invited users), Module (such as Customer, Vendor, Journal Entry, Inventory, Banking), and Actor. Choosing Custom adds From date and End date.

With no rows ticked, Export to CSV downloads the rows you see. Tick rows to swap it for Delete Logs (2) and a Clear button; Delete Logs asks Delete activity logs? and removes only the log lines, never the invoice or bill they describe.

More detail on this screen

Date range offers Today, This week, This month, and Custom, and starts at Today. User type offers All users, Client (owner), and Invited users. The delete warning says the records are deleted permanently and cannot be restored. Deleting a log line does not reverse the invoice or bill it describes. Make it recurring stores a repeat only on the customer invoice; on other entry screens it shows a 'not available yet' message.

7.13 Fixed asset screens and merge screens

Fixed assets use five pages, and merge uses four more. In the current version these pages show their forms but do not store assets, post depreciation, or merge records.

  1. 1Go toFixed Assets › New Fixed Asset
  2. 2DoFill asset fieldsAsset Number, Purchase Date, Accounts…
  3. 3ClickSave and CloseToast only; nothing stored
  4. 4Not posted yetThree asset lists emptyExport to CSV: coming soon
  5. 5DoSet Depreciated From / ToUpdate button has no action
  6. 6Go toMerge Customers (or others)Customer From / Customer To + Balance
  7. 7Not posted yetMerge: coming soonNothing combined
Step 1

Fill New Fixed Asset

Below New Item, Asset Number, Purchase Date, Account, and Depreciation Account, the New Asset form asks for Asset Type (such as Computer Equipment - Fixed Asset), Purchase Price (USD), Cost Basis (USD) (the total cost to depreciate), Acquired As (New or Used), Vendor/Payee, Location, Serial Number, Purchase Order Number, Warranty Expiration Date, and Description. Then come Depreciation Rate, a yearly percentage from 0 to 100, and Depreciation Method.

Accumulated depreciation to (today's date) (USD) is read-only. It spreads the yearly rate over the whole calendar months since the purchase date, so a $1,800.00 laptop at 20% bought 03/02/2026 still shows $ 0.00 in March and $180.00 six months later. A warning appears if the purchase date is before the fixed assets start date.

In the current version, Save and Close and Register show a success message but store nothing.

Step 2

Open the three asset lists

Pending Items List, Registered Items List, and Sold & Disposed Items are separate pages, each with Export to CSV and Add a New Asset. Pending items shows Item, Asset Number, Account, Purchase Date, Purchase Price, and Show history; Registered items adds Book value; Sold & disposed items shows Disposal Date, Disposal Price, and Gains/Losses. Add a New Asset opens the New Fixed Asset form in a window.

In the current version, Pending and Registered always show three built-in example rows, such as Laptop A-001 for $1,200.00, not your assets, and Sold & Disposed shows No records to show. Export to CSV shows 'coming soon'.

Step 3

Set the depreciation dates

The Depreciation page has Depreciated From and Depreciate To date fields and a Start Date button. Start Date opens a window that shows Current start date is: 04/01/2021, explains that the start date is the 1st of the first month in your financial year, and offers New Start Date with Cancel and OK.

Update has no action yet, so the Accounts table stays empty and March profit stays the posted invoices, bills, payroll, and card charges.

Step 4

Read a merge page before relying on it

Each merge page has a From panel (the record to merge away) and a To panel (the record to keep). Merge Customers and Merge Vendors show ID, Active, and Balance; Merge Accounts shows Account Number, Account Name, and Balance; Merge Items shows Item Type, Active, and Sales Price.

The lists hold example records only, such as Alpha Supplies and Bravo Traders on Merge Vendors. Merge checks that both sides are chosen and different, then shows 'coming soon' and combines nothing.

More detail on this screen

New Fixed Asset also asks for Asset Type, Acquired As (New or Used), Vendor/Payee, Location, Serial Number, Purchase Order Number, Warranty Expiration Date, and Description. Methods include Straight Line, Declining Balance, Double Declining Balance, Sum of Years' Digits, and Units of Production. The Depreciation page lists Account, Amount to be depreciated, and Accumulated depreciation Account. Merge Items shows Sales Price instead of Balance. Harbor Clinic and any duplicate stay separate, and Northline's March profit includes no depreciation.
