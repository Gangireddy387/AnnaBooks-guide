10. Pages and dialogs

10.1 Dialogs for consultants and timesheets

Consultant and timesheet work opens a small dialog before the main form. New Consultant first asks whether the person has a single service agreement or several.

  1. 1Go toConsultant › New ConsultantOpens agreement type dialog
  2. 2DecideMultiple Service Agreements?YesMulti-agreement formNoSingle agreement form
  3. 3Go toCreate TimesheetConsultant preview toolbar
  4. 4DoManual Entry or UploadCheck past dates, hours
  5. 5ClickCreate Time SheetConsultant, From/To Date required
  6. 6Not posted yetTime Sheet CreatedNothing posted yet
  7. 7ResultCreate Invoice postsInvoice plus auto journal
Step 1

Choose the consultant type

Consultant › New Consultant opens Select Consultant Type, which asks: Does this consultant have multiple service agreements or a single service agreement? Click one of the two cards; a check mark shows your pick. Single Service Agreement is for one agreement, with the customer, vendor, and service details set on the consultant, like Priya Shah at Harbor Clinic. Multiple Service Agreements sets those details separately for each agreement, like Grace Liu at Cedar Schools and Maple Dental.

Continue stays disabled until a card is chosen, then opens the matching New Consultant form. Cancel, the X, or a click outside the box closes the dialog and saves nothing.

To give an existing consultant more customers, use Enable Multiple Customer Service Agreements on the Consultants list. Its Confirm button keeps existing contracts, timesheets, and billing history, and the consultant must already have a customer assigned.

Step 2

Choose how the timesheet is entered

Create Timesheet sits in the toolbar of an open consultant's preview, such as Priya Shah's. It opens Select Timesheet Submission Type with the line Choose how you want to create the timesheet: and two choices. Manual Entry means you type the dates and hours yourself. Upload from File is for a timesheet you already have as a PDF or an image; AnnaBooks reads the dates and hours from it.

Manual Entry opens Add A New Time Sheet at once. Upload from File first opens your computer's file picker; after you pick the file, the same form opens with Upload Document for Data Extraction switched on, the file attached, and the read already started. This dialog has no Save button, and the X closes it without creating anything.

Step 3

Save the sheet

Add A New Time Sheet opens over the preview. Consultant Name is filled in and locked, with the hint Consultant is pre-selected and cannot be changed. From Date and To Date are required, typed as MM/DD/YYYY, and accept only past dates (before today). Hours, also required, holds the total. A consultant with several agreements also gets a required Customer Service box for the agreement the hours belong to.

Once both dates are set, Day-wise hours lists one row per day with Work Description, Regular Hours, Overtime Hours, and Total Hours, and shows the Timesheet frequency. Attach files adds supporting files of up to 10 MB each. Create Time Sheet saves the sheet with Invoice Status Time Sheet Created and posts nothing; Close leaves without saving.

Step 4

Post from the main screens

Create Invoice appears in the consultant preview toolbar only while the consultant has a sheet with status Time Sheet Created. For a consultant with several agreements, such as Grace Liu, it first opens Select Customer: This consultant has multiple agreements. Choose the customer agreement to continue. Pick the agreement in the Customer box. If none can be billed, the dialog shows No customer agreements found for invoicing.

Continue stays disabled until a customer is chosen, then opens Create Invoice for that customer; Cancel closes with no change. Nothing posts until you click Save and Close on Create Invoice, which posts the invoice and its journal at that agreement's Sales Price. For C2C consultants, Create Bill and Pay Bill ask the same way through Select Vendor and Select Vendor for Pay Bill.

More detail on this screen

Enable Multiple Customer Service Agreements creates a service agreement from the consultant's current details and keeps existing contracts, timesheets, and billing history linked to it; its Confirm button reads Processing... while it works. An uploaded timesheet must be a PDF or an image of 10 MB or less. Extract Data reads it and Data Extracted confirms the read. If the read fails you see Data extraction was unsuccessful and Retry Extraction; type From Date, To Date, and Hours yourself, and the file stays attached. Map or Create Consultant opens from a bank statement line: it maps a consultant who has no customer yet to that customer, or offers Create New Consultant. Preview, export, approval notes, and progress dialogs do not post anything. Pay posts only when a staged payroll run is applied and posted.

10.2 Dialogs for invoices, bills, and payments

Lists open a details dialog so you can read one saved document without leaving the list. Details dialogs only show what was saved, so closing one changes nothing.

  1. 1Go toOpen row from listInvoice, bill, or payment
  2. 2DoRead the details dialogLines, payments, Mail details
  3. 3Not posted yetClosing changes nothingDetails dialogs are read-only
  4. 4Go toVendor › Drafts & CapturedDrafts, Inbox, Captured, History
  5. 5DoOpen the draftOpen / Review & adjust
  6. 6ClickSave and CloseOn the Create Bill screen
  7. 7ResultBill and journal postDraft leaves Draft status
Step 1

Open the document from the list

On Customer › Invoices, the details button on a row (View invoice details including creation logs, journal entries, and timesheets) opens the invoice in a dialog. The header shows the customer and the invoice number, such as Cedar Schools and Invoice: INV-1053, with Invoice date, Due date, and Status. Beside them are Subtotal, Invoice total, Paid, and Balance; Tax, Bonus, and Discount appear only when they hold an amount.

Three tabs sit below. Line items lists each row with Type, Name, Details, Amount, Paid, and Balance. Consultant & timesheets shows each consultant with Timesheet ID, Date range, Hours, Rate, Status, and Attachments. Journal entries lists the Date, Account, Memo, Debit, Credit, and Reference lines the invoice posted. Nothing here can be edited, and the X closes the dialog without changes.

Step 2

Read the payment beside the bill

Bill Payment Details opens from a recorded check line on a saved bank statement, such as check 2205 to City Parking. Payment summary lists Vendor, Payment date, Payment method, Pay from, Amount paid, Check no, Bills, and Applied to bills. The Bills paid tab shows each bill with its Bill Date, Bill total, Applied, Balance due, and status, so CP-310 shows $240.00 applied and $0.00 due. Journal entries shows the posted lines.

Pay bill details gives the same view from the vendor side, with Payment date, Method, Payment total, Pay from, and the tabs Pay bill lines and Journal entries. Receipt preview shows a customer receipt with Download PDF and Close. Mail details lists each send of an invoice: From mailbox, Service, Sent to, Subject, PDF / attachments, and an Email content preview. These dialogs only read saved records.

Step 3

Add a name or a note

On a cash or credit card expense line, the item list ends with Create New Product Item and Create New Service Item, which open Create Product Item or Create Service Item over the expense. Create Service Item has two tabs: Basic Information asks for Service Name and Category, and Next moves on; Service Information has I buy this service with Purchase Price(USD), Expense Account, and Preferred Vendor. Save and Continue saves the item onto your line.

Notes live on the Notes tab of a vendor or customer preview. Add Note opens Add Vendor Note or Add Customer Note. Title, Note Type, Priority, and Notes are required; Tags, Attachments, Follow-up Date, and Private Note are optional. Save stores the note and changes no balance; Cancel drops it.

Step 4

Finish a draft bill in Create Bill

Vendor › Drafts & Captured has the tabs Drafts, Split, Inbox, Captured, and History. Drafts shows one card per unposted bill with a Draft or Captured badge, the vendor, bill number, amount, and date, then Consultant, Period and Hours, Rate, Payment terms, Due date, Entries, and Email. Search and the Vendor filter (All vendors) narrow the cards, and Total and Bills sum them up.

Open on a card, such as DS-9012 from Deskline Supplies for $89.00, loads the bill into Vendor › Create Bill. On the Captured tab, Review & adjust opens the matched draft with its mail, and Not this mail skips a wrong match. Check the bill and click Save and Close; that posts the bill and its journal.

There is no Accept button. A draft stays out of aging and profit until you save it on Create Bill. Bills submitted through the vendor portal wait under Invites & Submitted instead.

More detail on this screen

Drafts & Captured has the tabs Drafts, Split, Inbox, Captured, and History; Mail setup and Refresh appear on the mail tabs. Bills submitted through the vendor portal are reviewed under Invites & Submitted, where Create Bill opens them on the bill screen. Receive payment details shows which invoices a receipt cleared. Vendor direct expense details and credit card expense details each show one saved expense or card charge. Customer transactions and vendor transactions list that name's documents. The Journal entries tabs appear only for users allowed to view journal entries.

10.3 Dialogs for the bank, cards, and imports

Bank imports use dialogs to bring statement lines in and to explain a line that is already there. Importing saves the lines for review, and nothing posts until you record each line.

  1. 1Go toBanking › account UploadOpens Upload Bank Statement
  2. 2DoDrop the statementPDF or image; CSV elsewhere
  3. 3DoReview extracted rowsAdd row, move, remove
  4. 4ClickImport N TransactionsSaves statement lines
  5. 5Not posted yetLines not recorded yetNo journal from import
  6. 6DecideMatching record exists?YesLink, Mark as RecordedNoRecord New Payment
Step 1

Bring the file in

Banking › Bank Accounts lists each account with icon buttons. Upload on the Checking Account row opens Upload Bank Statement in the page. Drag the file onto Drop your bank statement here, or click Choose file; it takes PDF and image files (PNG, JPG, JPEG). The file name and size then show, with Remove if you picked the wrong one.

Process Statement reads the file. A progress panel shows the current step, a percentage, and step markers while the lines are extracted; nothing is saved yet. Cancel or Close leaves without saving. A CSV file goes another way: Upload CSV File on Banking › Bank Transactions opens Upload Transactions, which expects Date, Description, and Amount columns, with Category and Merchant optional.

Step 2

Fix what the read got wrong

When reading finishes, Statement Information shows Bank, Account Number, Account Type, and Statement Period, all editable, beside a Summary and a Statement Preview. Extracted Transactions has one tab per section, such as Deposits and Additions, Checks Paid, and Electronic Withdrawals, with S.No, DATE (MM/DD), DESCRIPTION, and AMOUNT, plus CUSTOMER on deposits. Validation Mismatch Detected appears when a tab's count or total differs from the summary.

Fix any cell in place. Add row adds a missed line; tick rows, then use Remove selected or Move to tab. Import 14 Transactions saves the lines for review and posts nothing. A card statement opens Review Parsed Statement instead: edit Date, Payee, Section, and Amount, untick lines to skip, then Confirm & Create Statement. Add Single Transaction on Bank Transactions opens Add Transaction for one line typed by hand.

Step 3

Record each line

Open the account's saved statement with View Saved Statements and click a line that is not recorded; its tooltip reads Click to mark as recorded. Record Transaction shows a badge such as Customer deposit, then the line's Date, Amount, and Party. Under Link to existing record choose Link Vendor Payment, Link Credit Card Payment, Link Fund Transfer, Link Receive Payment, Link Bank Deposit, Link Loan Payment, Organization Loan, or Link Journal Entry. Create Payroll Journal handles a payroll withdrawal.

Link Receive Payment opens Link Payment. Pick the receipt under Select payment to link; records with the same amount say (amount match). If the amounts differ, a Can't link message appears and Mark as Recorded stays disabled. Mark as Recorded links the line and posts nothing new. If no record exists, Record New Payment creates one from the line's amount and date.

Step 4

Link loans and open the source

For a loan line, click it and choose Organization Loan in Record Transaction. Record as Organization Loan shows the line's Date and Amount and notes that only loans with an unlinked draw matching this date and amount are listed. Choose the loan in the required Organization Loan box, such as Westline bridge loan for the $5,000.00 wire on 03/04/2026; a line under it confirms the match and the balance.

Link Draw links the bank line to that draw and posts no new journal entry; Cancel closes. For money you lent, the same dialog reads Record as Loan Given with Link Disbursement. Once a line is recorded, clicking it opens the record behind it, such as Payment details for a receipt, Bill Payment Details for a check, or Fund Transfer Details and Loan Payment Details.

This dialog cannot record a new draw. If no loan matches, it offers Create / record the loan first.

More detail on this screen

Map or Create Customer and Map or Create Vendor match a bank line's name to a record or create one. Add Transaction asks for Date, Transaction Type, Description, and Amount, all required, plus optional Category, Merchant, and Notes. Upload Transactions previews the first 5 rows of a CSV, and Clear starts over. Add credit card statement period sets the dates for a card statement with Add Period. Select Deposit Account belongs to Company › Review Staged Imports, where it picks the Deposit In account for imported receipts before Apply to production. Verify Identity to View Check uses Send OTP to Email, then Verify & View, before the check image opens. An imported line you have not recorded posts nothing, so the checking balance does not change.

10.4 Dialogs for the journal, the chart, and a new company

A new company opens Set Up Your Chart of Accounts, which creates the starting accounts that later invoices and bills post to. Journal lines can be edited later, but only while debits equal credits.

  1. 1DecideLoad Chart of Accounts?New company setup dialogYesPredefined accounts createdNoSkip for Now
  2. 2Go toImport company from backupZIP or Anna JSON › new company
  3. 3Go toJournal Entries › Edit LinesRow action on list
  4. 4DoChange debit, credit linesAdd line if needed
  5. 5DecideDebits equal credits?YesShows BalancedNoSave Lines disabled
  6. 6ClickSave Lines
  7. 7ResultAccount balances updatedPosted entry is rewritten
Step 1

Set up the chart on a new company

The first time you open a company with no chart loaded, Set Up Your Chart of Accounts appears with Welcome to Your New Company!. It says loading creates a set of predefined accounts organized by category, and lists what you get, such as Pre-configured asset, liability, and equity accounts and Revenue and expense categories ready to use. An Important note warns that without a Chart of Accounts you cannot track finances or produce accurate reports.

Load Chart of Accounts creates the accounts and reads Loading... while it works. Skip for Now, or a click outside the box, closes the dialog with no accounts created. You can add accounts later with New Account.

Step 2

Import a backup as a new company

Import in the Your Companies top bar opens Import company from backup: Restore a Data Export ZIP or Anna JSON backup as a new company. A stepper shows Source, Preview, and Confirm. On Source, pick Data Export ZIP or Anna JSON backup, both marked Recommended, then drop the file or click to browse. Parse & preview reads the file.

Preview shows Will create company with its name, counts of Datasets and Rows, and a Preview button on each dataset. Next goes to Confirm, where Confirm & import asks Create company from backup?. Yes, import now creates a separate company; it does not mix into Northline. Back returns a step.

The import will not start if that company already exists. Open that company and use Settings › Data Import instead.

Step 3

Open the journal behind a document

Several dialogs show the journal lines a document posted. The Journal entries tab in an invoice's details and in Pay bill details lists Account, Memo, Debit, and Credit. On reports such as General Ledger, Balance Sheet, and Profit and Loss, opening an entry shows the Journal Entry dialog: the entry number, Date and Status, then each Account, Memo, Debit, and Credit with Totals, like JE-8 on 03/16/2026.

On Company › Transactions Center › Journal Entries, View Details opens Journal Entry Details under the row, with Date, Status, and Related. A payroll entry with no pay run reads Related: Not linked, with a Map payroll pay run link. That opens Map Payroll Pay Run: pick the run in Pay run, such as PR-12, and click Save Link. Cancel leaves it unlinked.

Step 4

Edit lines only while balanced

Edit Lines, the pencil on a journal row or the button in its details, opens Edit Journal Entry Lines with the entry number and status, such as JE-8 · Posted. Each line has Account, Memo, Debit, and Credit, plus a trash button; an entry keeps at least two lines. Add line adds a row, and Totals adds up each column.

While the totals differ, the dialog says Debits and credits must be equal before you can save, and Save Lines stays disabled; every line also needs an account. When they match it shows Balanced. Save Lines rewrites the entry, and Cancel drops your edits. Edit Lines is not offered on cancelled entries or to invited users.

On a posted entry the dialog warns: This entry is posted. Saving will update account balances to match the new lines.

More detail on this screen

Edit Lines and Map Payroll Pay Run appear only on journal rows that allow them: Edit Lines on entries that are not cancelled, and Map payroll pay run on payroll entries with no pay run linked. Draft entries also have Post Entry, Cancel Entry, and Delete Entry. Formula help explains a calculated figure on a report. The profit and loss popup reads posted activity only. Print Job Completed asks Is your print job completed successfully?; Yes, Done marks the checks as printed, No, Not Done does not, and neither posts anything. Northline's March figures stay in Northline after any backup import.

10.5 Dialogs for settings, mail, and the session

Settings and sign-in use dialogs that change how the company looks or who can open it. None of these dialogs posts an amount.

  1. 1Go toCompany Preferences › Company DetailsConfigure Currency Format
  2. 2DoSet Positive, Negative formatLive amount previews
  3. 3ClickSave ChangesCurrency format saved
  4. 4DoEnter company passwordThen Unlock; not sign-in
  5. 5DecideSession ending soon?YesContinueNoLog out
  6. 6Not posted yetSession expiredSign in again
  7. 7DecideKeep unsaved edits?YesStay on PageNoLeave Page
Step 1

Set how amounts look

Company Preferences › Company Details has a Currency Format section. Configure Currency Format works once a Primary Currency is chosen and opens Currency Format Settings - US Dollar. Format Preview shows a Positive Amount and a Negative Amount and updates as you change settings. Three tabs hold the choices: Basic has Currency Symbol and Decimal Places, Format has Positive Format and Negative Format, and Separators has Decimal Separator, Thousands Separator, and Number Grouping.

Save Changes stores the format and shows Currency format settings saved successfully!. Cancel or the X closes without saving. Only the display changes: stored amounts and report totals stay the same.

Step 2

Unlock an encrypted company

When you open a company that has Enable data encryption turned on, Enter company password appears with the company name under the title and one box, Password. Type the company encryption password set under Data encryption on Company Details. It is separate from your sign-in password.

Unlock stays disabled until you type something, reads Checking… while AnnaBooks checks the password, and opens the company when it matches; pressing Enter does the same. A wrong password shows an error under the box. Cancel closes the dialog, and the company stays closed.

Step 3

Reset a password or renew the session

Forgot your password? on the sign-in page opens Reset Password, with the steps Email, Verify, and Password. Enter your Email Address and click Send OTP. Type the 6-digit code from the email under Verification Code and click Verify OTP; the code expires in 10 minutes. Then fill New Password and Confirm Password and click Reset Password. Back returns a step.

Session ending soon shows the time left, such as About 4m 59s left before your session ends, with Continue and Log out; Settings › Session Alert sets how early it appears. Continue only closes the warning, so save your work. Session expired says Your sign-in timed out for security and offers Sign in again.

Step 4

Answer the pause dialogs

Unsaved Changes appears when you try to leave Company Preferences with edits not saved. It reads You have unsaved changes. Are you sure you want to leave? and warns Your changes will be lost if you continue. Stay on Page, or a click outside the box, keeps you there with your edits. Leave Page discards them and goes where you were heading.

Delete Your Account? lists what is lost: all your companies and all your financial data, permanently. Delete Account stays disabled until you tick I understand that this action cannot be undone; Cancel backs out. Other delete confirmations also wait for your yes, and success, warning, and error dialogs only report what the last action did.

More detail on this screen

Configure Currency Format becomes available after you pick a Primary Currency. Select Email Service chooses Gmail or Outlook, and its button then reads Proceed with Gmail or Proceed with Outlook. Select Email Template Theme sets the look of company mail, and Crop & adjust frames the logo before you click Apply. The company encryption password is set under Enable data encryption on Company Details. Settings › Session Alert sets how long before expiry the Session ending soon warning appears. Mail sources and a new dunning category do not collect an open invoice.
